<p><strong>Ashwin (Seed 10) vs Girish (Seed 9)</strong><br/>The famed reaper bracket is upon us. The highly anticipated matchup of the bottom is here, and it features two teams who might be turning things around too late. Ashwin, a week removed from stopping Akash’s winning streak in its tracks, looks to escape the season long ridicule he’s faced all year. He boasts 3 impressive wins over Akash, Will, and Adit, all competitive teams. Led by his two star WR’s Keenan Allen and AB, who should feast this weekend against the Bengals and Raiders, he hopes to avoid the reaper. Girish on the other hand, is coming off a brutal loss to Sid’s Kittle. His season could have completely different with some lineup choices, as Girish had 5 losses this year decided by less than 10 points. His saving grace is the arrival of Nick Chubb, who is quickly cementing himself as a top 10 RB. It’s a good thing he’s there, because the rest of Girish’s team is basically in the infirmary.<br/><br/></p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/archive/2018/f45d88bece.png"/>
<p><strong>Sid (Seed 8) vs Adit (Seed 7)</strong><br/>Led by two David Johnson’s, Sid looks to keep him momentum going after a clutch win against Girish last week. Like recent years, Sid just missed the playoffs while rattling off a bunch of wins in the latter half of the season. The next step for Sid is making the other bracket, which I think he can do next year with a little. As for now, he should be able to avoid the reaper, as long as Mike Evans stops being bipolar and Cam’s shoulder doesn’t fall of. Patty Mahomes spearheads Adit’s team, who looked like he might make the playoffs there for a while. As much as I hate to say it, Adit looks like a solid contender for the reaper since his RB’s have gone back to being inconsistent and bad luck has plagued him again. Here’s to hoping that Sutton and Diggs can help him avoid that fate.<br/></p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/archive/2018/dbb9ca03ec.png"/>
<p><strong>Will (Seed 6) vs Anish (Seed 5)</strong><br/>The only two teams with nothing left to play for. In all honesty, both these teams have rosters that are playoff-quality material. Anish has Gurley, who might be one of the rare players to go #1 in back to back fantasy drafts next year. Will boasts an even deeper squad with best-white-WR Thielen, handsome squidward Michael Thomas, and Saquon. Both teams had decent drafts, added good players, but still are looking in from outside. Atleast neither of them are going to suffer from the reaper.<br/><br/></p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/archive/2018/705ff4f2eb.png"/>
<p><strong>Brian (Seed 3) vs Akash (Seed 2)</strong><br/>Perhaps the worst playoff team in history vs a team of wild boys. Brian lost Sanders as I was writing this, so he’s going to have to rely on some of that old waiver magic again. Even though everyone memed his RB strategy, it seems to be paying off since he’s got two RB1’s out of it. Now that Rodgers has been freed from the communist McCarthyism and Kelce has overtook Gronk as the top TE in the league, there is a slimmer of hope for Brian. On the other side is Akash’s team of wildboys. If Fournette and Lindsay can avoid fights, while Gordon/OBJ/Jones can steer free of drugs, Akash has a great shot of reaching the finals. Also what’s interesting to note is that all year Akash was searching for a kicker, when he had one in his RB1 slot the whole time. <br/></p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/archive/2018/bde2e9ffe3.png"/>
<p><strong>Arjun (Seed 4) vs Ruda (Seed 1)</strong><br/>Ruda smashed the PF record this year, set a high in number of wins in league history, and put together a great group of players. His reward? Facing a top 5 PF team of all-time and possible the best ever 4th seed in Arjun. Don’t let his 6-7 record fool you, because this team is armed to the teeth. He’s got probably the best WR corps in the league, the top defense, and Kelvin “I’ll eat the other team” Benjamin. Kamara and Tyfreak are built for playoff matchups with their scoring explosions, and with an ailing Ruda, Arjun is primed for an upset. With a hurt Julio and Conner, and a pretty bad QB situation no matter how hard he tries to hype up Lamar, Ruda has to be careful here. He hopes to survive Conner’s absence with the help of Zeke and GOAT CMC. If he can nail his WR’s choices for this week, he can make the finals for a third year in a row. <br/></p>
