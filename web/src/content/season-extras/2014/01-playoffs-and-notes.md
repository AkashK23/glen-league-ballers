<h3>2014 Season Notes</h3>
<p><ul><li>The season opened with what would become a new rivarly: Adit vs. Arnav. Arnav defeated Adit 96 to 78 due to a poor performance by Green Bay at Seattle to start the season. </li><li>Brian gained attention for a putrid start that was followed by several excellent waiver wire pickups (such as Odell Beckham), perhaps having one of the best teams in the league by the season's end. He did not make the playoffs, however.</li><li>Adit began the season poorly, but rattled off several wins in the second half as Green Bay's offense began to roll, surging into the playoffs with momentum.</li><li>The most lopsided regular season win in league history occurred during that surge when Adit defeated Will 165-72. Adit's performance also set the record for most points in a single game.</li><li>Arnav had both the #1 and #2 running backs in the league (Bell and Lynch), setting up the foundation for his championship run.</li><li>The Arnav-Adit rivalry finally blossomed in week 10 when they took part in one of the most intense games of the season. Arnav managed to gain a 60 point lead heading into the evening games, leaving Adit against near insurmountable odds with only a few players left. Shockingly, Adit furiously came back with ridiculous performances by his remaining players, most notably Aaron Rodgers, and drew to within 10 points of Arnav. However, it was not enough to overcome Arnav's massive lead, powered by Marshawn Lynch's 40 point day, and Arnav won 114-106.</li><li>The four teams that made the playoffs started the top four quarterbacks in the league in 2014. In order, they were: Aaron Rodgers, Andrew Luck, Peyton Manning, and Ben Roethlisberger.</li><li>Adit led the league in scoring this season with 1355 points. Ashwin came in second with 1344. On the other side of the spectrum, Brian led the league in points against, with 1311, an all-time record. Girish was second with 1220.</li><li>The championship game appeared to be going in favor of an upset in the first week, with Adit taking a slight lead. However, Arnav rallied back thanks to a dominant performance by the seemingly-injured Marshawn Lynch against Arizona (his "Beast Mode 2.0" run being the turning point), and the Patriots' decision to sit Rob Gronkowski. Arnav won the game soundly to secure a ring and the first championship of the GIB League.</li></ul></p>
