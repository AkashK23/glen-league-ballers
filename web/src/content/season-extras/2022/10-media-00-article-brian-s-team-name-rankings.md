<p>What’s in a name?<br/><br/>Today is a very special day as once again, we start off the GLB Fantasy Football season with the annual tradition of BRIAN’S TEAM NAME RANKINGS.<br/><br/>I can’t believe it. After a quick check on the amazing Weebly website (shoutout to Adit), we are going into our fifth(!) year of names to be taken and ranks to be given, and I must say, my job gets tougher and tougher with every single year as the owners get more and more creative (some… a bit too creative).<br/><br/>In the years past, I have compared the franchise flags, the lineup labels, and the team taglines, but this year, I want to go a bit of a different route. A name is a powerful thing. It doesn’t just define the team - nay it goes much further than that. See, a name is an identity, a symbol, to latch onto for the team, the organization, and especially the fans. And the identity the team and fans embody lead to some of the best traditions in football.<br/><br/>What do I mean by this? Well…<br/><br/>The Packers have their Cheese heads<br/><br/></p>

<p>The list goes on and on. So this year, I will rank each team’s names not just on the logo and team name itself, but also on the fanbase it ultimately creates and the identity of the entire organization it paints for itself. And so, without further ado, let us begin with…<br/> <br/><strong>10. Timy xD Time</strong><br/>The <em>Carolina Timy xD Time</em> is uhh… let’s just say a <em>traditional </em>franchise. Despite pressures from society and the rest of the league to change its name, the franchise has remained steadfast, and its leadership has insisted on keeping their god-awful name. As such, their fan base, which call themselves the “Timys” are also a stubborn bunch. They are also 2-dimensional, fat, annoying, and all named Eric Cartman and they watch in the stands making the xD face.<br/></p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/archive/2022/9320cba1fb.jpg"/>
<p><br/><strong>9. Browncos coundee Kmet’s Clyde</strong><br/>The <em>Boston Browncos coundee Kmet’s Clyde </em>unsurprisingly come up next in this list. This franchise has not only started to lose its grip on reality, but also its grip on the English language. Visiting sports fans coming to watch the Browncos coundee Kmet’s Clyde often must prep a couple months of Duolingo just to communicate with the locals around here as the fans have also begun speaking a bastardized form of the English language where every other word must be some unnecessary pun related to a player name.<br/><br/>Actual recording of a Browncos fan speaking:<br/><a href="https://youtu.be/nJ7QB3om-QY?t=35">https://youtu.be/nJ7QB3om-QY?t=35</a><br/><br/><strong>8. Ja’ MaRR R(a)</strong><br/>Coming in at number 8 are the <em>Indiannapolis Ja’MaRR R(a)s</em>. This franchise recently formed a partnership with Bollywood and hence the atrocious rebranded name. Ever since changing their name to something with too many punctuation marks and too many R’s, the composition of the fanbase has dramatically changed. It seems now, the only people who show up each week to cheer the Ja’MaRR R(a)s on have dark black hair, love gobi manchurian, are absolutely impeccable at IT, and also can pec fly tigers.<br/><br/>Here is a typical day for a Ja’MaRR R(a)s<br/><a href="https://youtu.be/7Crk_5XEb10?t=102">https://youtu.be/7Crk_5XEb10?t=102</a><br/> <br/><strong>7. A. Swift Brown Hard man</strong><br/>The <em>Chapel Hill Brown Hard Men </em>identify themselves as a group of very swift and hard brown men. Unfortunately, the fanbase has been in quite a disarray recently after its ownership has decided to execute their very own mascot. It would be unfathomable for NC State to put down their wolf Tuffy, or UNC to shoot their own Ramses, yet the Chapel Hill franchise has decided to part ways with their very own Mecole Hardman. Had this scandal not happened moments before the name rankings, this team name would be much higher. Alas, as their male fans try to decide if they are still hard or not, you can at least always count on them being swift and brown.<br/><br/><em>Note: do not confuse a Swift Brown Hard Man with the Ja’ MaRR R(a)s although their fanbases may share the same pigmentation, they are two very different cultures, and you would be cancelled for mistaking one for another.</em><br/></p>

<p>(I have no idea why this picture is what it is, I just googled “fast Indians”. It’s late.)<br/> <br/><strong>6. The Mooney Pie Factory</strong><br/>The <em>Maryland Mooney Pie Factories </em>make their way to number 6 overall on the team-name rankings. Named after the state’s historic boom in Moon Pie productions the Mooney Pie Factories have been one of the most successful franchises in league history. It is of course no surprise then that the franchise also has one of the largest fanbases in the entire country owing to the fact that many “casual” fans nowhere near Maryland often jump onto the bandwagon just because they like rooting for a team that has seen so much success. However, when I said the “Mooneys” are the <em>largest</em> fanbase in the entire country I meant that in more than one way. You see, just as it is common tradition in many college or high school football games for fans to do X number of pushups after their team scores X number of points, the “Mooneys” actually eat X number of Moon Pies after their team scores X number of points. And given that the team has historically been one of the highest scoring teams in league history, its fanbase is always suffering for cardiovascular disease and morbid obesity. But then again, who can blame them, the “Mooneys” get to support a winning team and eat moon pies every single year.<br/><br/>All the “Mooneys” after week 1:<br/><a href="https://youtu.be/tZuU6Zy695s?t=8">https://youtu.be/tZuU6Zy695s?t=8</a><br/> <br/><strong>5. Aaron’s Psychedelics</strong><br/>Breaking into the top half are the <em>New Haven Psychedelics</em>. This is a most curious franchise. Somehow after years of being in the cellar, always catching the short end of the stick, the ownership of this franchise seems to be the chillest of them all. How can this be? Well it seems the mystery has been solved after the franchises most recent team name reveal. Drugs. That is the only explanation. The owner, the player, the fans, hell I bet Cam Akers was on some shit last Thursday and that’s why he barely got any snaps. It’s not uncommon for a visitor going to a Psychedelics home game to accidentally think they stumbled into a Burning Man concert or maybe Woodstock. Through the haze of smoke and euphoria there isn’t much good football being but hey, the Psychedelics are cool as a cucumber and enjoying themselves and in the end that’s the real winner in this cursed game.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>4. All the SingleTaries</strong><br/>Up next are the <em>San Fransisco SingleTaries. </em>The Golden City franchise has recently up ended the dating pool after coming out with their new team’s name. Fans have become so attached to the identity of the SingleTaries that it has become a tradition for couples who walk into the stadium to break up immediately and leave the game single. At first these instances seemed like just a joke, but it soon became much more serious when kids were watching their parents file for divorce mid-game on the Jumbotron. Nonetheless, you cannot deny passion these fans have when they are ready to throw away their happy and healthy relationships in support of their own team.<br/><br/>Some snippets of the SingleTaries in full force:<br/><a href="https://youtu.be/J9-ueJhTLa0">https://youtu.be/J9-ueJhTLa0</a><br/> <br/><strong>3. Let Me Get A McPick 2</strong><br/>The <em>Blacksburg McPick 2s</em> annoyingly come in at number 3 in team name rankings this year. It would be so fitting to finish in the 2nd place, but competition was fierce this year and I couldn’t bring myself to rank it higher than the two remaining team names. The fans fortunately have whole-heartedly adopted the team image and calling themselves the “2’s” and have adapted the 2 into their lives. They love finishing in 2nd, their favorite movie is 2fast2furious, and Sunday is a great day since they can watch their franchise play football, but their 2nd favorite day of the week is Tuesday. It’s honestly becoming an epidemic, yet the “2’s” keep on passionately tooting their support in hopes that the McPick 2 finish at the top of the standings at the end of the year (but not too well since they much rather finish 2nd).<br/><br/>Instead of playing the National Anthem, the “2’s” play this song (and idk if its just me listening to this at 2:14AM but this is lowkey a banger)<br/><a href="https://youtu.be/E-kMsBMh6Ng">https://youtu.be/E-kMsBMh6Ng</a><br/> <br/><strong>2. Etienne Piggs in a Blanket</strong><br/>Coming in at number 2 we have the <em>Dayton Piggs in a Blanket</em>. This franchise has just come fresh from a finals loss, but not as fresh as the Pigs in a Blanket their fans bring to every home game. Man they are so yummy my tummy grumbles for them. I could sure down a whole dozen piping hot right out of the oven pigs in a blanket. They are so good. Yum. Jonathon Taylor is really good. So is the rest of the team. Pigs. Blankets. Eating.<br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>1. Curius Chase of Benjamin Sutton</strong><br/>And finally, rounding out at the top of the list are the <em>Seattle Benjamins</em>. A great name that the fans have all accepted whole-heartedly and thus it is not an uncommon sight to see the stadium to be filled with babies. Calling themselves the “Benjamin Suttons”, fans commonly engage the activity of reverse aging because they have realized if they go back to a point where they can no longer count past 3, they will never have to be distraught over the inevitable 3-win season this franchise tends to head towards every year. That’s a win-win in my books, and why the Curius Case of Benjamin Sutton is our number 1.<br/><br/></p>
