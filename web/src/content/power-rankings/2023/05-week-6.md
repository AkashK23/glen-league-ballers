<p>As you all know the NFL is scripted and so is our fantasy league. So it’s no surprise that we can draw eerie parallels between the two leagues that seem way too similar to be just a coincidence.<br/><br/>This week, I have given each franchise their NFL shadow team. Each NFL team was chosen to match the current situation of their fantasy team counterpart as closely as possible. The power rankings this week were based on the order of the NFL week 6 power rankings for your shadow team so don’t be mad at me. <br/><br/>Do you think you have a chance at winning it all this year? Well take a look and see if you have the same amount of faith that your NFL counterpart can win a Super Bowl. <br/><br/>(PS I wrote all the rankings and records before the Monday Night games because I already know who’s gonna win and lose, I got access to the GLB script early this year. Adit, it’s not looking good)<br/><br/><strong>12. Adit (0-5) / Carolina Panthers (0-5)</strong><br/>When looking at only winless teams left in both leagues there aren’t much positives to say about them. Defenses have terrorized them week in and week out: Dallas D scored 34 and Bills D scored 31 vs Adit while defenses against the Panthers have generated 15 sacks and 8 turnovers so far. Both the Panthers and Adit have some serious pieces that show promise as we’ve seen from Zack Moss, Dallas Goedert, and Herbert (like Thielen, Chubba, and Bryce) they just need to figure out how to work together to finally score that elusive first win. Keep Pounding Adit. <br/><br/><br/><strong>11. Sujay (1-4) / Chicago Bears (1-4)</strong><br/>What a statement week from Sujay and his NFL comparison the Chicago Bears. A team that became the laughingstock of the league after the first 4 weeks finally exploded in a way that no one was expecting. Even though Sujay’s/Bear’s matchup was against one of the weaker teams in the league led by a UNC alum, a win is a win. DJ Moore and Jamarr Chase combined for 98.7 points outscoring 7 teams this week! The boom potential of the Bears/Sujay is eye popping and suddenly this is a dangerous team that no one wants to face. Bear down Sujay. <br/><br/><br/><strong>10. Akash (2-3) / Washington Commanders (2-3)</strong><br/>Akash and the Commanders started the season white-hot after leaping out to a 2-0 start. However since then, the alarm bells must be ringing as they have now dropped three straight games. The previous two weeks losses could have been chalked up to facing elite teams (Bills, Eagles and Ruda, Ruda), so many a UNC fan were hoping that Howell/Krishna could right the ship this week facing a what seemed to be a very squishy opponent. But the Sujay/Bears were feeling more grizzly than teddy and absolutely mauled their opponents. The QB play has been more than serviceable, but if this team wants to fight for one of the last spots in the playoffs they need to generate some consistent playmaking from its other positions. Let’s go Akash-skins! (Because they apparently don’t have any actual chant) <br/><br/><br/><strong>9. Mayur (1-4) / Minnesota Vikings (1-4)</strong><br/>Truly a bamboozling conundrum. Mayur and the Vikings are absolutely loaded in talent with elite WRs, TE, and RB. While you don’t love the quarterback play (heh) you don’t hate it either so what’s the issue here? How can such an offensively loaded team be 1-4? The answer is defense. The Vikings have let their opponents score over 11 TDs since the start of the season and have allowed on average 344 yards per game which both rank near the bottom of the league. Meanwhile Mayur has started defenses that have scored 12, -1, 2, -2, and -5. Besides the first week, the defensive position is averaging a whopping -1.5 points per week. However the NFL/fantasy season is a marathon not a sprint. It’s not how you start but how you finish. This team has too much talent and with some crucial matchups coming up, it has the sneaky potential to squeak into playoffs, but they need to start winning immediately. SKOL SKOL SKOL MAYUR. <br/><br/><br/><strong>8. Sid (4-1) / Pittsburgh Steelers (3-2)</strong><br/>Probably the lowest a 4-1 team has ever been ranked in power rankings but let’s take a look at the facts. Out of every team that is 0.500 or above the Steelers/Sid are dead last in PF (Steelers averaging just a measly 15.8 points per game while Sid is averaging merely 94 points per game). For reference the top 3 scoring teams in the NFL (Dolphins, Lions, 49ers) are averaging more than double that at 33.8 points per game. Likewise, the top 2 scoring teams in the GLB (Arjun, Anish) are averaging 123.7 points per game. Sid and the Steelers seem to have the magical ability to stymie their opponents whenever they face him and bring the game to his terms. This team has talent though, and a large reason for low scoring is due to poor management from the top (fire Canada/Sid). Still, 4-1 is 4-1 and both Sid/Steelers are near/at the top of their division, so as long as they hold steady, they are locked into the playoffs. HERE WE GO SID! WHATEVER IT TAKES. <br/><br/><br/><strong>7. Girish (2-3) / LA Rams (2-3)</strong><br/>The Girish/Rams are an interesting team. Once at the top of the world, they have now tumbled quite far, and to be fair, Girish has been hit with the injury bug the hardest. Just like the Rams, he’s been without his best player for many many weeks. Here is a list of players that Girish has rostered that have missed time due to injury: Saquon Barkley, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Jamaal Williams, Brandon Aiyuk, Christian Watson, Jeff Wilson Jr., James Connor. But I must give credit where credit is due, even without Kupp, and even after having to start the second string RB over the starter (Kyren over Akers /Gary Brightwell over Saquon) the Rams/Girish have eked out a 2-3 record. As long as their QB stays healthy this team can show a second half surge and push its way into playoffs, although they are currently 3rd in their own division. And if, god forbid, Stafford/Allen gets hurt… then my condolences. L (ELL) A (AYY) Girish! <br/><br/><br/><strong>6. Ashwin (2-3) / Cleveland Browns (2-2)</strong><br/>Since 1999 the Cleveland Browns have had 3 winning seasons. Since 2014 Ashwin has had 2 winning seasons. Since 1999 the Cleveland Browns have finished with the WORST record in the entire NFL 4 times (1999, 2000, 2016, 2017). Since 2014 Ashwin has finished with the WORST record in the entire GLB 5 times (2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020). The comparisons are endless from having Deshaun Watson as quarterback to being a poverty franchise. One has to wonder if Ashwin Jackson is getting paid to lose. <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2022/02/05/sport/hue-jackson-nfl-cleveland-browns-not-paid-spt/index.html">https://www.cnn.com/2022/02/05/sport/hue-jackson-nfl-cleveland-browns-not-paid-spt/index.html <br/></a>This year the Ashwin/Browns have been quite bipolar, either winning huge or getting steamrolled in spectacular fashion. 2-3 isn’t the end of the world and playoffs are still well within reach, but like the Browns, Ashwin needs to shore up his RBs and focus on football instead of wasting his time with absurd lawsuits. Dawg Pound Ashwin, woof woof. <br/><br/><br/><strong>5. Will (3-2) / Jacksonville Jaguars (3-2)</strong><br/>After starting 1-2, fans of the Jags/Will were starting panic. Just like the Jags, Will took losses early on in the season due to a pandemic of endzone-phobia within the team as every single player refused to score a TD. However, that all changed in the past two weeks. Could it be a change of scenery? London Lawrence and APL Will looked in control as they lead their teams to two impressive victories. The good thing is that while Lawrence will have to return to Florida, Will is going to continue to work at APL for at least the next 10 weeks through the end of the Fantasy season. The Jags/Will are on a role and look to continue their momentum against two soft matchups in the (Colts, Saints / Adit, Akash). If this team gets hot, they will have some serious championship chances. Stand United Will. DUVALLLLLLL <br/><br/><br/><strong>4. Brian (3-2) / Seattle Seahawks (3-1)</strong><br/>Even though they took a rough week 1 loss to division rival Girish/Rams, the Brian/Seahawks have much to be optimistic about. The team is scoring in bunches and has a well rounded offense. The WR room is strong overall (DK, Lockett, Njigba / Evans, Thielen, Wilson, Hollywood). The RBs are surpassing everyone’s expectations (Kenneth Walker 3 / Breece, McLaughlin). This team has been subject to quite a bit of scoring volatility - Seahawks scored less than 14 once and over 35 twice while Brian similarly has scored under 66 once and over 120 twice. Unfortunately they share the same division with perhaps the best team in the NFL/GLB right now (49ers/Arjun) so winning the division, while not out of the question, is looking unlikely. But the recent strong play from Brian/Seahawks has its fans once again dreaming for a second championship title. 12th MAN. GO BRIAN. <br/><br/><br/><strong>3. Ruda (3-2) / Dallas Cowboys (3-2)</strong><br/>It’s hard to pinpoint exactly where Ruda/Cowboys sit in the league. Both teams have gotten their three wins from 3 below average teams (Cowboys vs Giants, Jets, Patriots and Ruda vs Will, Akash, and Akash), but It’s their losses that really make this team hard to place. This week’s loss is understandable as they were going against perhaps the best team in the league, but it’s their second loss that really is inexplicable. Ruda/Cowboys losing to the Mayur/Cardinals probably cost a lot of people money in Vegas and is now a big blot on their otherwise decent record. This team is talented and have a serious chance to win it all this year, they just need to string together some consistency. HOW ABOUT THEM RUDAS. <br/><br/><br/><strong>2. Anish (4-1) / Kansas City Chiefs (4-1)</strong><br/>A comparison to the reigning Super Bowl champions is never a bad thing and Anish has just the team to warrant such a strong likeness. With one of the best QB/TE stacks in the league (Anish: Kirk + Hock, Chiefs: Mahomes + Kelce) this team is a threat to blow out their opponents every week. Other than a shocking loss to an opponent from Detroit, this team has been stellar and once again Anish/Chiefs are a big threat to not just make playoffs but to win it all. OHHHHHH OH OOHOOHOOOOOOOOOOH *while doing the tomahawk chop* <br/><br/><br/><strong>1. Arjun (5-0) / San Francisco 49ers (5-0)</strong><br/>As Adit once famously said: “watching good football teams is wild”. This team seems to have it all it’s almost unfair. WRs? Have the deepest and scariest in the league (Deebo Aiyuk / Davante, Keenan, Diggs). RBs? Electric (CMC / Etienne, Kamara) TE? Always steady. (Kittle / Engram). Defense? Stifling opponents every week (49ers D / Steelers D). If you had to nitpick and critique this team in any way it would be that the QB position is not exceptional, but guess what? You apparently don’t need to be exceptional if everyone else on your team is god-tier. Purdy is not a bad player in his own right, but it’s almost as if he’s playing Madden on easy difficulty. Likewise, Arjun can start a QB that only scores 4 points and he can still lead the league in scoring. You can’t even argue that the Arjun/49ers have had an easy schedule up to this point because they just made one of the top teams in the league Ruda/Cowboys look like the week 1 Brian/Giants. This team currently looks unbeatable and are the clear favorites to win it all. BANG BANG Arjun GANG. </p>
