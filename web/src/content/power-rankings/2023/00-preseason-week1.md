<p>Welcome to the historic 10th season of the GLB fantasy football league! As part of our many changes this year, we have welcome two new members. Naturally, we begin by hazing them. So, Sujay, you're up, pledge.</p>
<p>* * *</p>
<p>In August of 2017, I joined a fantasy football team known as the “Glen League Ballers”. As a<br/>newcomer, I was only granted the role of co-owner but slowly over the course of 6 years I have<br/>gained the league’s trust and they have accepted me as one of their own, allowing me<br/>ownership of my own team. Over this course of time, I have learned much about each of the<br/>owners and the time has come for me to share my knowledge with the world. <br/>These are some of the most deranged people on the planet. From the months of August to<br/>January, fantasy football consumes them. I’ve witnessed it take over their lives, causing them<br/>physical and mental anguish. I have seen friend turn on friend, brother turn on brother and for<br/>what? They are all determined to win the championship but have lost themselves along the<br/>way. With my addition to the league, I fear I will soon succumb to this ravenous desire to obtain<br/>the coveted ring.<br/><br/>Below I have attached my notes on each of the owners. I have listed them in the order I believe<br/>they will perform this year given their draft but of course this means nothing in the world of<br/>fantasy football. I have thus far only looked from the outside, watching, studying, learning but I<br/>am now in the belly of the beast. Can I trust any of them? No, only a fool would turn his back to<br/>a hungry lion. Still as I delve further into this new chapter of my research, I am driven to prove<br/>myself as a worthy competitor. I only worry what the determination will cost me…<br/><br/><strong>12. Brian</strong><br/>The name "Brian Wu" has become synonymous with "absolute psychopath" in this<br/>league. He is by far the most passionate member, crying out from his losses as though<br/>they are whips on his very being. He is an absolute menace: vetoing for spite, leaving<br/>the league on multiple occasions, proposing trades with anyone foolish enough to listen<br/>to his siren song. For all his dedication though he has won a single championship which<br/>he believes will springboard him to greatness. However, Brian has already taken so<br/>many L’s this year before the season has even begun. With his first two picks, Travis<br/>Kelce and Jonathan Taylor, injured he has resorted to preaching that Daniel Jones aka<br/>“Vanilla Vick” will be his breakout player. It is a sad time to be a Brian Wu fan and an<br/>even sadder time to be Brian Wu.<br/><br/><strong>11. Ashwin</strong><br/>Ashwin is unfortunately the true definition of a poverty franchise. He is bottom of the<br/>league in wins, points and playoff appearances. The only statistic he is top of is loser<br/>brackets where he has a record 4 years of being at the bottom. This year Ashwin is<br/>putting his faith in Tony Pollard, A.J. Brown and Mark Andrews to pull him out of this<br/>hole, not exactly where I would put my trust. However, despite his historical failures I<br/>wish Ashwin the best because, to quote Trent Crimm, the Independent, I can’t help but<br/>root for him. <br/><br/><strong>10. Sid</strong><br/>Arre Judah. Sid has already made so many questionable decisions this year I don’t even<br/>know where to start. From his trade with Adit resulting in Vetogate 2 to drafting a<br/>defense in the 8th round, is it any wonder his franchise is in the bottom 3 in all time<br/>scoring? I have similarly heard very little positives about Sid over the years and a quick<br/>look at the website shows he has only had one playoff appearance in 2020. He has a<br/>thirst to prove himself though and I won’t count him out yet with powerful weapons like<br/>Patrick Mahomes, Dameon Pierce and (eventually) Cooper Kupp. He could be the one to<br/>shake up the league but we will see if he manages to find himself in the playoffs or at<br/>the bottom of the league and the bottle.<br/><br/><strong>9. Ruda</strong><br/>Before meeting Ruda this past month, I knew exactly three facts about him. <br/>1) His name is Ruda. <br/>2) He lives in DC. <br/>3) He’s an absolute god at fantasy football. <br/>With 2 championships, 4 scoring titles and 8 winning seasons, the numbers speak for<br/>themselves, he is not one to take lightly. This year he is starting with some very good<br/>players rounding out his roster such as Christian McCaffrey, DK Metcalf and Lamar<br/>Jackson. However, I have heard rumors that his true skill lies in being a sniper on the<br/>waiver wire, catching more eligible athletes than the entire Kardashian family.  His ball<br/>knowledge is undisputed and most owners seem to respect him more than Girish<br/>despite having less rings to his name. However, this may be a fact he is trying to change<br/>this year as he attempts to take back his crown.<br/><br/><strong>8. Adit</strong><br/>The first NFL game was played on October 3, 1920. The following year Adit won his first<br/>and only fantasy championship. He has held tightly to this victory as only the other<br/>owners’ great grandfathers can remember a time when Adit has won a ring. This year he<br/>is even more enraged from the onset of Vetogate 2 and is sure to be bringing the heat.<br/>After taking Nick Chubb with the third overall pick, Adit went on to take Stefan Diggs<br/>and Aaron Jones which could be a recipe for success. Nevertheless, Adit has consistently<br/>had 6 or 7 victories per season, truly exemplifying a meh franchise. Will he be able to<br/>overcome this hurdle of mediocrity or has the dementia finally set in?  <br/><br/><strong>7. Arjun</strong><br/>Arjun is the dark horse of the victor’s circle and no I don’t mean his skin color. He has<br/>one ring under his belt as well but has not made it back to a championship since 2018.<br/>He started this year by trading away his first round pick to draft Davante Adams and<br/>Najee Harris in the second round. While his team has no superstars, there are a good<br/>batch of middle tier players which will do him well. Arjun is a staunch defender of the<br/>voiceless, standing up for his fellow owners and fighting to keep the peace. Will his<br/>players fight as hard for him though?<br/><br/><strong>6. Will</strong><br/>Who is William? For the blind, He is the vision. For the hungry, He is the chef. For the<br/>thirsty, He is the water. If William thinks, I agree. If William speaks, I’m listening. If<br/>William has one fan, it is me. If William has no fans, I don’t exist.<br/>Ah my former co-owner, how much you have suffered. Is there a tale in this league<br/>more tragic than Will’s? After being absolutely shafted in 2019 and having to perform<br/>the worst punishment in the league, he came back with a vengeance making it to the<br/>finals in 2021 just to have his heart shattered again. Will has been a long-time believer<br/>in WR&gt;RB, an ideal he continues to practice in this draft taking Justin Jefferson with his<br/>first round pick and Jaylen Waddle with his second. This leaves his RBs in the capable<br/>hands of Joe Mixon and (*checks notes) Samaje Perine? I wish my former partner luck<br/>and if I want anyone to win here that’s not me, it is William.<br/><br/><strong>5. Girish</strong><br/>Girish has won a total of 3 championships, the most of anyone in the league. If you ever<br/>forget that fact, don’t worry he only mentions it every 5 fucking seconds. To any outside<br/>observer, you may think this means he is pretty good at fantasy, right? Wrong, ask any<br/>member of the league and they will instantly put their hands on their head and lament<br/>the fact that someone who sucks ass so much is capable of winning 3 rings. Some have<br/>conceded that Girish might actually have some skill while others have stated that his<br/>victories alone demonstrates that fantasy is all luck. This year he is putting his faith in<br/>Saquon Barkley and Amon St. Brown to turn 3 rings to 4. Girish has stated that he has<br/>proven all he needs to and this year he will be simply enjoying the spoils of war. With his<br/>underground moves and questionable ethics though, only an idiot would believe he’s<br/>not aiming for the gold again.<br/><br/><strong>4. Akash</strong><br/>Now here’s a guy who knows a thing or two about picking black men. I have been in this<br/>league for 6 years and I have honestly not heard anything of note about Akash. He is the<br/>definition of a true middle of the pack owner. For all I know Akash has done absolutely<br/>fuck all over his fantasy career but this might be his year to turn things around. He has<br/>long since been the league’s manager, moderator and resident rapper. As a fellow fan of<br/>the poetry arts, I have written a limerick to describe his team:<br/>Akash’s team is always close to the worst<br/>This year he took Ekeler in the first<br/>With Godwin and Wilson<br/>If the playoffs he’s missin’<br/>Then much like his QB that Hurts<br/><br/><strong>3. Sujay</strong><br/>As an objective researcher I must remain neutral in my self-assessment. That being said I<br/>do believe I’ve drafted a pretty good team, if I dare say so. With an absolute steal of<br/>Jamar Chase followed by my second pick of CeeDee Lamb, it is clear my time in the<br/>Furth franchise has given me a taste for the WR approach. With decent running backs as<br/>well, I am proud of my first draft and excited for it to all fall apart at the end of week 1.<br/><br/><strong>2. Mayur</strong><br/>This year along with myself another newcomer has been accepted into the fold. Is he<br/>another researcher, gathering intelligence on these owners or is he another lost soul<br/>come to waste away in this godforsaken league? As a result, I have no prior notes on<br/>him and can only discuss how he performed in the draft this year. Mayur has managed<br/>to get solid picks for most positions with Tyreek Hill and Josh Jacobs likely to be leading<br/>his team. He seems to be a capable owner but he also did just make a questionable<br/>trade for Younghoe Koo solely for his team name so the jury is still out on what the<br/>future holds for Mayur.<br/><br/><strong>1. Anish</strong><br/>With an exceptional draft this year, Anish is coming in first on the list. Anish has been a<br/>pretty big wild card over the years: drafting, trading and playing the game in a way he<br/>describes as “so genius the rest of us would not understand”. He is an agent of chaos; he<br/>would stand by and watch the world burn if it made a good meme. Although he was<br/>hyping up Nick Chubb for the last month and begging everyone to leave him, he has<br/>landed on Bijan Robinson and Derrick Henry as his starting RBs, both good picks. That<br/>along with his solid WRs makes this poised to finally be Anish’s year. I would also like to<br/>note I have no idea what Timy xD means and I am frankly scared to ask about the<br/>Monkey Man part.<br/><br/>This concludes my research notes on the “Glen League Ballers” fantasy football league, As I<br/>begin this new season, an official part of the league, I am confident in my abilities but face<br/>trepidation of what the price of victory will be. May God forgive me for the decision to join this<br/>league as I will never forgive myself. To my fellow competitors all I will say is: <br/>Good Luck. Have Fun. This is Fantasy Football.</p>
