<p>GLB decided to stop making sense in Week 2. Most of the previously bad teams did well and most of the previously good teams did bad. This left us rankers in a state of disarray, so instead of actually putting together a list for this week we just used a random number generator and then did our best to justify the results. Makes about as much sense as any proper ranking we could come up with.<br/><br/><strong>10. Brian {0-2} (-)</strong><br/>Brian sucks. Even the randomness of a random number generator couldn't bypass that fact. Actually, he scored pretty alright this week, but so did all the other bad teams. When Cooper Kupp is done pretending like he's a top 10 receiver, we expect a crash back to Earth. Also, in a stunning twist, David Montgomery didn't look elite.<br/><br/><strong>9. Arjun {0-2} (-)</strong><br/>These rankings don't consider the CMC injury, and I think we all know where Arjun would have ranked if they did. I mean, it's random, but still. Is it time to hit the panic button yet? We've seen 0-2 starts turn into something magical before, but this team is basically Kyler dragging a bunch of nobodies and McCaffrey watching from the sideline. The battle of 0-2 teams this week is a big one, and the for the loser, it is indeed time to panic. <br/><br/><strong>8. Will {1-1} (-4)<br/></strong>Hey, don't blame us, blame the random number generator. Will's fascinating path from 8 to 4 to 8 is deserving of a movie. Or something. After proving that they are consistent RB1s in Week 1, Will's RBs did not play anything like RB1s in Week 2. Imagine losing to Anish. <br/><br/><strong>7. Anish {1-1} (-)<br/></strong>Anishio came out on top over Will and thus ranks one spot ahead. Or rather, that's how we retroactively explain his completely random ranking. Thanks to the brilliant move of starting both Patrick Mahomes and the defense he was playing against, Anish didn't actually score all that much. Sort of an uninspiring team, but one that at least has potential.<br/><br/><br/><strong>6. Akash {1-1} (-3)<br/></strong>Example #6000 of high-scoring Week 1 teams that lost in Week 2, Akash was especially bad as he scored the lowest of any team. By a lot. If this is what he can accomplish with Wilson still playing well, just imagine how bad he will look when Wilson inevitably falls apart in a month and a half. Also, get a dang TE.<br/><br/><strong>5. Ruda {1-1} (+3)<br/></strong>Look, you can make an argument for Ruda basically anywhere between 3 and 7 right now. Things like this are why we left the decision to fate. Pretty much all of his points came from Derrick Henry, and while he is a dependable team-carrying RB, it's hard to put Rudy any higher than 5 until the rest of his team starts doing well too. At least Najee didn't look unbelievably awful this week. That's a start.<br/><br/><strong>4. Adit {1-1} (-2)<br/></strong>Of the many teams to win in Week 1 and lose in Week 2, I at least looked the most stylish while doing it. Still basically scored 100, and Lamar realized that he is simply not a productive thrower of the football, so stuck to running like the running back he is. That's the good news. The bad news is my supposedly lethal RB duo of Kamara and Gibson is not very lethal. Gibson sat and watched JD McKissic get like 1400 points in two-minute drills, while Kamara sat and watched Jameis Winston's eyesight return to pre-Lasik levels as the Panthers pass rush spanked him.<br/><br/><strong>3. Ashwin {1-1} (+2)<br/></strong>I am so happy to be writing this ranking. Randomness is truly the way. We were RIGHT about Ashwin, and as I said before: this is the first time he has drafted a good team since Obama was president. Rodgers, Adams, Chubb, and McLaurin form one of the most dependable player cores in the league. Ashwin got past the fluke of Week 1 and emerged as the contender we always knew him to be. I'm so proud.<br/><br/><strong>2. Sid {2-0} (+4)<br/></strong>Oh. Alright. Sid is #2 I guess. I mean, he is 2-0. On one hand, his team doesn't look amazing when you look at it. On the other hand, he's 2-0. On the third hand (Johnson and Johnson's fault), he is Sid. Brady, Aaron Jones, and Kelce good. Everyone else bad. Sid confusing. <br/><br/><strong>1. Girish {2-0} (-)<br/></strong>This was inevitable. What have we, as a league, done to deserve this? The running joke is that Girish is supposed to win despite being bad. He's actually good and it's annoying as hell. What's even more annoying is how well-rounded this team is. Everyone except Antonio Brown scored double digits, it was a full team effort. And Girish didn't even have Josh Jacobs. This is poopoo peepee. Someone become better than him.<br/><br/></p>
