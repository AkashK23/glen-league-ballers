Akash {4-7} (-3)</strong><br/>What the hell happened to this man. This was Akash's best performance in three weeks and it was just 78 points. This was after a pretty good start too. The injuries hurt (pun) but gotta push through. Season over for this team.<br/><br/><strong>9. Anish {3-8} (-)<br/></strong>Not a bad week, but this is obviously too little too late for a team that was essentially eliminated weeks ago. Anish's best hope is just to act as a huge spoiler now, with great opportunities against playoff contenders in Ruda and Brian coming up. Then, we get to see if he's truly learned as many lessons as he claims he has for next year.<br/><br/><strong>8. Arjun {3-8} (+2)<br/></strong>An out-of-the-blue thorough beatdown of Brian had resounding impacts on the league playoff race, and also kept Arjun safe from the dreaded all-time low W/L record. Worst he can do is tie it, as NINE teams have done in past years. Happy for you bro. Only thing is Brian ruined your PA so you have less ammo for complaining now.<br/><br/><strong>7. Sid {6-5} (+1)<br/></strong>Sid put together his best performance in months right when it seemed like he was set for a historically low-scoring season. How this team is 6-5 right now is beyond me. It is STILL last in scoring in the league. Somehow, some way, there is still a route to the playoffs here. If that happens, we will all know that fantasy is truly just luck.<br/><br/><strong>6. Brian {6-5} (-3)<br/></strong>Brian has scored 65 or less in three of the past four weeks. The fourth week was 136 for some reason. It's hard to tell what to think of this team, but the playoff odds are in decent shape thanks to a high-scoring early season. Still, it's hard to justify ranking a team doing SO poorly most of the recent weeks in the top half. HUGE prove-it game against Girish this week.<br/><br/><strong>5. Ashwin {5-6} (+1)<br/></strong>Ashwin is another one of those teams with tiny playoff hopes hanging in there. Right when it was needed most, he delivered his strongest performance of the season. I don't want to call this team GOOD just yet - the season scoring just isn't there - but I think the word DANGEROUS is appropriate. That lethal Packers duo can carry any week. Now, the best Ashwin can do is win out and hope the rest of the league plays out the right way.<br/><br/><strong>4. Ruda {7-4} (+1)<br/></strong>We all know that Ruda is a force to fear because of his history. We all know he'll probably cruise into the playoffs. But the books tell a different story over the past few weeks - a story of a team unable to top 81 in a three-game losing streak. The loss of Henry was huge, but doesn't explain scoring THIS low. Can the rest of his team turn it around? It might not HAVE to thanks to that clutch seventh win, but it probably wants to just to be sure. <br/><br/><strong>3. Adit {6-5} (+1) <br/></strong>Scoring 104 and losing to the lowest-scoring team late in the season with playoffs on the line is vintage me. I'm still in the best place to make that fourth seed over Brian thanks to my strong PF (#2), but six wins is not gonna cut it. I HAVE to win at least one more, which is a scary thought considering one of those games is against red-hot Will. Euuueueueeuururrrrrgghhhgghhghhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh<br/><br/><strong>2. Girish {8-3} (-1)<br/></strong>Girish's team did their jobs with a solid performance in a huge game against Ruda, essentially clinching his playoff spot. What isn't talked about as much is that it'll take a lot to knock him out of the division lead too. This excellent team is in position for a first or second seed finish as Girish will look to make his fourth championship appearance in five years.<br/><br/><strong>1. Will {7-4} (+1)<br/></strong>It can't be denied any longer: this is the best team in the league. Will is on pace for 1404 points, which would be third-most all time. Another near-accomplishment of note: Will is on a 6-game winning stream (the record is 9, second place is 7). Having the best fantasy QB and two top-five RBs will do that. Will is cruising, and safe to say that the goal for him is no longer just playoffs - Will is eyeing his first championship appearance.</p>
