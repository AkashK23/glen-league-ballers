<p>Honestly, I'm proud to say that this year's preseason power rankings were probably the most accurate of all time. And by that I mean "didn't get literally every team completely wrong". Let's start off this week by analyzing one of those teams we were very, very correct about.<br/><br/><strong>10. Brian {0-1} (-)<br/></strong>It's almost like when the entire league laughs at you for your draft picks, there's a reason for that. It's almost like when you completely ignore ADP and rankings, things tend to go wrong. And it's almost like drafting FIVE (5) running backs in the first six rounds, including four straight to start off, is a dumbass thing to do. Well, actually, it can work out if you make good picks. Not James Robinson and Raheem Mostert (who was gonna get about 8 carries a game even if healthy). <br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>9. Arjun {0-1} (-4)<br/></strong>Well this was a major yikes on many fronts. While it's important to keep in mind that Arjun became the second player in history to tank in Week 1, and would have scored much better if he hadn't, this was a concerning start nonetheless. Week 1 NVP Kyler Murray will be great, and CMC will be great, but the rest of the team is looking lackluster - though DK and Waller are exceptions to that. The key will be securing an actual RB2, which would mean Elijah Mitchell securing more than... 8 carries a game. Let's see if the tank works.<br/><br/><strong>8. Ruda {0-1} (-2)<br/></strong>ALL IM SAYIN IS<br/><br/>All I'm sayin is<br/><br/>all im saying is that anyone who has to take a "depression nap" on Sunday afternoon probably isn't making it into the top 5, no matter what history says. This was one of the biggest blowout losses of Week 1, thanks in no small part to the WR corps we already knew was... questionable. The only players on this team to even hit double digits were Dak and Robbie Gould. Ruda, just let us enjoy this before Najee gets on his 2000 yard pace, okay? I don't think I've ever made a negative meme about you so I'm taking the one chance I'll ever get.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>7. Anish {0-1} (+2)</strong><br/>As Anish has already reminded us about 4,000 times, he lost last week even though he scored pretty well. Contrary to his belief, though, this does not automatically result in him being ranked #1. Last week his team was basically Mahomes and CeeDee and... no one else. We can expect the WRs to do better but those running backs are seriously suspect and Kyle Pitts looks to be shocking the world and doing exactly what every other rookie TE in history has done: suck. Looking forward to another several thousand complaints about Anish's tough schedule this season. And you guys call ME the cherry picked stats guy. <br/><br/><strong>6. Sid {1-0} (+1)<br/></strong>Travis Kelce is apparently more aerodynamic without his beard, and it helped Sid to a solid week (though nothing crazy). The promise here is that he put up nearly 100 with Aaron Jones doing literally nothing in a flukey blowout loss, so we can expect more in the future. Still, what really sets this team back is its HORRIBLE running back situation behind Jones. To the surprise of absolutely no one, the Atlanta Falcons offense cannot support good production from an RB. And Myles Gaskin is just... not exciting. <br/><br/><strong>5. Ashwin {0-1} (-2)<br/></strong>This only losing team to make the top 5, and it's because of that fluke I just mentioned with Sid. This team actually did pretty solid if you just ignore Rodgers and Adams. Rodgers scored ONE POINT. I mean, come on. Even if he doesn't have a typical elite season, he's gonna score a hell of a lot more than that lol. Rodgers and Adams should combine for 25 at a MINIMUM every week, and if that had happened this first week then Ashwin would have scored closer to 100. And if those two Packers had scored really well, like they usually do, we would be talking about a fantastic performance from this team. So all Ashwin has to do is just root for the Packers to get going again. I'm sure that won't be difficult for him.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>4. Will {1-0} (+4)<br/></strong>We are haters no more. This game started off rough, but Will got going over time and wound up topping 115. This was all thanks to key contributors and household production names: Jalen Hurts and Joe Mixon. Ah yes. The reason Will isn't higher is because we need to see a larger sample size from Hurts. Also, we're all just sort of waiting to see if one of his 90 trade offers offering Ty'Son or Javonte works out. Still, if JT and Mixon can keep it up, I actually see this being a really fun and disruptive team. Maybe by the time you make the playoffs you'll have convinced ESPN to not hate you.<br/><br/><strong>3. Akash {1-0} (+1)<br/></strong>Pretty much exactly what we expected from this solid team - great Seahawks offensive production (until about Week 8), Tyreek exploding, and the remaining wide receivers doing what they could. Oh, with one huge exception: some guy named Sakwa... Sacre... Saquon? Did I get that right? Yeah, some guy named Saquon Barkley did terribly. Luckily, Akash hardly invested any draft capital in that no-name, and has comfortably benched him for the coming week. Honestly, he's just wasting space on your bench. Drop him and sign someone better.<br/><br/><strong>2. Adit {1-0} (-)<br/></strong>Well, I actually wanted to rank myself lower in Week 1, but Arjun nailed it. I rejected Anish's... chubby husband's... bukkake efforts and put up an impressive af 120 points. Interestingly, this wasn't even due to my RBs and TE, who are supposed to be the core of my team, but rather mainly due to my WRs. Tyler Lockett and Deebo Samuel are the opposite of consistency, so they'll fall off, but at this point we can reasonably hope that my RBs step up to even things out. Starting with this week when Kamara plays the... Panthers. On second thought, WRs pls help.<br/><br/><strong>1. Girish {1-0} (-)<br/></strong>This was another correct ranking, but I think it was an easy one too. Most people seemed to be in agreement that Girish had the best draft, and it proved true this past week as he led the league in scoring. The impressive thing about this was that it was an all-round effort - Ridley was the only player who didn't hit double digits outside of the defense. Add to this the fact that Girish's bench was somehow, like, even better than the people he actually started, and we have the makings of an early championship contender here. That is, if Girish can ever start the right players. At least he knows he can rely on Matt Ryan's favorite target.<br/><br/></p>
