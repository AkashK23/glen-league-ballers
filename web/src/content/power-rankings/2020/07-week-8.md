<p><br/><strong>9. Ashwin {1-6} (+1)</strong><br/>REJOICE. AshWIN got his first win of the season and is also not ranked last in this week’s power rankings. Now as mentioned earlier, your position here is a result of something you have that Brian doesn’t: hope. While critics may look at this ranking as too high due to Ashwin’s historically low PF, I believe this team has potential to get better. Not only is the team starting to find some rhythm each week, but Ashwin is actually going to try to make waiver claims unlike Brian who denounced them earlier today. Another big thing I consider is experience, and for Ashwin that is his experience in the reaper bracket. How many times has it happened that Ashwin’s team is terrible, yet he still escapes the punishment. I think he knows something we don’t, and he’s proven that even in the lowest of lows, he doesn’t need to worry about the punishment. If he can get a win this next week against Adit’s bye week then the comeback might be real.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>8. Arjun {4-3} (-2)</strong><br/>Sadly, this team has fallen off hard. After starting 2020 with lots of promise and plenty of potential, this team hit rock bottom with the lowest game score of all time, literally 30 points. Devante Adams outscored his whole team this week, and it’s clear Arjun might need to reconsider some lineup changes. I don’t expect him to hit this low again and it’s entirely possible he starts booming next week. The key for this team is making sure it has a salvageable few weeks to keep it afloat until Chubb comes back, and hope the season isn’t too lost by then to make a playoff push. That starts with a tough matchup against Girish this week, and with the right moves he could try and get out of this decline. On top of this, you gotta make sure that at the very least you stay above Brian to avoid your side bet punishment, but knock on wood that shouldn’t be too hard.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>7. Anish {3-4} (-2)<br/></strong>The kaiju has only gone and done it, what most thought to be impossible has become possible: losing to Ashwin. Ignoring Ashwin’s effect for somehow being able to bring down his opponents when he plays them, this team might be in some trouble. Players just aren’t performing like they used to and the Jacobs trade looks like it blew up in Anish’s face. Along with that, Anish seems to be in possibly one of the hardest divisions in league history. Despite his decent record, he is last in the division with the other teams showing no signs of slowing down. He doesn’t need to just stay afloat anymore, he needs to ball out, which is a tough request looking at his upcoming schedule. Maybe his irrational need for trades can help him change his team for the coming weeks and his squad will be well equipped for the rest of the season. Hopefully he can bounce back and the players he drafted and traded for will become what they once were at the start of the season.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>6. Will {4-3} (-4)</strong><br/>Did you ever heard the Tragedy of Will the wise? I thought not. It’s not a story the League would tell you. After 1 loss, we said no big deal. After 2, we thought tough luck. But now at 3, we’re starting to worry. The league’s golden boy might find himself repeating the biggest heartbreaks in history after a big loss to Sid and terrible injuries plaguing this team. Will it happen again and Will be subject to heartbreak? Unlikely, but we don’t wanna jinx anything here. On the bright side, this team seems to hopefully be going through a small bump due to a combination of slightly lower scoring and a tough schedule, and plus if the collapse happens again it can’t be worse than last time since you’re prepared for it. In all honesty, this team hasn’t really had too many bad performances, just insane opponents. Keep up the scoring and hopefully the wins will follow. If you lose to Brian next week, who is so uninvolved in fantasy at this point that he probably hasn’t even read this far in the rankings, I’m scared of what might happen to your mental health. Please win.<br/><br/></p>

Much like it seemed like this was the year Sid would finally be good, it also seemed like this was the year Ruda would finally be bad. Sure, he’s coming off a tough loss to Akash this week but he really can’t even be mad. On top of all that, the man has literally ascended fantasy and is setting his lineups to troll his opponents. I’ve thought about this a lot especially recently, but I think the only way to describe this team is the ”<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ib7YcAUWBBM&amp;authuser=0">Feast of the East</a>” episode from Game of Zones, based of the Red Wedding from the original Game of Thrones. After the better Supreme Court hearing that happened this week, the results didn’t really matter after my commanding win over Brian. Adit {5-2} (+3)</strong><br/>Contradicting my previous statement in literally the next sentence, we find Adit really at first in the league in power rankings, the true leaderboard of the league.
