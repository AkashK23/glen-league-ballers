<p>Welcome back to your Election Week Coverage, only on GLB News! After days of excruciating vote counting, we are pleased to announce that the process has concluded and the election is over. It is finally time to hear how each of our candidates faired in this crucial election!<br/><br/>The final electoral map is as follows:<br/><br/></p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/power-rankings/2020/08-week-9-bf613a05.png"/>
<p><br/>Keep reading for detailed analyses on how each candidate performed.<br/><br/><br/><strong>10. Ashwin Inala {1-7} (-1)</strong><br/><em>Title: NAACP Officer, Illinois<br/>States Won: AK<br/>Electoral Votes: 3</em><br/><br/>Due to systemic racism or something, Mr. Inala unfortunately could not make a meaningful push with his fellow African Americans. However, he did win one state... Alaska, which he has absolutely no ties to. Reporters in Anchorage noted similar confusion among Alaskans, many of whom responded "who is that?" when asked why they voted for Mr. Inala.<br/><br/><br/><strong>9. Arjun Keshava {4-4} (-1)</strong><br/><em>Title: West Virginia Chief of Public Health<br/>States Won: WV<br/>Electoral Votes: 5</em><br/><br/>Dr. Keshava continued his plummet from grace on Election Day, finishing with only one state despite polling among the best earlier in his campaign. Poor decision after poor decision led to a staggering drop in the polls, which was confirmed by these results. He can be proud that the mountain people of his home state still believed in him, though.<br/><br/><br/><strong>8. Brian Wu {2-6} (+2)</strong><br/><em>Title: Organizer, Massachusetts "End the Curfew" Campaign<br/>States Won: MA, ME, NH, VT<br/>Electoral Votes: 22</em><br/><br/>Despite completely disappearing into the New England forests multiple times over the course of the fall, Mr. Wu actually outperformed expectations in a surprising twist. Perhaps the far Northeast, where he has lived for a staggering two months, truly believed in the message that made him famous: "yo why tf I gotta go inside at 9:30, also why is COVID resurging here". <br/><br/><br/><strong>7. Anish Kottu {3-5} (-)</strong><br/><em>Title: Entrepreneur<br/>States Won: IA, MN, ND, WI<br/>Electoral Votes: 29</em><br/><br/>Mr. Kottu experienced a similar turn of events to Dr. Keshava this campaign season, turning early hopes into almost nothing. Consistently poor performances at debates were highlighted by repeated use of the phrases "I don't really care" and "I might not even vote for myself". Still, he carried a chunk of the northern Midwest, partly due to his inexplicable attraction to the Packers' color scheme.<br/><br/><br/><strong>6. Will Furth {4-4} (-)</strong><br/><em>Title: Stand-Up Comedian<br/>States Won: AZ, CO, NV, UT<br/>Electoral Votes: 32</em><br/><br/>For the second campaign in a row, Mr. Furth was taken down by unbelievably unfortunate events. Who can forget when his campaign manager was made bedridden by an unknown illness, or when his senior advisor was struck and killed by a meteorite fragment? All these losses took their toll, but Mr. Furth, who based his operations out of Tucson, won over enough hearts in the desert Southwest.<br/><br/><br/><strong>5. Akash Krishna {4-4} (-1)</strong><br/><em>Title: Philosopher<br/>States Won: IL, MI, SD<br/>Electoral Votes: 39</em><br/><br/>Running for president because he found no other way to make money with his Philosophy degree, Mr. Krishna pulled off an impressive performance, given the odds. His home state of Michigan and neighboring Illinois are large prizes, and it is no small feat to earn them. However, he had to do a lot more to really compete with the more popular candidates.<br/><br/><br/><strong>4. Girish Ganesh {6-2} (-2)</strong><br/><em>Title: Professor, NC State University<br/>States Won: DC, NC, SC, VA<br/>Electoral Votes: 40</em><br/><br/>While Dr. Ganesh was a big-time candidate for most of his campaign, he may always be remembered for sneaking away with 40 electoral votes while earning the lowest winning voter share in this country's history. Seriously, barely anyone voted for this guy and he still won multiple large states. Guess the coastal South couldn't decide who it wanted and went with the local prof. Who started with just getting a Masters but decided to switch to a PhD and then become a prof.<br/><br/><br/><strong>3. Siddarth Moorthy {5-3} (+2)</strong><br/><em>Title: None<br/>States Won: HI, OK, TX, WA<br/>Electoral Votes: 61</em><br/><br/>Beloved by millions of Americans for seemingly no reason, Bernie San-... I mean, Sid Moorthy performed admirably on Election Day. No political experience, no job, no actual platform or political views even - it didn't matter. The American people love Mr. Moorthy and nothing could stop many of them from proudly marking down his name on their ballots. Though he did not win the election, there is no doubt that his mystical charm continues to earn him tens of millions of followers throughout the land. They will remember his name for eternity.<br/><br/><br/><strong>2. Adit Acharya {6-2} (-1)</strong><br/><em>Title: Incumbent President<br/>States Won: AL, AR, FL, GA, ID, KS, KY, LA, MO, MS, MT, NE, TN, WY<br/>Electoral Votes: 124</em><br/><br/>Through low approval ratings and the making of many enemies, Mr. Acharya still believed he would get enough turnout to take this election. And he certainly got a surprisingly high turnout, capturing all the areas that typically lean red and even the surprising prize of Florida. However, reality is beginning to set in that he is not going to win, and he doesn't appear to be taking it well. Just this morning, Mr. Acharya tweeted: <em>"THIS WHOLE F***ING COUNTRY IS F***ING RIGGED, STOP THE VOTE COUNT, STOP DEMOCRACY, STOP MODERN CIVILIZATION, IM BEING CHEATED AND THIS IS A F***ING WITCH HUNT. F*** ALL OF YOU AND YOUR F***ING ****** VOTING FOR A ******! SAD!" </em><br/><br/><br/><strong>1. Ruda Nighot {5-3} (+2)</strong><br/><em>Title: Former Vice President<br/>States Won: CA, CT, DE, IN, MD, NJ, NM, NY, OH, OR, PA, RI<br/>Electoral Votes: 183</em><br/><br/>In a truly inspirational sequence of events, Mr. Ruda Nighot has been declared winner of this election. A long-established superior member of his party, Mr. Nighot finally earned his place in history with a staggering performance that saw him seize most of the heavily populated regions in the country. After early campaign struggles, the left realized they could say his last name without handing out n-word passes, and there was no stopping Mr. Nighot after that. The President-Elect, and our top candidate in this ranking, patiently awaits the day that Mr. Acharya is carried out of the White House by the Secret Service. Congratulations, Mr. President.<br/><br/><br/>---<br/><br/>Couple things. Here's a look at our rankings over the course of the season, brought to you by Dr. Keshava himself.
