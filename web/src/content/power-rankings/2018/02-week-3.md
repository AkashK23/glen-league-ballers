<p>2018 Week 3 Power Rankings<br/><br/>Let’s talk about numbers this week, because there’s a lot of interesting stuff going on. Put simply, the first two weeks of this season have been a scoring explosion, both in the NFL and in our fantasy league. In the NFL last year there were six 400-yard performances by quarterbacks all season. This season, two weeks in? There have already been four. Look around at the scores and stats; it’s pretty insane.<br/><br/>I think there’s a common trend in our fantasy league to overestimate how often teams score high. A lot of us probably think that to be good, a team consistently needs to score 100 week in and week out. Well, let’s look at some historical numbers. Averaging 100 points a week obviously makes a team finish with 1300 points in our 13-week season. Only eight team seasons in our four-year history have done this (Ruda 3x, Ashwin, Adit, Akash, Anish, and Will, spread out across the years); that’s two a year. I’d consider that “elite” more than “good”. Anyway, through two weeks, we have SIX teams at or above the 100-a-week mark. Obviously two weeks isn’t much, but hear me out.<br/><br/>I make these two points for the same reason. While there’s a chance it’s just going to be a wacky season (like 2016), odds are we’re going to see some major regression across the board. NFL teams will stop playing college games, and many of our fantasy teams are most likely going to take a hit. The question isn’t so much “if” as it is “when” and “how much”. Also, “who” because historically speaking, two of us will survive it. There’s a storm coming.<br/><br/>10: Sid (-)<br/>Sid did pretty much exactly what everyone expected him to do: die horribly. The lowest scorer of the week by a full 25 points, he doesn’t have much to be excited about until Ingram, Jeffery, and Bell come back... if the latter ever happens. Glancing at Sid’s scoreboard tells you that this team is basically Cam Newton and no one else, much like the Panthers themselves. David Johnson is being held back by an incredibly awful Cardinals offense, Amari Cooper scores either 10+ on Sid’s bench or 0.2 as a starter, and REX BURKHEAD IS NOT A VIABLE FANTASY STARTER. The name “Flex Burkhead” was a joke I made because starting him even as a flex seemed laughable. He is Sid’s RB2. Sid’s actual flex scored 0.7. Also his kicker died. There are better days ahead, but this team better hope they are MUCH better days or the reaper is a-comin’.<br/><br/>9: Ashwin (-2)<br/>Yeesh. This was a manageable matchup against a mediocre team, but da Peppers weren’t up to the challenge. If the Cardinals offense is ruining DJ, the Bills offense is pretty much ending McCoy’s career. Ashwin’s WRs apparently took a wrong turn and missed the stadium, and there was very little on the bench to inspire any confidence. There are some positives; Ajayi scored again, and is looking like a rock-solid starter with over 30 points in two weeks. Brown and Allen are sure to recover, and the Chicago investment paid a few dividends this week. Those bright spots better get real luminous real soon, or this 0-2 team is headed for the gutter.<br/><br/>8: Girish (-)<br/>A win is a win, and it’s not like scoring 90 points is that bad. Remember, we can’t keep acting like it’s necessary to hit 100 every week. It was nice to see the Nuklear Bombs’ receivers do what was expected of them and combine for about 37 points. The concern lies with... everything else. Crowell is trash and Yeldon is temporary, and Ebron cannot continue his current rate of production. What separates Girish from the teams below him are the RBs he inexplicably benched: Drake and Lynch. They won’t ever singlehandedly win him games, but they should provide enough 7-14 point efforts each that the talented wide receivers can put this team in a respectable place. That can only happen if Girish actually starts them, though.<br/></p>

<p>7: Akash (+2)<br/>Hey, that was pretty good! A hard-fought rollercoaster win actually makes this team pretty hard to read; this week’s 109 point performance was a 30-point increase over last week’s. The star of the show was clearly Stefon Diggs, who managed to outshine a fearsome performance on the other end by AJ Green. Hunt wasn’t bad, and Coleman and Lindsay produced well. The trade right before games started may have been ill-advised, however, and that’s a big reason why Akash isn’t moving up further just yet. Coleman will be relegated to backup duties with Freeman returning soon, and it’s clear that Jared Cook is no Zach Ertz. You have to be hopeful for Will Fuller, but we’re not convinced that the rest of this bench is even decent, contrary to what Akash would tell you. Maybe Gordon can work some magic in New England? He’ll either score high or get high, I guess. Whatever happens, I’m sure we’re in for some charming lighthearted commentary every Sunday from our friend Akash.<br/></p>

<p>6: Anish (-3)<br/>It has to be frustrating, scoring so much and simply being unable to win. This team is great and, like before, the rankings start to get murky around here. It’s hard to determine who would beat who, so record starts playing a significant role in rankings and Anish is unfortunately winless. It’s not for lack of effort, though. Gurley is an absolute monster and is the only one of the top four running backs playing at the level expected so far, with 50 points in two weeks. Davante Adams is also producing as Anish hoped, being the Packers’ main redzone threat. Gronk had a rough week but obviously will return to form in time. After those three, though, things get murky. Wilson is struggling and Big Ben isn’t going to throw for 450 yards every week (see: intro above). Royce Freeman seems to be in freefall as Phillip Lindsey earns a larger share. Also, Anish simply isn’t making good lineup choices so far. This team is very talented, but it’s stuck firmly around the middle of the rankings unless a few things change.<br/><br/>5: Brian (+1)<br/>Melvin Gordon is the heart and soul of this solid team, and he’s doing it despite Ekeler vulturing major yardage. The potent LA offense and Gordon’s brute strength in short yardage make for a deadly combo and we see no reason for that to slow down anytime soon. The big headline for the Sloppy Joes, however, is the intriguing benching of Aaron Rodgers for surprise star Ryan Fitzpatrick. Statistically the move makes sense considering his unbelievable 72 points through two weeks, but you have to wonder if and when FitzMagic becomes FitzTragic. Two of those four 400-yard performances were Fitzpatrick himself. To be fair, he has a tasty matchup with Pittsburgh this week, so we’ll back Brian’s decision to start him. For now.<br/><br/>As for why this team isn’t ranked higher, take a look at the rest of the players. While Mixon is great, he’s sidelined for a little while, and Beckham is just not exploding in this unbelievably lame Giants offense. Marvin Jones and Kyle Rudolph are meh, though Sanders is a bright spot. We also can’t expect the Bears defense to score 19 every week. Those points need to come from somewhere.<br/><br/>4: Adit (-2)<br/>Nothing sums up this week’s game between Adit and Akash like this picture:</p>

<p>Watching Patrick Mahomes and Matt Breida combine for 60 points on your bench after considering starting both is... frustrating, to say the least. The wild loss slows down the hype train for Adit, but honestly there were a lot of positives. First and foremost, the electrifying young quarterback from Kansas City will take the reigns as leader of this team. With TEN touchdowns in the first two weeks, Mahomes has a shot at being this year’s Wentz even with expected regression. AJ Green is a monster and made trading away Bell seem worth it. Evans and Tate are both getting heavily targeted, and Jimmy Graham looks like he’s starting to get a little more comfortable in the Green Bay offense. Holding this team back are its iffy running backs. Freeman’s health issues are concerning, and Derrick Henry is looking more and more like a bust. Hyde has proven to be great value, but there definitely needs to be a second running back producing well. Adit needs to hope Freeman comes back soon, especially since this next week’s matchup with Will may not go so well.<br/><br/>3: Arjun (+1)<br/>This is the best 1-1 team in the league, moving up a spot despite the loss. Arjun took a page out of Ruda’s 2017 boom, investing heavily in the KC offense. It has paid off. Hill and Kelce are having monstrous starts to the season, and combined with JuJu were able to easily make up for a mediocre day by Kamara. The biggest point of concern is at quarterback; Alex Smith is shaky at best. Cook is playing pretty well, but not enough to justify his second-round pick. Also, Demaryius Thomas doesn’t really make any sense. There’s a lot of potential on the bench, though, which will come in handy soon as bye weeks start to roll in. We expect Kamara to score a lot more than 12 points most weeks, so if Arjun’s other star players continue their level of play and he starts involving some of those bench players as needed, he’s going to have a great season.<br/><br/>2: Ruda (+3)<br/>After falling three places last week, Zeke Squad jumps right back to where they started. Dropping 120 points and leading the week in scoring will do that. Ruda is currently tied with Arjun for the season lead in PF (yes, tied - both have exactly 230.4 points) and so far looks like the clear favorite to top the West. Zeke will pick up the pace a little, and McCaffrey is becoming ridiculously heavily involved in the Panthers offense (14 receptions! That’s the fourth-most by an RB in NFL history). We can’t expect Golladay to keep producing, but the smart trade for Ertz should make up for that. With no end in sight for Bell’s holdout, Conner will continue to score well and at this point may even earn some playing time if Bell comes back. Ruda also consistently picks the good lineups and we expect that to continue. This team is probably staying put in the top 3 for some time.<br/><br/>1: Will (-)<br/>Before we get started on some well-deserved praise for Will, I want to throw out some historical facts that make this coming matchup with Adit an intriguing one. This isn’t just for bragging, I promise; just hear out these ridiculous statistics. In our four years playing together, Will and Adit have faced off four times in the regular season and once in the playoffs. Adit is 5-0 in these matchups, winning by a combined score of 703.8 to 440.7. Yes, you heard that right. The average Will-Adit game sees Will lose by 44 points. The largest margin of victory in a regular season game (95) AND the largest margin of victory in a playoff game (97) both were Adit over Will.<br/><br/>So why am I spouting all this? Well first of all, it’s kinda funny. But really, I’m just showcasing how crazy this curse is because if there’s ever a time for Will to break it, it’s now. This is perhaps the best team he has ever fielded. Kirk Cousins adapted to the Minnesota offense remarkably fast, and Saquon Barkley’s usage is ridiculous. Remember how CMC had the fourth-most receptions of all time on Sunday with 14? Barkley hit 14 as well. He’s only bound to improve, and so is Jordan Howard. Michael Thomas is receiving a huge chunk of Brees’s attention, important in a year where the Saints seem less run-dependent. Thielen is a perfect complement to Cousins, Jordan Reed looks like Alex Smith’s favorite new target, and Brandin Cooks can casually put up 160 yards on Will’s bench. The only position of any concern here is defense, and maybe flex if Cooks/Miller can’t keep it up. <br/><br/><br/>This is the best team in the league right now. Will has one hurdle, one monkey to rip off his back, before he’s ready to have the season of his dreams. Let’s see if he can #breakthecurse.<br/><br/><br/><br/><br/>#pleasedont</p>
