<p>10: Ashwin {2-8} (-)<br/>Atleast the Bears are having a decent season, because Ashwin team’s has been pretty horrible so far. Fresh off a 40 point whopping by Girish, Ashwin is likely locked into the last spot for the Reaper Bracket. It’s an unsurprising ranking for the least-talented team that only has 3 players averaging double digit scoring. What is surprising is the lack of action taken by Ashwin, as he failed to put in any waiver claims. I think Ashwin actually just wants to eat the reaper.<br/><br/>9: Sid {4-6} (-)<br/>I still don’t understand how Sid scored freaking 128 points against me a while ago. It’s a been a crazy season for the Kid, as he’s seen high draft picks like David Johnson largely disappoint while unlikely heroes like George “Tea” Kittle have emerged. This week, Sid faces off against Ashwin in a battle of the bottom. With an improving Johnson, good WR corps, and SuperCam, he has a decent shot at climbing back to .500. <br/><br/>8: Girish {4-6} (-1)<br/>How the mighty have fallen. After clutching out a championship ring last year, Girish has been essentially eliminated from the playoffs. We’re seeing the results of his questionable No RB Draft strategy late in the year, as he is starting Derrick Henry and the Colts #2 RB (albeit bye’s are at play). One saving grace is the Brees trade, as after rocky initial results, the Saints QB has been amazing for Girish recently. A tough matchup with Ruda awaits, and it’ll be one to watch, as Girish and Ruda have had a ton of close matchups recently (all 3 last matchups have been decided by 5 points or less). <br/><br/>7: Will {4-6} (-1)<br/>Flat out, I think this team is a lot better than its record indicates. The RB’s are solid, the WR’s are solid, and it has a lot of depth. I can’t quite pinpoint what went wrong for Will. A tough PA may be to explain, as Will has the 2nd highest PA this year. He seems to be at the top of that category on an annual basis. Although playoffs seem like a long shot here, Will can still play spoiler as he tries to take down an Arjun team that’s missing James White and Ghost. <br/><br/>6: Adit {5-5} (+2)<br/>For a team that looked like it was dead in the water after a 3 game losing streak, Adit surprisingly finds himself back in the thick of the playoff race. His trades for RB’s came up huge last week in an upset win over Binkie, and he hopes that the combo of Hambones, Ingram, and Kerryon can lead to another upset win over division leading Akash. The WR’s are also bolstered by getting Diggs basically for free, so I’d say Adit has a shot at another great win this week.<br/><br/>5: Anish {5-5} (-)<br/>I am of the personal opinion that a team with Gurley should never lose, but I give Anish a pass here because his TE, Flex, and Defense combined for .1 points last week. He has a matchup against a flailing Brian up next. Gronk has been a really massive disappointment this year, but it’s possible that he comes back in time to make an impact in the playoffs for Binkie. Still, a team with a hot Big Ben, Gurley, and Adams has to be feared. Also Anish if you are reading this I don’t think Watkins is gonna play so don’t make the mistake of starting him (again). <br/><br/>4: Brian {6-4} (-1)<br/>The pace at which Brian’s season has gone off the rails has been Adit 2017-like. Playoffs seemed almost guaranteed for him 2 weeks ago at 6-2, but now he’s on the bubble. Brian hasn’t topped 80 points recently, and the ill-timed trade of Diggs isn’t going to help the cause. A tough matchup with Anish awaits, and if Brian falls ot 6-5, I think he might enter a new level of depression. Nonetheless, I still believe in the strong RB corps of Mixon and MG3, and a stud TE in Kelce is key. Here’s to hoping that the ghost of Dez in Brian’s IR spot gives him some good luck. <br/><br/>3: Arjun {5-5} (+1)<br/>I wish Arjun’s phone was working last week because I would have killed to see his reaction to his awful loss to Akash, as he became the first one in GIB’s history to lose by less than one point. He can look past that though, as 2 out of his next 3 opponents have losing records, which means he’s likely for the playoffs. Matt Ryan has been a revelation for this team. Now only if he can figure out his tricky TE spot, he should be a tough opponent the rest of the way. <br/><br/>2: Akash {7-3} (-)<br/>The king of late comebacks, Akash has been on an absolute tear the past 4 weeks. He hasn’t scored below 109 in this span, and all of his production seems sustainable. Matthew Berry was actually right for once as Aaron Jones has been a godsend. Add him onto Sony Michel, Kareem “the Dream” Hunt, OBJ, and the lord Gordon, and Akash has a lot of star power. One position I’ve seen Akash struggle with is Kicker, and that’ll be important to watch, especially in the playoffs where every choice is crucial.<br/><br/>1: Ruda {8-2} (-)<br/>Ruda has only scored below 110 once, back in Week 3 in a grinder against Girish. He faces him again this week, although both teams look drastically different. Julio has changed sides, and has given Ruda some much needed WR help. The RB’s are among the best in the league, as Le'veon Bell has continued to help Ruda despite not being on his team this year. One position of slight concern is QB, as Rivers is good-not-great and Brady is looking washed-up this year. Perhaps Lamar Jackson can give this team a jolt. <br/><br/></p>
