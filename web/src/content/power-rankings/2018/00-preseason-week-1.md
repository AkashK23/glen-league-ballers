<p>2018 Preseason Power Rankings<br/><br/>Welcome back to the GIB league as it kicks off its fifth season. Ahead of opening day (next Thursday), let’s go over our teams and their rankings.<br/><br/>10: Akash<br/>It’s hard to fully discount anyone before the season begins, and nearly impossible to predict what will happen. Maybe Akash’s risky picks exceed expectations. For now, though, it has to be assumed that they won’t. There are a few stellar players here; Hunt and Fournette form a formidable RB corps and Luck is primed for a major bounce-back season. Ertz is always a safe bet, especially while Alshon Jeffery is out. The problem lies with the wide receivers. Not only is Diggs the second receiver on his own team, but Josh Gordon is a major risk for obvious reasons and likely won’t even start initially. “Flex” Burkhead is, well, bad; never trust a Patriots RB. While there are some promising prospects on the bench, right now there’s nowhere to rank “Booker Kottu” but last.<br/><br/>9: Girish<br/>The reigning champion had one of the odder drafts in recent history. Essentially, Girish accidentally went Zero-RB in a year that RBs kind of suck after a certain point. Nuk, Julio, and Hilton form what could be the most intimidating WR corps since Brian’s OBJ-Dez-DT combo in 2014, but at what cost?<br/><br/><a href="https://goo.gl/images/8fZBwE">https://goo.gl/images/8fZBwE</a><br/><br/>These RBs are weak. Lynch is on the back end of his career, and Drake is bound for some regression. The bench backs don’t inspire much hope, either. In fact, the entire bench is pretty pathetic. The wide receivers, Watson, and Olsen can carry the Nuklear Bombs to some wins, but it’s hard to imagine a stellar season in their current state.<br/><br/>8: Ashwin<br/>Are we looking at yet another mediocre season for this franchise?<br/><br/><br/>Yes.<br/><br/>McCoy is aging and on an offense bound to struggle. Ajayi is on one of the most heavily split RB committees in the league after NE. The Texans defense... isn’t good. I don’t really know who Trey Burton is. Actually, this team has a strange dependence on Chicago. What an exciting offense. Like Girish, however, the WRs provide some hope. Antonio Brown is a scary boi and if Keenan Allen can stay healthy, he’s an absolute force. There’s a chance that Allen Robinson (retch) returns to form as well. This team is very boom or bust, depending on a few factors.<br/><br/>7: Sid<br/>One day, Sid will be high in the rankings. Today is not that day. That’s not to say this team is devoid of stars. Cam is always one of the safest bets to finish as a top 5 QB, and David Johnson is possibly the most talented running back in the league. AJ Green, often forgotten about, is a threat to go off for 25+ any week. Plus, Ingram on the bench will give this team a boost when he comes back. The problem lies in the remaining players. Sid’s love affair with Robby Anderson is cute, but unwarranted; he is bound for regression and finished last year horribly anyway. Engram won’t get targets with both OBJ and Barkley added to the offense. McKinnon is talented but a total toss-up. And Amari Cooper is probably going to give Sid an aneurysm by Week 4. Sid probably isn’t taking the West this year.<br/><br/>6: Will<br/>Now we’re getting to the good stuff, and the stuff that’s harder to rank. Looking at Will’s team will immediately impress you. The core of this team will be Barkley, Howard, Thielen, and Thomas; Will could do a lot worse. I’d also argue that Broccoli’s bench is stellar. Cooks, Shepard, AP, and Ross are all great potential FLEX plays. The reason Will’s team falls below the other contenders is risk. No one really knows how Barkley will perform in a mediocre offense. Howard’s team is supposedly moving to a pass-happy offense. And a new quarterback (hey look, Will has him too) in Minnesota raises some questions about how seamlessly the offense can get back to form. This is a good team, but things could really easily fall apart.<br/><br/>5: Brian<br/>Chef Gordon Ramsey is going to have some incredible weeks. There will be times where Rodgers hits 32 while OBJ nabs multiple touchdowns, right as Gordon takes a screen to the house and Jones puts up ridiculous yardage, giving Brian a solid 130. It won’t happen every time, though, and the other players on Brian’s team aren’t amazing. Jones himself is going to regress massively, and Sanders hasn’t produced properly in years. The Jags D is great but will inevitably stop scoring so many damn touchdowns. And no one except Brian is going to be surprised when Mixon turns out to be mediocre af.<br/><br/><a href="https://goo.gl/images/cLUZTS">https://goo.gl/images/cLUZTS</a><br/><br/>He’ll finish exactly as RB17. Mark my words.<br/><br/>4: Arjun<br/>This team is out to prove that it was underrated last year. Drafting Kamara, Smith, and JuJu again was a bold move, but probably a good one. I do believe Kamara will slow down (he had 6.1 YPC last year; that is unsustainable), but Ingram’s suspension ups his volume. Tyreek Hill, already a deadly deep threat, will likely benefit from Patrick Mahomes’s cannon of an arm, and Ruda showed last year that owning multiple Chiefs isn’t a bad investment. Dalvin Cook was a risky and expensive pick; he’s the starting running back on a solid offense, but check out his stats from before the injury. He had one great week and two solid one in four games. It was a good look, but does that make him worth the second round pick? Guess we’ll find out.<br/><br/>Also, DT sucks.<br/><br/>3: Adit<br/>Has the curse been broken? It sure looks like it after a solid draft for this franchise. Adit continued his trend towards RB-heavy squads by selecting the juggernaut trio of Bell, Freeman, and Henry; no doubt the best corps in the league right now. Brees was a great value pick late in the draft, as well. Some good bench players like Alshon Jeffery cement the Liberty Bells as an early playoff contender. The question marks lie with the WRs - can Evans return to form after a down year? We typically know what we’re getting out of Golden Tate, so a lot will ride on Evans’s shoulders. And Jameis Winston.<br/><br/><a href="https://goo.gl/images/yGBpXM">https://goo.gl/images/yGBpXM</a><br/><br/>Yay. Also, Jimmy Graham has a lot of potential but tends to randomly suck every now and then. I’m sure Mike McCarthy will do his absolute best to utilize Graham in the worst way possible. At least this team has an opera-singing kicker. Watch for the Liberty Bells and its lightning running backs to start off hot.<br/><br/>2: Ruda<br/>Fucking stop. After making the last two championship games and winning one, Ruda has primed himself for a great season yet again. Zeke Squad’s flagship player is probably going to do really well - consider that he finished as a top 10 RB last year despite missing six games. SIX. Offensive line woes may slow him down, but they won’t stop him from being elite. Brady is the signal caller for this franchise once again - can’t go wrong with him. McCaffrey forms a nice complement to Elliott, giving this team a solid foundation on its running backs. And never let yourself forget about the grace of Larry Fitzgerald, or the incredibly wide eyes of Chris Hogan. Seriously, those guys are underrated WRs who will help this team more than they hurt it. I don’t like Jamaal Williams at FLEX, but that’s not enough to bring this team down. Ruda can take the West.<br/><br/>1: Anish<br/>“Hi this is Anish, I can’t take your call right now.” Probably because he was busy doing some serious homework for this draft, and man did it show. The only questionable starter on this squad is Royce Freeman, and even he will probably pan out in time. Wilson is a top 5 QB lock, Gurley is the RB to have this year (smart pick trading by Anish), and Davante Adams is a good bet to lead the league in touchdowns. Throw in Gronk just for kicks. Alex Collins is locked in as a capable starter in Baltimore, as well. The bench is what holds this team back, although Edelman should provide valuable flex material once he returns from his suspension. There aren’t many weaknesses I can pick out, other than the god awful fucking SHIT ASS name. But that’s for another power ranking.<br/><br/>Anish actually ranked himself second here, but I convinced him he should be on top. Mainly because I have a burning desire to be close to Ruda. But also because if I had to pick a champion right now, it’s Anish.<br/><br/><br/><br/>Football is a random sport and anything can happen. These rankings might be a joke in two or three weeks. But that’s the fun part. Let’s have a great fifth season, and may the best team win.<br/><br/><br/><br/><br/><br/>Unlike last year.</p>
