<p>It was an intense and emotional week for most teams in the league. The scoring average dropped back to 97.5, though that number is heavily skewed by one massive (and historic) shootout, which we'll discuss later. Most teams scored low - shockingly low, in fact. The East is in absolute shambles after its top three teams (ranked 2, 3, and 4 last week) averaged a meager 79.2 and all lost. That division is now anyone's for the taking, whereas the elite are separating themselves from the pack in the West; this is heavily reflected in the rankings. Let's dive in.<br/><br/>10: Ashwin {1-4} (-)<br/>A very impressive win over the #2 team might normally be a huge boost in rankings, but Ashwin remains last place in standings and didn't exactly explode last week. 91.1 is a solid score and, most importantly, Antonio Brown finally had the game we were all waiting for him to have. Still, Ashwin is by far last in PF (on pace for 1063) and just lost Jay Ajayi for the season. Let's keep careful track of McCoy-Eagles trade rumors, but for now there's no reason to rank this team above any other.<br/><br/>9: Girish {2-3} (-)<br/>The Nuklear Bombs topped 100 for the second straight week and earned a much-needed win over a very angry Sid. So why does he remain at 9? Well, TJ Yeldon isn't going to be the starter much longer, and the signing of Jamaal Charles isn't great. It's great to see the Texans offense continue to find its identity (Watson and Hopkins combined for 34), and Eric Ebron is the real deal at tight end - currently #2. But Julio is disappointing, Kerryon showed he isn't ready to be an RB2 yet, Lynch is completely touchdown-dependent, and Hilton continues to be sidelined with an injury. Olsen's return would be exciting if Ebron wasn't already tearing it up, so that doesn't add much to this team. Girish could prove us wrong by beating a suddenly questionable Adit this week. <br/><br/>8: Sid {2-3} (-)<br/>The magical mini-streak came to an end on Monday, but left Sid's team with hope for the future. It was a slight down week in a weird game for Cam, but David Johnson (henceforth known as Django) looked like his old self and the new secret weapon, Mark Ingram, made quite an entrance. We don't talk about the old secret weapon, who was he anyway? Rumors of Bell's return grow every day, and George Kittle is actually a really good tight end in a horribly disappointing year for the position. Sid has more upside than a lot of lower-tier teams, but needs to catch up quick in a division whose top two teams are pulling away. Let's be real, though. He's just going to start Amari Cooper again and wail when he scores exactly 1.2 points.</p>

<p>7: Akash {3-2} (-1)<br/>It wasn't even a great week for Akash, but this was no normal week in the East. Akash toppled Will and moved to 3-2, placing him (and every other team) in the running for the division. He moves down only because Anish did better, but just know that this division is wide open; the only thing holding him back is a lowish PF (6th). Akash is still tops in the league with 531.6 PA (on pace for 1382 - would be an all-time record) so his 3 wins are impressive, but he'll need to score more than 81.5 in the future. Especially against Brian next week.<br/><br/>6: Anish {2-3} (+1)<br/>Now here's the battle that really destroyed the division. Shoving Arjun down to 2-3 and scoring 107 was impressive, and places Anish in a decent spot to rattle off a few wins and rise in standings. I don't even know what more I can say about Todd Gurley... except this: Gurley is on pace for 385.6 points, which would be the 7th best standard-scoring fantasy season of all time. Right in between Aaron Rodgers of 2016 and Cam Newton of 2015 (AND WHO OWNED BOTH, I MIGHT ADD???). Point is, Anish basically has an elite quarterback playing as his running back. Also, no one really believes Gronk will keep getting held in check, do they?</p>

<p>5: Brian {3-2} (-)<br/><br/><br/>jk gotchu didnt i<br/><br/>5: Will {3-2} (-2)<br/>How the mighty have fallen. Well, that's a bit dramatic. Will has still hit 100+ in three of the five weeks so far, and the combination of Barkley, Thielens, and Thomas is going to guarantee him several wins this year. Barkley especially is really living up to the hype, especially considering that Bell, Django, and AB were taken ahead of him. But there's no ignoring the scary fact that, after bursting out of the gate with 119 points in Week 1, Will has managed to score less than the previous week every single week since then. That downward trend is what keeps this team from being ranked higher. Still, I want to point out that from here on out the teams get very close and difficult to rank; only our #1 team has put any sort of distance between himself and the others. Will is only about 5% worse than our #2 team, and he can prove that with a potential beat down of Ashwin this week.<br/><br/>4: Arjun {2-3} (-)<br/>Now that Brian-5 memes have ended, guess it's time for Arjun-4. Seriously, Arjun has been ranked #4 in five out of the six weeks so far. It's not like we planned that, it just always seems to work out. Anyway, the East has been destroyed, Arjun flopped, all ye who have sinned must repent, yada yada. A clear flaw was exposed in this team, and it's that Arjun is way too dependent on Kamara. Prior to this week, Kamara accounted for over 20% of Arjun's points three times out of five. Naturally, when Kamara crapped the bed, Arjun scored his lowest total of the season and lost. That's reason for concern, because a good chunk of that lost production went instead to Mark Ingram in his return. Still, I don't see Kamara being held under 4 (or 10) again soon, and the Chiefs won't be playing the Jags every week. In fact, they play the Patriots this week. Slurp. Expect a big bounce-back from Arjun. He'll need it against Ruda.<br/><br/>3: Adit {3-2} (-1)<br/>Things were exciting for about 30 minutes on Sunday. The fun ended there. The good news is that Adit managed to maintain his grip on the division, for now. Note, however, that ranks 7 through 3 are all in the East. No team is standing out yet and anyone could beat anyone. Now a lot of Adit's problems this week are things we can expect to go away; Golden Tate is a big yardage guy and will have more than 40 usually, and Hyde will see enough goal line work to return to his scoring ways. Plus, as mentioned earlier, Mahomes won't be playing the Jaguars every week. The concern is, once again, mounting injuries. Breida is hurt and will likely miss time, and now Freeman is suddenly in doubt as well. While this team's quarterback and WR situations are ridiculously solid, huge issues in the RB corps could spell major trouble. Adit will try to maintain his precarious lead with a win over Girish - or risk falling once again to .500.<br/><br/>2: Brian {3-2} (+3)</p>

<p>This is it. It finally happened. It's real. This is not a drill. Brian isn't ranked five anymore. In fact, he has shot up three spots - tied for the largest jump this season since Week 2 (tied with none other than Ruda). Now, that jump was a combination of an excellent performance, despite the loss, and terrible days by all three Eastern teams ahead of him. But it shows that Brian is putting together consistent success and deserves to be in the conversation with the top teams. Melvin Gordon, Joe Mixon, and James White are powering the way, each scoring double digits week in and week out. I don't think the OBJ trade was a good one - pretty much no one does - but it's not like Diggs won't be a solid player for Brian. Brian is currently second in PF (though still dead last in PA) and there isn't much reason to believe this team will fall off entirely anytime soon. Enjoy being almost on top, for now.<br/><br/>1: Ruda {4-1} (-)<br/>The only semblance of consistency, both in this season and across the last few years, Ruda is a tier above the other teams once again. 572.4 points is absurd, absolutely absurd. That would translate to 1488 points in a whole season - compare to the current record of 1386... also by Ruda. By the way, third place? 1364, Ruda again. The last three and a half seasons have been an incredible and dominating ride for this franchise. Will this season's pace continue? Probably not. 55 of Ruda's 135 points came from James Conner and the Cincy D/ST(???), which probably won't happen again. But armed with Tom Brady (who, I assume, will not be sitting happily on the bench in the future), Ezekiel Elliott, Christian McCaffrey, the REAL Babytron, Zach Ertz, and yes, James Conner... this is the team to beat yet again. If Ruda can pull off a tough win against Arjun next week he'll move to 5-1 and those playoff chances will start looking mighty good. <br/><br/>...but not 100% &lt;sobs in remembrance&gt;.<br/><br/>Most important thing to remember is that it's still early folks. Don't look at these rankings and sit comfortably or give up. We have seen the incredible things that can happen down the stretch, and we'll probably see some more in the next two months. It's anyone's ring to win. Game on.<br/></p>
