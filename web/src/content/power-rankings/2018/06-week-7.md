<p>Half the league topped 100, while the other half kinda sucked this week. Though the East continues its self-destruction, we are now at the halfway point approaching Week 7 and it’s time to start discussing the playoff picture. That’s going to be the theme of this week’s discussion.<br/><br/>10: Girish {2-4} (-1)<br/>One of three 2-4 teams, Girish has to be in panic mode at this point. Though he lost narrowly, he only scored 77. This is an example of what happens when the Texans offense collapses, and it’s not pretty. His three Texans combined for only 25, and to add to it Julio was kept out of the endzone yet again. If even greater concern is the looming return of Fournette. I literally don’t know who Girish is going to start at RB once that happens. At 2-4, he needs to almost win out to make the playoffs, and that’s going to take a level of improvement I don’t see happening.<br/><br/>9: Ashwin {2-4} (+1)<br/>We can’t ignore scoring more than 210 points in two weeks, and as a result have pulled Ashwin out of the dungeon for the first time since Week 3. We’ve had a 7-6 playoff team before, and an 8-5 non-playoff team, but generally we can assume that 8-5 (like 10-6 in the NFL) means playoffs, and Ashwin needs to go 6-1 down the stretch for that. The good news is that most of Ashwin’s players, even his benched ones, performed well last week. Wentz should continue to improve, and Antonio Brown is back to his show-stopping ways. Buttttt 6-1 is never easy. It’ll take a miracle.<br/><br/>8: Sid {2-4} (+1)<br/>What a short-lived period of excitement. Like Ashwin, Sid’s two wins came in succession, only that they feel like distant memories at this point. Again depending on Cam Newton for a full 30% of his points, Sid looked a lot like his old self. The disappearance of Alfred Morris was a perplexing fluke, but there are a lot of other things to worry about. Like how Sid plans to win six of his next seven games, especially as Bell continues to apparently just sit on his computer and tweet all day.<br/><br/>7: Arjun {2-4} (-3)<br/>A big drop, I know. Arjun is easily the best of the 2-4 teams and will likely see his winning percentage climb steadily in the future. He does, after all, lead the entire East in scoring despite being in absolute last place. It’s just hard to put him above some teams that are showing potential, like Anish. I know Arjun scored 107 last week, but are we really expecting 32 points from Hill every week? Not to mention that 22 from Gostkowski. In this strange division though, it’s still anyone’s game. If there’s any 2-4 team with a chance to turn it around, it’s this one.<br/><br/>6: Akash {3-3} (+1)<br/>Before Sony Michel and Kareem Hunt played, Akash had six players combine for 20.3 points. I’ll let that sink in.<br/><br/>Yes, Akash did rise in the rankings, but that’s a product of (shocker) this inexplicable division and... well that’s pretty much it. He’s also last in PF in the East. Honestly Anish I don’t know why he’s above Arjun, but it’s already Saturday and if I asked you I’d get a response by like Tuesday. Akash you better hope Sony Michel doesn’t go the way of every Patriots RB ever.<br/><br/>5: Will {3-3} (-)<br/>I’ll go ahead and spoil it now: all the Eastern teams are ranked together in one big blob centered around 5. Will, then, is the most average of the average. Which honestly isn’t the worst thing in the world considering we’re still at the halfway point of the season. Will needs to finish up by going 5-2; it sounds a little daunting but keep in mind that only three teams have to win less than 5 remaining games. Broccoli is right in the thick of it. What Will needs to fix is the scoring slide; after averaging 110.4 over the first three weeks, he’s averaged just 86 in the three weeks since.<br/><br/>4: Anish {3-3} (+2)<br/>Anish put on a show as he routed Sid by nearly 50 points. This is the fifth time in six weeks Anish has scored 100+ (the sixth game was... slightly worse). It’s just pretty much impossible for any team with Todd Gurley to be bad right now. Anish was struggling with depth at the RB position, but with Coleman suddenly thrust into the starter position for at least 8 weeks, that has been addressed well. It’s a little shocking, but Anish is suddenly looking like a dangerous team that could make some playoff-contending noise.<br/><br/>3: Adit {4-2} (-)<br/>A win is a win, though this one was ugly for sure. The Warriors have scored just 162 points in the last two weeks, and it took a monumental effort from Patrick Mahomes and Jimmy Graham to seal the comeback win over Girish late Monday night. The good news is that there’s no way the receiving corps continues to struggle, but the terrible news is the absolute devastation at the running back position. With Freeman out long-term, Adit has to find replacement options ASAP.<br/><br/>2: Brian {4-2} (-)<br/>What’s not to like about this team right now? Okay, let’s get this out of the way: Gordon and Butker won’t score 33 and 18 points a week, respectively. But we honestly CAN expect Rodgers to keep getting 28. Mixon is doing exactly what you’d hope as an RB2, and with the Chargers suddenly looking very strong on offense Gordon should have plenty of great games left in him. What a great flex James White is too! I’m sure Brian would never trade him. Unfortunately his WRs are ass.<br/><br/>Adit and Brian both are shooting to go 4-3 or better. That’s not a bad place to be in at all; to say that you can start doing worse than you’ve already done and still make the playoffs is pretty great. They haven’t set themselves far enough apart to start worrying about seeding yet, though. In fact, they’ll play each other next week in a crucial matchup. Let’s see how that goes first.<br/><br/>1: Ruda {5-1} (-)<br/>Coming as a surprise to absolutely no one, Ruda tops our rankings for the fourth week in a row. I’m sure that’s approaching some sort of record, but I’m too lazy to check. It has been a full month since this team scored less than 116 points - that is incredible. Ruda’s pace is now approaching 1500 on the season. Keep in mind that the previous record is less than 1400; we could be skipping the 1400’s entirely here. Are we witnessing history? Maybe, so long as Bell stays home. Ruda has to just go 3-4 to make the playoffs - with his PF so high he shouldn’t need to worry about tiebreakers keeping him out at 8-5. This is the only team in the league right now that is looking past just making the playoffs, and trying to secure the highest seed. Ruda can start that by beating Akash next week.</p>
