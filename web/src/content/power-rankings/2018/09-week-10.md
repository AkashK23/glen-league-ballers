<p>In a season full of weird weeks, this one was actually pretty normal as division matchups returned. We had a close game or two (including a wild finish), an upset, and one super high scorer. Honestly, couldn’t we all use this breath of fresh air? The world didn’t fall apart for once.<br/><br/>10: Ashwin {2-7} (-1)<br/>In one of the more embarrassing defeats in recent memory, Ashwin was chewed up and spit out by a vastly superior championship contender. Last in PF and wins, this team has a grim outlook and little hope for change. Maybe start getting that spice tolerance up, bud.<br/><br/>9: Sid {3-6} (+1)<br/>For the first time in a month, Sid won a game. He also put a final end to his Bell experiment, trading him for Chris Carson in what now appears to be a great move despite Carson’s moderate injury. But this is still a team that has only topped 100 once this season. Bad.<br/><br/>8: Adit {4-5} (+1)<br/>What a tragic season for Adit. What started off as a good team has been decimated by injuries, trades, and jet skis. Thanks to an excellent start and a tight division, Adit is actually totally in the running. Something has to change though. Those RBs need to make the trades worth it.<br/><br/>7: Girish {3-6} (+1)<br/>Huuuuuge rivalry win. Girish seems to beat Brian a lot, doesn’t he? Girish has actually had a pretty whacky alternating season. Check out his scores the last several weeks: 76, 120, 102, 77, 105, 76, 104. The 80’s and 90’s feeling left out, man.<br/><br/>6: Will {4-5} (-1)<br/>That had to be the second most heartbreaking loss of the season, though it was a spectacular game. It looked like Michael Thomas sealed the win for Will with a huge 75 yard touchdown, but Akash responded with a gigantic Gordon touchdown of his own to sneak by at the last second. Will still had a great week and is poised to make some noise, but he’s playing from behind with a 4-5 record and needs to notch a win this week against Ruda.<br/><br/>5: Anish {5-4} (-2)<br/>Like Will, Anish had a solid showing but lost because of a tough matchup. Let’s not expect Coleman to repeat that performance, though. To be fair, this was Gurley’s worst week in the Ram’s first loss of the season and he should rebound. The big concern here is Gronk, especially in a year where the tight end position is so thin. Can he get healthy again?<br/><br/>4: Arjun {5-4} (+2)<br/>Woah. 138.5 points was the season high. Fun fact, two of the top three scoring weeks have been Arjun. Now the Bears defense isn’t going to score 29 every week, but the encouraging thing is that Kamara and White are more than capable of repeating their excellent performances week in and week out. Arjun’s trade for White is turning out to be one of the best of the season.<br/><br/>3: Brian {6-3} (-1)<br/>Well. That was bad. We can excuse one bad performance after several great weeks, but the concerns are just enough that Brian drops out of the top two for the first time in a while. Part of that was possibly the most ill-advised trade in recent memory; Brian essentially just gave up Odell Beckham for free over the course of the season and multiple trades. Maybe Mixon coming back will help.<br/><br/>2: Akash {6-3} (+2)<br/>What a turnaround this season for Akash. He’s averaged an impressive 118 points over the past three weeks, with his latest victory being one of the most intense of the season. Josh Gordon saved the day and provides this team with some stability at the WR position behind Beckham, who returns this coming week. And now Sony Michel returns from injury. This team is hot and has to be considered the greatest threat to Ruda right now.<br/><br/>1: Ruda {7-2} (-)<br/>53.9 is one of the larger margins of victory ever recorded. Now that Ruda is 7-2 we have to start wondering if his final record could be historic. We haven’t seen a 10-win season since having three in the first two seasons. Keep in mind that the current best record ever is 10-2-1, set by Ashwin in 2014. Could Ruda win out and achieve the fabled 11-win season? Stay tuned.</p>
