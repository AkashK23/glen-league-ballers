<p>I am happy to announce that Arjun and I have regained control over the GLB website and power rankings. Rest assured that your precious rankings are in our hands now, which we know none of you have any problems with.<br/><br/>We're now entering Week 5 and we have a much clearer picture of how the various teams are going to fare this season. There are a... wide range of observations. <br/><br/>I'm gonna take it old school with these rankings, and by that I mean "put less effort into them". Remember when rankings were just a blurb for each team and maybe two or three memes? Those were the days. Actually, even before that they were just list-based rankings published by Anish starting in 2016. In 2017 I offered to add blurbs, and in 2018 the memes started rolling in. Arjun took over rankings in 2020 (I think) and I doomed myself for all of eternity by starting to put WAY too much effort into these. Luckily the good members of GLB have been kind enough to save me with repeated guest rankings this past year or so. I'll need them as I finish school lol. <br/><br/>In any case, here come some old school rankings. Party like it's 2019.<br/><br/><br/> <strong>10. Brian (-) {0-4}<br/></strong> <em><u>On pace for:</u> 978.3 PF and 1412.8 PA <br/><u>Fun stat:</u> Brian's PF pace would be the third lowest score total of all time, and the lowest since 2015. If you're wondering who it was who just barely had a lower score in 2015... look deep within your heart and you shall know.</em><br/><br/>This is probably the least surprising ranking of all time. And maybe the worst team of all time, and I seriously mean that. Scoring like the lowest of the low of the 2014-2015 teams in this day and age is... appalling. We even added a quarter PPR for some players lmao. And now after setting that incredible pace, Brian has lost a starting RB for the season. I don't even know what else to say, I'm just gonna leave this chart from a few weeks ago here, dare someone to find me where I was wrong.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>8. Sid (-) {1-3}<br/></strong><em><u>On pace for:</u> 1312.5 PF and 1389.0 PA<br/><u>Fun stat:</u> Despite his W-L record so far, this would actually be Sid's second time ever hitting the 1300 PF mark if he stays on this pace. And he is still scoring pretty well (although it has been a high scoring year so far). But we might need to start facing the reality that this team isn't as good as we thought it was. Jefferson is really up and down, and Sid's RB situation is desperate at best. There are just no players that WOW you here... unless you count Brian Robinson's incredible imminent return. Maybe that's the spark Sid needs.<br/><br/><strong>7. Arjun (-1) {1-3}<br/></strong><em><u>On pace for:</u> 1265.0 PF and 1389.0 PA<br/><u>Fun stat:</u> As of the website update in July, Arjun ranks 2nd in W/L record, which is ranked by win percentage. But because Arjun has played far fewer games than the rest of us, his percentage is sort of precarious and can swing wildly with each game. Right now me, Akash, and Will are ALL tied for third place right below him, and if he loses next week then any of us three who win will actually leapfrog him in the wins table. Arjun could drop from 2nd to 5th all-time wins in one week.</em><br/><br/>The real tragedy here is Arjun, who managed to lead the league in scoring for weeks without obtaining wins. Now, after an abysmal game including a tank effort, he has dropped all the way to 8th in PF. There's still hope though because Lamar Jackson is balling out, and a lot of last week can be excused by rampant injuries. Now with Melvin Gordon as a firm lead back, Arjun is as poised as anyone to turn around his season.<br/><br/><strong>6. Akash (+1) {2-2}<br/></strong><em><u>On pace for: </u>1328.8 PF and 1300.7 PA<br/><u>Fun stat:</u> Akash is the only team to have increased his scoring every week of this season. Someone make an extrapolation for Week 13.</em><br/><br/>If Arjun is a tragedy, then Akash is an inspirational comeback story. He has gone from Brian-level bottom dweller to a scary team basically overnight. This past week, he scored a league season high 138 points... WITHOUT his quarterback (basically). All of a sudden no one wants to play this man, his RBs are legitimately terrifying. We're gonna need to see more from Russell Wilson and the WRs before we're ready to push him into the top 5 though. Although if you're ranking us based on ability to make offensive jokes, Akash has held the #1 spot for years.<br/><br/></p>

Unfortunately for me, there are an odd number of weeks in the regular season, which means I'll probably end 6-7. Aaaand it's an even year, so that checks out with my fun stat. The world makes so much sense sometimes.<br/><br/><strong>4. Will (-3) {2-2}<br/></strong><em><u>On pace for:</u> 1391.9 PF and 1487.4 PA<br/><u>Fun stat:</u> Will is currently on pace for the highest PA of all time, soundly eclipsing Anish's 1442 from last year.
