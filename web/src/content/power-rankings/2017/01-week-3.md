<p>10: Anish<br/>Is it time for panic mode yet? Maybe, maybe not. On one hand, Brown and Zeke had ridiculous down weeks that likely won't happen again. On the other, the rest of this team leaves a lot to be desired. Cousins and Murray have been disappointments, there isn't a viable TE, and the bench is just sad. Anish better make use of his waiver position, or he'll be setting more low-scoring records soon.<br/><br/>9: Girish<br/>The Fantastic Gore wasn't really that bad this week, especially when you consider that Olsen had to leave very early due to injury. Still, at the end of the day this is an 0-2 team off to a rocky start. There's promise in the bench, but this team is going nowhere unless it can find a suitable replacement for Olsen.<br/><br/>8: Sid<br/>No one really knows what's happening with Sid's team. Why Brees and Bell continue to do basically nothing is beyond me. Tomlin seems to be actively avoiding using Bell, after doing the opposite last year. That can be expected to change soon, so don't count Sid out yet. If Brees and Bell can start putting up 40 a week (which they are more than capable of), this team may be able to put up some wins.<br/><br/>7: Will<br/>Some regression to the mean was unavoidable after Bradford and the Jag D tore it up Week 1. Problem is, the Mixon Administration dropped way past the mean, into the gutter. If the Bengals offense could actually do something, Green would probably be able to push this team back into contention. Add in a good bench and you have a solid squad, but right now this is a hard team to judge.<br/><br/>6: Arjun<br/>This is no doubt a very low ranking for one of the three remaining undefeated teams. It's not so much a shot at Arjun's team as it is a testament to the strength of some of the 1-1 teams out there. Ice in my Veins has a good set of starters and managed to put up 84 and a win despite losing its top receiver. However, this team really needs Nelson to return soon, and CMC to start picking it up. Those question marks keep Arjun out of the top 5. <br/><br/>5: Ashwin<br/>Talk about a turnaround. Ashwin's RBs helped carry the team to the highest score of the week. Ty Montgomery looks like the real deal, and if CJ can keep it up too, this team is in for some wins. We can't expect the same defensive performance every week, and his bench doesn't look amazing, so it's hard to say how good this team is right now. Putting them in the middle makes the most sense.<br/><br/>4: Brian<br/>Could this be the long-fabled Year of Brian, that many thought would never come? Hard to say. What we do know is that Brady and Gronk returned to lethal form, decimating the Saints and Brian's opponent alike. We also saw solid performances from some other players, like Lynch and Dez. The Patriots won't explode this much every week, but it looks like they'll carry Brian to more wins in the coming months. The only big question here is the flex position.<br/><br/>3: Adit<br/>What do you do when your RBs don't deliver? Explode on the WR front, of course. In what looked like a disappointing week at first, the Brittish Empire unleashed a torrent of touchdowns in the evening and night from Crabtree and Adams. We've seen a lot of fluctuation here between RBs and WRs, which is cause for concern, but it's reasonable to expect Cam Newton to improve in the coming weeks and make up for any regression. The East runs through the Empire, for now.<br/><br/>2: Ruda<br/>The loss was disappointing, but there's not usually much you can do when your opponent goes off for 117 points. Ruda gets a pass because Odell was clearly rusty and the Giants offense looked miserable... two things that can be expected to change very quickly. The Chiefs continue to carry this team well, and it should return to dominance soon.<br/><br/>1: Akash<br/>This RB corps just isn't fair. Who allowed this? Absolute 0's three RBs combined for nearly 50 points... WITHOUT #1 OVERALL PICK DAVID JOHNSON. Let that sink in. This team put up 113 on a bad day for Rodgers. It's scary to imagine what it can do on a good day. It's even scarier to imagine what it will do once DJ returns, possibly in the playoffs. Graham's injury is a concern, but no one wants to play Akash right now. He's got the best team in the league. <br/><br/></p>
