<p>10: Will<br/>Will has turned an exciting start into three weeks where he's averaged 68.5 points, a shocking trend for a franchise that has made the playoffs each of the past two years. The problem? Stafford, Ajayi, and Mixon are just not cutting it as big-time starters. Chris Carson might've been the savior at RB, but now he's out for a long time. Green and Jeffery may be stepping it up at WR, but it's going to be tough to stop an angry Ruda coming off of a big upset. 1-4 would likely be the beginning of the end for this season.<br/><br/>9: Anish<br/>One high-flying performance in week 3 has all the makings of a fluke, looking back now. While #freezeke isn't quite as bad as its record-setting week 2 would indicate, this still isn't a good team. It is over-dependent on its two biggest stars, Elliott and Brown. Brown may be the #4 WR, but he's basically alternating between amazing and terrible weeks, and that inconsistency is hurting this team bad. Still, perhaps picking up Latavius Murray (this team has two Murrays now) and Eli Manning were steps in the right direction. Anish has a shot this week.<br/><br/>8: Sid<br/>0-4, and staring disaster in the face after the loss of Dalvin Cook. Not what one would typically describe as "eighth place". Let's look at some numbers. Sid has averaged 99 points over the last two weeks. Good. No one has ever made the playoffs in our league after an 0-4 start (best turnaround was Adit with a 2-4 start in 2014). Bad. Sid plays a team with 5 major players out this week. Good. Sid is in 5th place in a tough division. Bad. It's not the worst situation, but it's pretty crappy and will be hard to get out of. Let's see what he can do. Bonus: Sid has still not named his team Cole Miners. Very bad.<br/><br/>7: Girish<br/>The Fantastic Gore only topped 100 this week thanks to a random defensive touchdown at the very end of MNF (which, by the way, actually caused the Chiefs to cover the Vegas spread and won/lost money for people). Even without that, though, week 4 was a step in the right direction after failing to top 81.3 in any week before. Girish is now 2-2 and entering a favorable matchup that could push his winning streak to 3. We still have to see whether the trade with Arjun actually benefitted him, but if McCaffrey can flip the switch we've all been waiting for, Girish cannot be ignored in the West.<br/><br/>6: Ashwin<br/>Pani Puris has now won the title of "Hardest Team to Read" as we hit the one month mark of the season. In two weeks of this season, they have averaged 113.6 points, while in the other two, they have averaged 77.7. That is ridiculous fluctuation, and the two bad weeks sandwich the good ones. We're likely going to see this team fall to 2-3 in a rough week where most stars are on bye or hurt, so it will continue to be hard to judge this team for the near future. It's like actual Indian street food, I guess. It'll either be amazingly delicious, or will kill you with cholera.<br/><br/>5: Brian <br/>Ooh, boy. Just waiting for all the hate mail from Dezpacito fans this week. 3-1 and with a chance to take over the division this week, how could Brian be at 5? A good chunk of it is simply other teams being impressive, but not all of it. This week we saw what happens when the Patriots have a "normal" day. Yes, they were beaten, but this was a fairly standard day for a good NFL offense, and Brian's 3 Pats players combined for 40.5 points off of it. And that's solid. But it's not the kind of absolute explosion that was rocketing this team to 100+ on its own in previous weeks, and the supporting cast including Chris Thompson and Doug Martin seems iffy. This is a good team though that can easily shoot up the standings if it gets the win this week.<br/><br/>4: Akash<br/>Absolute 0 shined once again this week, tying its season high of 112.7 and leading the league in scoring. Stellar performances from the RBs have become a theme here (Gurley 27.5 pts, Fournette 20.5 pts), but it's hard to ignore that perhaps the team is becoming a little too dependent on them. Akash's RB2, WR1, WR2, and TE combined for just 25.6 points in week 4. What happens if Gurley or Fournette don't explode one week? Someone else has to step up. Still, this is a freakishly talented team that will want to win a key divisional matchup this week. They're exciting to watch and are a serious playoff contender.<br/><br/>3: Adit<br/>The Empire struck back in a big way last week, as Adit toppled division rival Ruda for the first time in three years. It looks more and more that his dismal week 3 performance was of the fluke nature, but that doesn't mean this team isn't without concerns. Different players have carried this team every week. Literally, every winning week. It was the RBs in week 1, the WRs in week 2, and QB+D/ST in week 4. That kind of fluctuation is unsustainable and the Empire needs to find their core set of players. Perhaps the deep running back corps will do the trick. Either way, 3-1 is a fantastic start putting Adit in the thick of the playoff race. Let's see if he can overcome his decimated WR situation to beat Akash.<br/><br/>2: Ruda<br/>This team deserves to be better than 2-2 and everyone knows it. Yet here they are. Why? Last week, it was a tough competition and a single poor lineup decision with the defense. In week 2 it was again tough competition. Hotel Beckham seems to just be getting unlucky. This is a team averaging 109 points a game, which would be 1416 points in a full season - an all-time record. They are bound to bounce back in a big way this week and start clawing their way back to the division lead. As long as the Chiefs stay good, this team is hard to beat.<br/><br/>1: Arjun<br/>Knock knock. Who's there? Foreign. Foreign who? FOUR AND OH. Undefeated, consistent, and the number one seed in the league. Sounds like a lot of ice running through those veins. Are there issues? Of course. This is now the only team to have not broken 100 yet this season. The consistency is remarkable, but scoring in the low 90's every week can only get you so far. With the second lowest PA, one might be tempted to explain away this winning streak with ease of schedule. At the end of the day, though, what counts are the dubs, and Arjun is raining them down on the league. This is a huge, huge week to determine just how good this team is. If he can rise to the challenge and beat Brian without some of his stars, he's truly #1. If not, well, 4-1 ain't so bad either. There's breathing room.</p>
