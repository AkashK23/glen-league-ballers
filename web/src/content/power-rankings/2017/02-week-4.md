<p>10: Sid<br/>Sid isn't really the worst in the league, but everything just seems to go wrong always. Finally, Brees and Bell put together big games like they always should have, and it's still not enough. Perhaps there's potential in the recent receiver explosions, but the fact remains that Sid is 0-3 and locked down at the bottom of the incredibly difficult East, and there isn't any obvious saving grace.<br/><br/>9: Girish<br/>Girish finally got into the win column, but it was really only by being less worse than the opposition, who had a historically bad week. The Fantastic Gore is currently averaging a very un-fantastic 71.1 points per week. TE and WR2 remain major issues, and a QB controversy is never good for a struggling team. This isn't looking good.<br/><br/>8: Will<br/>A great start has given way to disaster for the Mixon Administration. Ajayi and Crowell aren't really panning out as starting RBs so far, and Green/Jeffery is an inconsistent WR duo despite its talent. It's great that Mixon is starting to get involved in the Cincy offense, though. That and a winnable matchup against Girish next week give this team some hope.<br/><br/>7: Anish<br/>Well, this team is capable of winning after all. Speculation after one good week is never a wise idea, but fans certainly should be happy to see it's at least possible for this team to eclipse the century mark. Murray having a great game on the bench means even more promise. Still, Hooper at TE is only harming this team, and there are still a lot of negatives here.<br/><br/>6: Adit<br/>Adit's team has officially earned the title of Hardest to Predict. Pretty much nothing makes sense here, as the team violently fluctuates back and forth week to week. Scoring 55 less points than they did in week 2 is cause for major concern, but keep in mind the strong performance by the bench. It's going to take a little longer to actually get an idea of how good the Brittish Empire is, but the good news for them is a 2-1 start that leaves some room for error. The biggest question mark here is Cam. Let's see what happens. <br/><br/>5: Ashwin<br/>This tasty Indian snack has been firing on all cylinders, and is one of just two teams to breach 100 for the past two weeks. An area of concern is inconsistency by individual players and position groups. Like Adit's team, the RBs that seemed to be the core of this team completely shut down this week and had to be saved by exceptional WR performances. This is another team that is hard to judge, but one that could make a spicy statement with a win over Brian, if they bring out all the chutneys.<br/><br/>4: Akash<br/>For the first week, Akash failed to dominate on the scoreboard. This is still an amazing team, but a 1-2 start is bad, tough schedule or not. It's even more concerning that a huge amount of the points came from Gurley's ridiculous TNF performance. Still, Akash's bench was and we can all expect a bounce back next week. Will Diggs shine as a starter? Stay tuned.<br/><br/>3: Brian<br/>For the first time in the history of mankind, Brian is a top 3 team in the league. The loss on Monday was soul-crushing for fans and owner alike, but it shouldn't make us forget the great two weeks Brian has had. I said in week 2 that we can't always expect the Patriots to explode offensively like they did then, but they came right around and did it again. Perhaps what helps Brian the most is how bad their defense is, forcing them to pass all game. No doubt Hogan and Blount will come in as suddenly viable starters, further bolstering this rock-solid team.<br/><br/>2: Arjun<br/>Undefeated three weeks in and sitting pretty at the top of the West, many might put Ice right up at the top of these rankings. There's certainly a case to be made for them. But consider this: only two teams have yet to pass the 100 mark yet this season... Girish, and Arjun. Consistently scoring in the 90's is a great way to make the playoffs, but it's not going to work against teams capable of exploding to 120+, like Ruda's. Don't let all that negative talk distract you, though. This team is scary, and if Matt Ryan gets back into his groove, watch out. <br/><br/>1: Ruda<br/>When your opponent scores a lot, score even more. Hotel Beckham rose up to stomp a scary near-upset, and this time saw major contributions from its slow-starting big names, like Beckham and Cooks. Maybe we can expect to see the KC offense regress a bit, or at least for Hunt to get somewhat more contained, but the continuing rise of their supporting players on Ruda's teams erases any fears. He's at the top of the East, and is the clear favorite in a big game to keep control of the division next week. <br/><br/></p>
