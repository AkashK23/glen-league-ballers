<p>10: Will (n)<br/>It finally came together for Will this week, and he stopped a 7-game skid. He avoids contesting for the all-time record 9 consecutive losses, but tied for second. Interestingly, Will also leapfrogged Arjun and is now not last in PF for the first time in a while. Alshon Jeffery is really stepping up, and him plus Green could be a deadly combo. These are only minor victories in a lost season, but after almost two months of dismay, any positive developments are welcome.<br/><br/>9: Anish (-1)<br/>The chance of a winning season went out the window this week. Anish didn't do badly, but just caught Will on a surprising good week. It seems that Kirk Cousins is dragging this team down from its solid scores earlier, though other factors are in play too. If Anish gets something more out of his QB, he should be able to eke out the much-needed third win. Interesting fact: Anish is currently on pace for the most PA in league history, 1341.6.<br/><br/>8: Ashwin (+1)<br/>A solid performance in week 9 both ended Sid's season and kept Ashwin's alive. At 4-5 and a very low PF, Ashwin will have to run the table with very high scoring to have a playoff shot. His WRs continue to impress, and Dak Prescott is shining as the signal caller. If Ashwin wants any chance though, he needs his RBs to step up. CJ Anderson is not cutting it, and Montgomery has fallen down the depth chart. Orleans Darkwa may be able to help.<br/><br/>7: Arjun (n)<br/>Things are surprisingly grim for Arjun, despite the manageable 5-4 record. Now last in PF, there's little hope of winning any tiebreakers to sneak into the playoffs at 8-5. Arjun has to win out and finish 9-4 with a team on pace for just 1021 points this season (for reference, the third lowest of all time is 1004). It might sound impossible, but a weak remaining schedule means we can't dismiss Arjun. The biggest hurdle is toppling a surging Girish this week; if he can do that, he has every chance to make the postseason. This is a huge matchup that may effectively decide Arjun's season. He made a solid trade to get himself a good kicker, a starting caliber TE, and Dez Bryant to replace Nelson. That's the kind of move you want to see now that every game is essentially a playoff game for this team. <br/><br/>6: Sid (n)<br/>Rip. The magic came to an end this week, and Sid's 2017 season is over. It was a roller coaster, and Sid deserves respect for turning around a putrid start. The focus now is on ending as well as possible; with Brian and Girish having good seasons, Sid is trying to avoid becoming a bottom-tier franchise in league history. His team is good, there's no denying that. Let's see if he can end 6-7 or so.<br/><br/>5: Brian (-3)<br/>Three straight losses have sent Team Dezpacito into a downward spiral that it needs to pull out of soon. This team was 5-1 not too long ago, and though some things out of their control happened (Ajayi trade, Watson injury, etc.), there is no reason they should be dropping game after game like this. Brian needs to get his locker room under control and make a late season surge to contest for the division - it's still very possible. Perhaps that surge can come from a risky but understandable trade with Arjun. I respect that Brian recognized a struggling WR group, and made a change to it. Brian's PF is solid enough that winning out may not be necessary; can he end at least 3-1? The Patriots are back, and are known for strong finishes. Brian still has fight left in him.<br/><br/>4: Adit (-3)<br/>It's a little shocking to see a team that was unquestionably the best in the league since about week 3 fall to fourth in standings and third in the East. Such is the way of fantasy football. There was never a great chance to beat Akash this week, with all of Adit's byes + a surprise injury to Ertz. His Eagles (and Crabtree) may be out this coming week, but the RB corps is the machine that powers this team, and it should be back with a vengeance. If the Empire can beat Ashwin this week and move to 7-3, it enters the final stretch with a truly fearsome arsenal of talent and no major byes remaining. The East is a total crapshoot, however; being a good team won't guarantee making the playoffs. In fact, the standings are so close right now (even compared to the West; Adit is within 1 PF of both Brian and Girish) that it's hard to tell whether he's in contention for first seed, or fourth. He'll just have to win as much as possible and hope things work out in his favor. <br/><br/>3: Girish (n)<br/>Boom. Girish throttled Brian in perhaps the biggest game in both franchises' histories. Wilson is balling(ish). AP is balling. Hopkins is balling, with Tom Savage. CMC is balling. McCoy will be back to balling soon. Olsen and Clay will return in the near future. This team is stacked, and Girish can use it to run away with the division. The main concern is WRs. Evans was already starting to disappoint, and now he's suspended. Outside of Watkins, there's really no one else on this team. Still, Girish has averaged 105 points per game over the past six (!) weeks, tops in the league. He's not just trying to win the division. He's aiming for first seed.<br/><br/>2: Akash (+3)<br/>Every week I say Akash's defenses have to slow down soon, and every week they just don't. At this point, screw it. He sold his soul or something. Let's just assume they will always be good. With them, Akash is scoring in the 90's relatively consistently, and a four game win streak has placed him at second in the East. I can't even describe to you how huge his next two games are, against a Chiefs-less Ruda and Girish. Like Adit, Akash's position is extremely undefined, and there's no way of telling whether he'll end up in first or even fifth. He just has to fous on beating Ruda and Girish, or he's in trouble. He'll finally have his feared combo of Gurley and Fournette back, so it's very possible. <br/><br/>1: Ruda (+3)<br/>Five straight weeks of scoring within 1.7 of 95 is remarkable, and Ruda has gone 4-2 in that time. A big win over Arjun boosted Ruda to first seed and right in the thick of the war for the East. On pace for a very solid 1318 PF, Ruda can very reasonably hope for at least 9 wins. Unfortunately, he must face a surging Akash without his signature Chiefs this week. Hotel Beckham will have to rely on some mediocre players like Matt Ryan and CJ Fideorifiekwizkjowz. Whether or not he pulls this off, all eyes are set on the week 13 duel with Adit. The implications of that game may be massive, even if the East sends 3 to the postseason. Nothing is guaranteed for anyone, and there are six teams beating up on each other to make the playoffs. Buckle your seat belts, and keep your hands and feet in the vehicle at all times. There's blood, tears, and glory ahead.</p>
