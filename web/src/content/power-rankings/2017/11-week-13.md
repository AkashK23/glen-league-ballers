<p>10: Ashwin<br/>For the first time in league history, no team will finish with 3 wins. Ashwin is in last place with 4, and will battle with Will this week to determine who finishes last and gets the golden toilet. Ashwin already holds the record for lowest PF in a season, but it looks like he'll avoid being on that list a second time. <br/><br/>9: Arjun<br/>A unique season came to an end last week, as Arjun fell to Anish in his sixth loss. Remaining in the playoff race until now was impressive for a first season, even considering the strange ease of his schedule. Ultimately, the unprecedented competition for playoff spots knocked Arjun out, and he'll compete with Sid to finish as the sixth seed.<br/><br/>8: Will<br/>It came too late, but man has this team caught fire. Four straight 100+ weeks (the only such streak this season) have propelled Will from 1-7 to 4-8. Taking down Girish and Arjun had huge impacts on the league, too. It's hard to pinpoint what exactly caused the change; several players improved at once. Notably, Alshon Jeffery's resurgence coincided with these four games. Will will try to avoid last seed this week by beating Ashwin. <br/><br/>7: Anish<br/>An impactful win netted Anish his fifth win, continuing this roller coaster of a season. He's still on pace for the third most PA of all time, so if he wins next week he can tout a 6-7 finish despite a historically difficult schedule. More importantly, a win could easily knock Girish out of the playoffs. You never know with this team, though.<br/><br/>6: Sid<br/>Sid is playing spoiler here in the home stretch, recording extremely damaging victories against Adit and Ruda in consecutive weeks. Sid's streakiness is intriguing; 4 straight losses were followed by 3 straight wins, then 3 straight losses, and then 2 straight wins. He can't knock Akash out of contention this week, though. <br/><br/>5: Brian<br/>Brian eked out a win to somehow keep his hopes alive and well. He'll most likely have to beat Arjun next week to make the playoffs. The Patriots offense has regained some of its luster, and Brian basically has an army of its players. Meanwhile, Jamison Crowder showed a lot of promise in the best game of his career. This team is basically being held together by hot glue and staples; that's easy enough to see just scrolling through its players (who is Josh Reynolds?). The New England core is strong enough, though, that a few complementary performances can easily cause a win. Arjun hasn't scored 100 all season and we don't expect him to start now. Brian will probably need to just avoid scoring low, and hit the 90's or so. That will entirely depend on the Patriots' divisional matchup with Buffalo.<br/><br/>4: Adit<br/>Two devastating losses in a row have thrown Adit from eyeing first seed to clawing back into the playoffs. It's actually still very possible that he gets any seed from 1 to 5, because of how close the competition is. Still, he's officially 4 right now and needs to beat an always-dangerous Ruda. Once again, it showed that the RBs carry this team. Howard, Gordon, and McKinnon stumbled after a strong Week 11 showing. McKinnon no longer seems like a viable starter, but Adit will probably have to start him thanks to Crabtree's suspension. He'll also probably have to start Sterling Shepard, whose new QB is Geno Smith. Things seem a little bit dire. Keep in mind, though, that Adit would have won with Wentz. While having two strong QBs is a problem sometimes, all Adit needs is a good day from whoever he chooses, and good days from Gordon and Howard, and this team can get rolling. <br/><br/>3: Girish<br/>Like almost every other contender, Girish was upset in Week 12 by a supposedly lesser team. His team actually did really well though, putting up 103. Wilson continues to ball out as QB, and Hopkins showed that he's still QB-proof. We can't expect Girish's defense to score 18 every week, but there were some promising bench performances that should merit starting consideration to make up for lost points. Olsen wasn't able to finish the game due to soreness, so whether he plays fully or not next week could be a major factor for Girish. It may be a difficult decision between three good TEs. Girish now faces an unpredictable Anish, and the situation is complicated, but a win will get him into the playoffs. This year, a 7-6 team could make it, but no one wants to deal with the ensuing mess of tiebreakers, especially Girish who has the lowest PF of the remaining contenders.<br/><br/>2: Ruda<br/>Ruda suffered his worst loss in a while, failing to hit 60 and dropping to 7-5. It couldn't be more obvious what the problem is: Kansas City. The team that carried Ruda earlier this season is now poisoning him; his three players combined for just 11.9 points. He'll now face the difficult situation of which ones of them to bench and keep. They are so talented that benching any of them is hard, but this offense has become so anemic that few players produce at all any week. Ruda's QB play also leaves a lot to be desired. Still, the danger of this team is always its capability to explode. Boom-or-bust players are riddled throughout, and perhaps it's time for a boom. It's actually very possible that Ruda makes the playoffs even with a loss this week, thanks to his high PF. Nothing is guaranteed this season, though, so taking down Adit is the goal. It's a sad matchup between two former juggernauts who could lose everything this week. <br/><br/>1: Akash<br/>Only one team has clinched a playoff spot. Only one team has eight wins. Only one team is first seed. Akash somehow clutched out a win last week, securing his spot in the Second Season. It's been a crazy season, and initial devastating injuries have been overcome thanks to both luck and skill. This week, Akash takes on Sid in a matchup that will only affect his seeding. In such a year, it doesn't appear that seeding will really matter, as every team is basically equal strength at this point. Akash will just go forth with a strong team that showed it's capable of winning even without explosions from its RBs. <br/><br/>There are five teams vying for four spots. Really, there are four teams vying for three spots. Someone will fall in crushing fashion. This is the closest competition and highest intensity our league has ever had this late; there are so many possibilities that we can't even begin to explore them all. It's just time to prepare, wait, and pray. May the better teams win.<br/><br/></p>
