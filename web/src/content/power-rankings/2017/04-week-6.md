<p>10: Anish<br/>Another week, another loss. 1-4 is approaching "lost season" status, and there aren't any major signs of hope for this team. Anish is on pace for 948 points this season, which would be the lowest of all time - second lowest is 971. Compare this to his 1374 points last season, and you get the epic scale of this collapse. The only two players powering Anish are Zeke and Brown. Demarco Murray appears to be slowing down with age, Tarik Cohen has lost his grip on split carries, and once-promising Latavius Murray was thoroughly outperformed by McKinnon on Monday. At this point, Anish is really just trying to avoid the wrong end of the record books. A Zeke-less matchup with Adit next week won't help. <br/><br/>9: Will<br/>There were finally signs of improvement for Will this week, and it came mostly at the deadly hands of AJ Green, who is beginning to look like a smart first round pick. Joe Mixon is also starting to find his groove, which helps this team make up for the disappointing production of Jay Ajayi. Will almost hit 90 last week and would have beaten Ruda if not for a poor D/ST decision (to be fair, the Jaguars make no sense at all this season). Unfortunately, the Bengals have a bye this week, and if Will drops to 1-5, he's probably past the point of no return. <br/><br/>8: Sid<br/>Scoring 120 points is a great way to rebound from a dismal 0-4 start. In fact, Sid has averaged 106 points over the past three weeks, which is tops in the league. Now, expectations have to be tempered some, because two of Sid's players this week were rentals from Brian, and it's not clear how the Packers will use Aaron Jones once Montgomery returns. Alex Smith also had a stellar outing, so it's not like there's any huge advantage to Brees returning to this team. But if you're trying to save a wretched start, this is the kind of performance you want to see. Perhaps there's hope for Sid yet.<br/><br/>7: Ashwin<br/>This was always going to be Ashwin's roughest week due to the number of byes. Panty... ahem, Pani Puris continued their theme of scoring either &gt;100 or &lt;80, which has left them last in the division in PF. The two great weeks have left this team at a manageable 2-3 start, with plenty of firepower to make a playoff push in stars like Julio Jones and Michael Thomas. A major issue could arise with Ty Montgomery, however. Aaron Jones is making a case for more playing time once Ty returns, and Ashwin really has no other options at RB, as this is a very WR-heavy team.<br/><br/>6: Girish<br/>No one really understands how this team is 3-2, but it is. It's been a weird season of very low PF's and very low PA's for Girish. However, he's averaged 98 over the last two weeks (Deandre Hopkins has been a major boost). We're still waiting on CMC to break out, but if he does, this team will look like a serious playoff contender. Girish has some major tests the next few weeks. If he wants to be in serious contention, he has to be able to beat teams like Ashwin without issue, and then hang with Ruda. These next few weeks will help us see where this team really is. <br/><br/>5: Arjun<br/>Well. That just happened. Our former numero uno team completely faceplanted into the lowest single-game score of all time: 39.5. Talk of consistency in the 90's is pretty much out the window now, even considering that the Falcons were on bye. There's no reason to consider this performance anything but a fluke, but it has sparked enough concern to drop Arjun several places. He's now a shocking 8th in PF. The worst news is that this week was only the start of a difficult stretch; he has to take on the juggernauts of the East (Akash, Adit, and Ruda) in the next 4 weeks. A rebound is certain, but Arjun needs to regroup and recover to try and take back the division lead.<br/><br/>4: Ruda<br/>A close primetime win was overshadowed by the season-ending injury to franchise face Odell Beckham. The fanbase will soon get over its shock to realize that this team is still seriously good, but just how good can it be without OBJ? Last week, if Odell was replaced by Ruda's next highest scorer, Wayne Gallman, Ruda would have scored 85 instead of 93 and lost. The week before, playing a bench player like Miller or Abdullah over OBJ would have actually improved his score and possibly led to a win over Adit. The week before, Ruda would have only lost 6 points and still scored 116. It's safe to say that, while the loss is huge, Ruda has enough depth to survive it most weeks. It will probably cost him at least a game or two, and has led to Ruda's slight tumble in the rankings, but this team still has its eyes set on a third postseason run. First, it will have to take on Brian in a difficult inter-divisional matchup.<br/><br/>3: Akash<br/>This is the best 2-3 team I've ever seen. Second in scoring and on pace for 1344 points, Akash has been throttled by an extremely unlucky schedule. Don't let him being fourth in PA fool you; that stat is seriously swayed by one week against Anish. Outside of that game, Akash's opponents have averaged an astounding 113 points per game. We simply can't expect that trend to continue, and Akash is putting up boatloads of points every week. That will translate to wins. Fournette and Benjamin are both improving rapidly, and were able to cover quite well for a down week by Gurley last week. There isn't much depth on the bench, but this is possibly the best group of starters in the league. Expect a series of wins soon.<br/><br/>2: Brian<br/>Brian's scores are trending downwards, but a shift back up is inevitable, and a historically easy schedule (on pace for only 924 PA) has allowed him to take control of the West. The scariest thing about this team is its depth. Martin, Brate, Garçon, and Fuller are starting-caliber players that Brian doesn't even need every week. His team is equipped with the tools to handle bye weeks and injuries very well, which will come in handy as the peak of byes approaches. The Patriots have had a few down weeks (biggest reason for his scoring drop-off), which they always bounce back from in a big way. Brian will need it, as his upcoming matchups against Ruda, Akash, and a suddenly hot Sid are no joke. Can he stay in contention for first seed?<br/><br/>1: Adit<br/>The Brittish Empire has begun colonizing the entire league. A scoring explosion has led to a 4-1 record and the number one overall seed. Adit has emerged victorious from two difficult matchups with Ruda and Akash, and is the hottest team in the league right now. The biggest key has been the return of Cam Newton to MVP form, as he has averaged 30 points over the past two weeks. The WR corps is healed up, the RB's look strong as ever, and the Empire has suddenly found itself some strong depth in guys like McKinnon, Wentz, and Shepard. The end of the season will be difficult thanks to byes and tough opponents, so Adit should try and get as many wins as possible in the next few weeks. The biggest key to that will be Newton keeping his form; his dismal start can't be forgotten. A favorable matchup with Anish could have Adit looking at 5-1 very soon. </p>
