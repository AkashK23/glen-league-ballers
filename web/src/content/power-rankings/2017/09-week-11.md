<p>10: Will (n)<br/>It does look as though Will's team has improved, hitting 100 in consecutive weeks. A big trade gets rid of some of that production, but brings in some fresh faces, including a heavily-utilized Adrian Peterson. Not much else to say here. Will should be able to get the crucial third win to avoid shame. He's also got the chance to make killer blows to Arjun and Girish's seasons, so he'll look to do that in the next two weeks.<br/><br/>9: Ashwin (-1)<br/>Ashwin's season came to a crushing end on Monday. A would-be upset was thwarted by Adit, bringing him to 6 losses and out of the playoffs. Like Will, Ashwin will have a chance to make a significant impact in his division, with matchups against Ruda and Akash coming up. He's safely away from the record books.<br/><br/>8: Anish (+1)<br/>A solid win proved Anish actually fielded a decent team this year. He's still on pace to have the most PA of all time; bad fortune has cost this team dearly. You want to talk about a chance to impact the playoffs though? Anish plays all three of the western contenders to close the season. The results of those matchups could rewrite history. He's already beaten Brian once and is coming off of a 100 point outing. No future opponent of his is too keen about this.<br/><br/>7: Sid (-1)<br/>Sid's team did a good job, but it wasn't enough against Anish, and he lost $5 to boot. Brees and Bell just aren't the duo they were supposed to be. Sid is actually fifth in PF right now, but little known fact: he's on pace for the second most PA of all time (he is also currently third on that list from last season). Someone upstairs hates him. Like Anish, he plays all eastern contenders coming up, and also like Anish, no one really wants to play him. <br/><br/>6: Arjun (+1)<br/>Arjun earned an absolutely crucial victory over Girish to keep his season alive and healthy. He may still be last in PF, but he's one of three 6-4 teams in the west, which is going into absolute dogfight mode. His scoring isn't great, but at least it's trending towards 90, and sometimes that's enough. It certainly could be with Arjun's very weak closing schedule. If he can win against Will and Anish, it may all come down to a week 13 matchup with Brian. Remember, Arjun can't afford to drop a single game. He has to end 9-4. <br/><br/>5: Girish (-2)<br/>Total collapse at the worst possible time. The Fantastic Gore took a tumble after averaging more than 115 over the past two weeks. Wilson, Hopkins, and CMC (promisingly) showed up, but no one else did. Perhaps the Bills aren't a team to rely on offensively - that problem should be solved when Greg Olsen returns to replace  Clay in two weeks. Girish will now have his hands on AJ Green and Isaiah Crowell, at the cost of AP and Mike Evans. The trade should work in his favor as long as CMC continues to play well. He's now 6-4 and second in the division, but with 915 PF he's fallen behind the pack. If he can't put out some high-scoring wins, he'll have to run the table to make the playoffs. The biggest test in that is Akash this week. If Girish can't stop the #1 seed's win streak, his season will start to crumble. <br/><br/>4: Ruda (-3)<br/>Ruda had his own win streak going, but it came to a close as he fell to Akash. Putting up 85 without his Chiefs was actually solid, and Ruda came close to winning for a few moments there on Sunday night. Most of that was on the back of Mark Ingram, who has quickly turned into one of the deadliest weapons in fantasy now that Sean Payton has dislodged his head from his ass and learned to run the ball. The talented and handsome RB has put up double digits in four of the past five weeks. Now, Hotel Beckham gets their Chiefs back with a moderate schedule ahead. The QB position is still a major question mark, and it remains to be seen whether they will try to trade a Chief (particularly Hill) before next week's deadline. Still, the league scoring leader cannot be ignored.  <br/><br/>3: Brian (+2)<br/>Last week I said Brian needed to get his team under control immediately, and he did just that. 107 points led the week in scoring and snapped a three-game skid that threatened to derail his entire season. A huge amount of that came from Robert Woods's explosion, and he was only temporary. However, it was good to see Tom Brady return to form. Brian has to do a lot of reshuffling every week, but he's done it well enough to snatch the division lead back from Girish. Some big games remain. A playoff team has to be able to put down teams like Anish's, up first. Then, he faces Adit in a very important game. Finally, Arjun in week 13 could be a win and in. The good news for Brian is that due to his high PF, he doesn't have to necessarily win out. 8-5 may be enough, but his hopes are higher. <br/><br/>2: Adit (+2)<br/>SuperCam saved the day for Adit, in what otherwise should have been a disastrous loss. The infamous RB machine completely broke down (inexplicably, in the case of Gordon), forcing other players to step up. Against all odds, they did, with Shepard racking up 142 yards and backup TE Hooper finding the endzone. What almost was a 1-3 stretch (after a 5-1 start) became 2-2, leaving Adit just behind Akash for the #1 spot. Cam may have the week off, but the Eagles are back, and Wentz plus the addition of Ertz should more than handle the load. Also promising is the emergence of the WRs here, when they may be most needed. Adams and Shepard were both fantastic, and with the return of Crabtree, this team could see a shift in focus from RB-heavy to more all-round. That's usually a good thing. Two big things will decide games for Adit: the QB controversy (Cam vs Wentz), and the FLEX controversy (McKinnon vs Shepard). He needs to choose wisely going forward.<br/><br/>1: Akash (+1)<br/>A lot could be said about Akash's streak of luck (following his streak of bad luck), but at the end of the day, he's on a five game win streak and at the top of the league. He's also second in scoring. Adit is uncomfortably close in second, but Akash essentially controls his own fate, because after a big game with Girish this week, he faces Ashwin and Sid - not easy, but manageable. 10-3 is definitely a possibility this year, and it may be necessary to leap into the playoffs as #1. Doing so would tie Akash for the longest win streak in history; he's already just one more win away from third. I fully expect Leonard Fournette to return to form in the near future, and Jimmy Graham is really heating up as the Seahawks find their rhythm. This team is dangerous, even without defensive explosions. Can it hold on to its lead? One of many questions as we enter the final three weeks. Stay tuned.</p>
