<p>10: Will (n)<br/>rip<br/><br/>9: Ashwin (n)<br/>Ashwin managed to win with 59.8 points in week 8. I don't really know how that's possible. His matchup with Arjun ended up becoming the third lowest scoring game in league. However unimpressive the win was, it kept Ashwin's season alive another week. Prospects remain bleak for a team that didn't have a single starter break 9 points, with remaining games against contenders like Ruda, Akash, and Adit. <br/><br/>8: Anish (n)<br/>The miracle of the past two weeks came to an end in week 8,  and so did Anish's season. His sixth loss eliminates him from playoff contention. Zeke's suspension troubles have return, to boot. Anish can take comfort in the fact that his PF and win totals should stay clear of the low record books. Also, his PA is the highest in the league. A lost season, but not an atrocious one. <br/><br/>7: Arjun (-2)<br/>Arjun's fans may continue to mail us death threats, and occasionally poison, but we're sticking with our guns here. Sure, 7 sounds shockingly low for a 5-3 team tied for the division lead. But consider that Arjun is 9th in PF, and it's worse than it sounds; the next lowest PF of the other 5+ win teams is a full 115 ahead of him. In the first four weeks, Arjun averaged a healthy 92 ppg; in the following four, he's averaged just 63(!). The main place confidence in this team can be found is its talent; Alex Smith is the #1 QB, Freeman + Hyde is a great duo, and Nelson + Fitz can probably hold the fort down despite their QB injuries. Also, Arjun seems to have the magical ability to cause his opponents to suck. All that has led to a 5-3 record and a shot at the playoffs. Problem is, the West is weak, and there's no guarantee it'll send a wild card out. Let's see what he can do.<br/><br/>6: Sid (+1)<br/>Well, the magical winning streak ended, but the chance at a miracle finish has not. Sid needs to run the table now, and while his last three games are difficult, his next two are very winnable. His goal right now is to be sitting at 5-5 in two weeks, then step it up big. Does he have what it takes? This week, past trades and a Steelers bye harm his chances, but I expect him to pull off a win against a struggling Pani Puris team. He'll then return to full strength, featuring deadly running backs like Bell, Coleman, and a suddenly hot Aaron Jones on a newly run-heavy Packers team, as well as a consistent Evan Engram. The critical variables lie in his WR corps. It's talented, but different receivers seem to explode each week. Sid needs to pick his starters right every week from here on out. A step up in performance from Drew Brees is very possible, too. This is going to be tough, but it's doable. <br/><br/>5: Akash (-1)<br/>Akash pulled off the upset of the season last week, and shifted his sights on to the division title. He also has a very winnable matchup with a Brittish Empire team decimated by byes this week. Akash could be sitting atop the division in a few days. So why is he only 5? Last week, Akash's defense accounted for 28% of his total score. The week before, 19%. Before that, 20%. It hasn't always been the Ravens D, either. This rate is just unsustainable, and it looks even worse when you factor in his kicker. Expect his defenses to falter. However, this team is still championship material, thanks to the ridiculous combination of Gurley and Fournette, who can carry a team on their own. They're both back, and Akash's team looks strong. Watch for a rise in the standings soon. <br/><br/>4: Ruda (-1)<br/>Ruda is the new Arjun, with even more stunning consistency. He has scored within 1.6 points of 95 for the last 5 weeks straight. Ruda is yet to have a bad game this season, though we should note that his recent inability to explode past 100 could be concerning against stronger teams. He has only gone 3-2 during that 5 week stretch, after all. It appears that he is sticking with his Chiefs investments; Hunt and Hill had bad games last week, but Kelce made up for it, and perhaps that rounding out factor is what is keeping Ruda satisfied with KC. He has every chance to win this division, so he'll want to try and win out leading into what could be a crucial week 13 matchup with Adit. <br/><br/>3: Girish (+3)<br/>Girish has put together several good weeks in a row, and showed that his team wasn't deterred after the narrow loss in week 7. 137.2 is the second highest score of the season, and it came mostly out of Wilson, McCoy, and Hopkins. This QB-RB-WR trio is absolutely on fire right now, and Girish will continue to ride them to more wins. They'll fall back to earth a little bit, so he'll want other players like CMC and Mike Evans to return to their solid play from earlier in the season. If they do, this team is downright scary. Girish is 5-3 and faces his mortal enemy next week in a huge battle for the West. Keep in mind that due to low PFs, it's very possible that only one playoff team comes out of this division. No one wants to finish second here. <br/><br/>2: Brian (-1)<br/>That was incredibly disappointing. A sure win turned into a horrific choke job as Brian succumbed to the trap game. It's not like his team did horribly - it almost hit 90 - but it's clear that the Patriots trio isn't always going to do quite enough to carry this team. The big error was benching Will Fuller; we can safely assume Brian won't make that mistake again. With Fuller in, Dez helping fill in for a (probably) suspended Zeke, and a probable comeback for Chris Thompson, there are plenty of things to be excited about in this team. Perhaps the defining factor will be how much the Ajayi trade affects Blount. If he can be a serviceable, touchdown-dependent flex, Brian is a championship contender. First, he'll have to take down Girish in the game of the season. This rivalry is reaching peak levels of intensity and hype. Get ready.<br/><br/>1: Adit (+1)<br/>Adit soared back from his down week, firing off 6 touchdowns and 115 points. He has now surpassed the 100 mark in an incredible 4 of his past 5 games. The three-headed RB monster of Gordon, Howard, and McKinnon is nigh unstoppable (except by the bye week); last week they combined for 52 points. Ertz continues to rumble along as the #1 TE. Wentz is firing on all cylinders. At 6-2, Adit claims the best record in the league, and can hope to enter the playoffs as first seed. Unfortunately, his entire RB corps, as well as stud kicker Gostkowski, are out on byes this week as he faces Akash. If he loses, he'll lose control of the division lead for the first time in a while. There may be some clawing back needed, but if there's any team that can do it, it's this one. We have an exciting home stretch coming up, folks. </p>
