Good name though. Oh wait, that's Brian's job. Brian get on this shit.<br/><br/><strong>10. Arjun</strong><br/>You are armed to the teeth with a monster trio of three elite wide receivers, some of the best in the league: Davante Adams, Stefon Diggs, and Keenan Allen. Next.<br/><br/><strong>9. Will</strong><br/>Picking De'von Achane as RB1 was a genius move because he will surely reproduce his totally sustainable 7.8 yards per carry from last year. Akash</strong><br/>While I actually respect the pick of Jayden Daniels at QB, I must say it feels like Akash messed up with most of his other picks. Going RB heavy in a year where RBs 1) are devalued and 2) suck was a bold move. That's how you end up with... George Pickens and Diontae Johnson as WR2 and WR3. Excellent.<br/><br/><strong>7. Brian</strong><br/>This team is the definition of mediocre in my eyes. The reach for Barkley in Round 1 was puzzling, but he's still a good player. The rest of Brian's team just looks... decent at literally every position. Look at it. No player is bad here, but no player is inspiring at all. Yawn.<br/><br/><strong>6. Sujay<br/></strong>Some solid picks here for sure. You can't go wrong with Josh Allen at QB, and there is no doubt watching film that Jahmyr Gibbs is an incredibly talented player. There's a lot less to be excited about as you get further down the roster here, though. Drafting two Colts receivers was a choice. Let is also be known that Sujay took Gabe Davis thinking he was still on Buffalo lol.<br/><br/><strong>5. Girish<br/></strong>Another year of Girish drafting a decent team that probably deserves 7-7 and a first round playoff exit but will instead deliver a championship for no discernable reason. There's no point in analyzing this team. Girish could draft a team of exclusively retired players and they'd all un-retire just to win him a ring.<br/><br/><strong>4. Anish<br/></strong>I'm impressed with this draft. Anish managed to pull off a pretty good RB corps without sacrificing his WR corps, which was a tall task this year. Mostert at FLEX is excellent value. The investment in a solid Chiefs offense will likely pay dividends. However, Anish fails to rise higher than 4 thanks to his choice of Caleb Williams as QB1. Looking forward to the hate mail from Ashwin for that.<br/><br/><strong>3. Sid<br/></strong>Applause. Tua at QB: great pick. JT at RB1: great pick. DK at WR1: great pick. The list goes on. My only knock here is Brock Bowers at TE, but there's a chance he's still great. The real thing to worry about is Sid cancelling himself the next time Amari Cooper fails him.<br/><br/><strong>2. Ruda<br/></strong>Another year of Ruda drafting a fantastic team that probably deserves 11-3 and a championship but will instead exit early in the playoffs for no discernable reason. There's no point in analyzing this team. Ruda could draft a team of exclusively MVP candidates and they'd all retire just to cost him a ring.<br/><br/><strong>1. Ashwin<br/></strong>Give it to him: Ashwin has honestly put together a very solid team this year. We all have heard enough about his first round pick of Christian McCaffrey, but what really stands out is the selections of Evans and LaPorta, who can really fortify the WR and TE positions in a year where it is very much needed. Throw in a kick-ass QB in Lamar Jackson (until he poops himself in Week 4) and you have the makings of a championship team here. Too bad the same can't be said for his Bears.<br/><br/></p>
