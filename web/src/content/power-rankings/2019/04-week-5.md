<p>Welcome to History Week here at GLB Rankings! This week I'll be including some fun historical facts about each team, along with the regular analysis. <br/><br/>We now have no undefeated teams, and no winless teams. What started as a very heavily split two groups of teams is now a little murkier in the middle. This means we have a pretty spicy second 1/3 of the season starting now. Let's see where teams stand as we dive in.<br/><br/><u>#nottop5gang</u><br/><br/><strong>10. Sid (-2) {1-3}</strong><br/><br/><em>History Fact: This is the fourth time in six years that Sid is starting 1-3 or worse. One exception? 2015, where Sid raced to a 3-0 start... before dropping the next SIX in a row.</em><br/><br/>You poor, poor soul. Everyone's favorite loveable loser is at it again, and this week was a truly pathetic performance in a winnable game. We started off thinking Sid was just getting unlucky after scoring high and losing against good teams, but in the last two weeks he's combined for a paltry 138 points. That's less than a few teams have scored in a single week this season. Even worse is that Sid takes on Brian, a consensus... top 2 team, in Week 5. Bell returning off the bye gives this team a much-needed boost, but this is a must-win for Sid. Dropping to 1-4 is likely going to be a death sentence this year, with how good some teams are looking in comparison to others. Time for an upset my man.<br/><br/><br/><strong>9. Girish (-) {1-3}</strong><br/><br/><em>History Fact: Girish is currently on pace for 1467 PA, which would be an all-time record. Don't feel too bad for him, though, because previously his average PA per season has been a laughable 1177, second-lowest.</em><br/><br/>Well, that actually ended as a respectable performance. Doesn't change the fact that this team is ass. Watson is underperforming, Conner has been abysmal outside of this week, Jacobs is only aight, and Thomas can't do much without Brees. Golladay and Hooper are promising but that's about it for the positives. I suppose if Hooper keeps performing well, the trade with Ruda will have been well worth it. BUT THAT DOESN'T MATTER IF YOU CONTINUE TO MAKE RIDICULOUS LINEUP DECISIONS. DON'T BENCH PLAYERS WHO HAVE SCORED TOUCHDOWNS EVERY WEEK OF THE SEASON SO FAR. <br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>8. Ashwin (-1) {1-3}</strong><br/><br/><em>History Fact: In 2014 Ashwin opened the first season by winning his first 8 games (a streak that hasn't been topped since). The following year, Ashwin opened by losing his first 7 games.</em><br/><br/>Congratulations on somehow being lowest scorer, in a week where Sid scored 61.8. Look, I know you tanked in week 1, but you haven't even scored 300 points in FOUR WEEKS. You are on pace for 943 points the entire season, which would break your own record of low scoring (take a wild guess which season it was, hint: it was mentioned a few sentences ago). Wilson is random and makes no sense, and James White is not a capable RB2 for you. It was good to see Freeman actually accomplish something, but the Falcons are inept and that might not last. And what in the world has happened to this receiving group? Hopkins, JuJu, and Watkins combined for 7 points. Awful, awful, awful. The Bears Defense might legitimately be the best player on this team. The only reason Ashwin isn't ranked lower is because we can reasonably expect those receivers to get a little better. Like Sid, though, Ashwin has a tough matchup coming up (me) and if he loses again, playoffs are a long shot.<br/><br/><br/><strong>7. Akash (+3) {1-3}</strong><br/><br/><em>History Fact: Akash started 2-2 in 2015, 2016, 2017, and 2018. The last time he didn't do that, it was a 1-3 start (much like now), in a season where he finished 3-10.</em><br/><br/>Maybe we were a little too harsh last week. Huuuuge upset over an undefeated team. Now let's not get too excited about Gallman, but maybe he can be a somewhat capable fill-in while Barkles is out. But Fournette finally had the kind of high-volume, high-production game he was drafted for. Will Dissly might be the savior at TE. And even in a terrible week, Lamar served Akash well. Even the bench looked solid. The biggest news for Akash is that he has two very winnable matchups coming up, against Girish and Ashwin. It's well within his power to suddenly be 3-3 in a few weeks, which is pretty surprising. After that, things get much tougher, so he better keep this up or he's in trouble.<br/><br/><br/><strong>6. Anish (-) {2-2}</strong><br/><br/><em>History Fact: Anish has been involved in three of the top 5 shootouts in league history. Sure didn't look like it last week, though.</em><br/><br/>A win is a win. We're willing to excuse the dismal performance of Anish's team because of the early and exciting return of Gordon, which should be followed by Hill's return shortly after. Anish has surprisingly won two in a row and has pushed himself into contention territory, honestly a great story considering how awful things looked just a little while ago. Now tied with Arjun in record, and with a surefire rise in scoring coming up with his returning players, Anish looks poised to push himself into the previously staunch top 5. David Montgomery is ass though. Also, for the love of God and for the sake of everyone's sanity, Anish stop asking about trades every six minutes. There's a reason no one ever trades with you.<br/><br/><br/><u>#top5gang</u><br/><br/><strong>5. Arjun (-1) {2-2}</strong><br/><br/><em>History Fact: In 2017, Arjun had a winning record with the sixth-lowest PF of all time. This included a record-low 39.5 points in Week 5. To put that in perspective, that means all his starters averaged 4.39 points, including his quarterback.</em><br/><br/>Dude, you need an RB2. Everything is fairly solid outside of that position, but it's killing you every week and is the reason Anish is hot on your tail right now. I respect the trade for Hyde and think he will usually do a little better than that, but like he got outscored by Duke Johnson, who you also own. That's kinda funny actually. The 98.1 this week seems respectable but when 19 come from your defense it's a little hard to feel excited. Throw in Davante Adams's turf toe, which could keep him out a week or two, and things look pretty shaky. The absolute worst part is a tough game against Ruda this coming week. He might be able to afford a loss considering Anish plays Will, but things are going to be seriously uncomfortable if he falls into losing-record territory with a 3-game losing streak. He'd be able to fix that with another solid trade, but that doesn't seem to be working out.<br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>4. Ruda (-1) {3-1}</strong><br/><br/><em>History Fact: Ruda could have sat out this entire season, and Will and I (third place in wins) could go 7-6 and still have fewer wins than him. Girish, Sid, Brian, and Ashwin could have perfect 13-0 seasons and still not catch up to Ruda's win total.</em><br/><br/>That was... surprising. This continues to be one of the shakier Ruda teams by his own standards, but considering how high those standards are that doesn't mean much. The team seems to be able to consistently score 90+, but its potential also seems capped at below 110. This week Mahomes didn't go off, and it slowed the whole team down. Elliott will certainly play better, but Mixon is officially entering bust territory and the God of Wins can't score 30 every week (right? I hope not). Bench is also a little suspect. But the trade for Kittle helps settle a position of concern, and all it will really take is for Patty and Zeke do start combining for 40+ every week (easily doable) to get this team to a winning season. Of concern, though, is a tough two weeks ahead with Arjun and Will on the docket.<br/><br/><br/><strong>3. Adit (+2) {3-1}</strong><br/><br/><em>History Fact: This is the third season in a row that I've started 3-1. In fact, in 2017 I started 5-1. Obviously, I somehow didn't make the playoffs either time.</em><br/><br/>Well I'm 3-1 again. What could possibly go wrong? I'm a playoff lock at this point. <br/><br/>For real, though, got a solid team over here. The three-headed monster at RB is really paying off, and Chubb is looking like a superstar. Even in a down week for the WRs (and TE) I was able to put up 113. Underrated point: Joey Slye is actually working wonders for me. Man is averaging 12 points a week. From the kicker position, that's like free money. Hokie Hokie Hokie Slye. The main worry for me is that the RBs seem a little unstable. Carson definitely saved his starting job this week, but you can't blame the Seahawks for flipping the script if he has another fumble or something next week. Aaron Jones will benefit from Jamaal Williams's injury, but has shown he can be injury prone himself with a full workload in the past. And at some point we'll have to see what Kareem Hunt's return does to Chubb's role. Those are all concerns for the future and I've built a pretty solid bench to try and combat anything that does happen. For now, though, I'm looking to take down Ashwin and move to 4-1.<br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>2. Will (-1) {3-1}</strong><br/><br/><em>History Fact: Will has had the second and third highest single-season PA of all time, but also the absolute lowest of all time. Been a rollercoaster of schedules over the years.<br/></em><br/>For the first time all season, Will finally drops out of the 1 spot. It's probably the longest anyone has ever held onto that spot. The good news is, Will can make it back there pretty easily. It was a really bad down week that showed this team isn't invincible, but it's also not something we can expect to keep happening. TY was out, and it looks like Mack suffered as a result. Cook and Kamara were bound to have some weeks where they don't explode, and Will is deep at WR after trading away Hyde (though I think it was a questionable decision). Oh, and he's definitely never touching the Ravens D again. I think the worst part of this week isn't the actual loss, but the fact that it was to Brian. There's a lot of bad blood out there at the top of the East. Will will have another chance to defeat Brian, but he can take comfort in the fact that he's still at the top of the standings thanks to his stellar PF before this week. Brian is only 20 behind, though.<br/><br/><br/><strong>1. Brian (+1) {3-1}<br/></strong><br/><em>History Fact: Brian actually was ranked #1 once before, in Week 8 of 2017 (you can view it in the 2017 page). You all might remember what happened that week, shortly after the ranking was written. Hint: it involved Akash and a lot of byes.</em><br/><br/>Hell has indeed frozen over. Except, as noted above, this actually isn't the first time Brian has been ranked at the top. Where else could we rank him though? He took down the previous fearsome #1 team with ease. The most impressive thing about this team, as Brian has noted, is that it has yet to score below 111 in any week. That kind of remarkable consistency is exactly what everyone drafts hoping for. Even a dip in productoin, down to scoring 100, would keep this team a cut above many others. All the risks he took are paying off: Engram is great, Kupp is probably the best receiver on the Rams right now, DJ is servicable, and Ekeler has been absolutely killing it. Oh, about that, though. Ekeler gone bro. The early return of Gordon might cause major issues for this team. You can point to Gurley on the bench and say Brian will be fine, but we are talking about the #2 RB in the league just vanishing. Or at least, fading to FLEX-worthy. Huge loss we always knew was coming. For now, though, there was nowhere else to rank BWu. He is our king, for this week. <br/><br/></p>
