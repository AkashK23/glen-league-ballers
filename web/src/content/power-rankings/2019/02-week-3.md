<p>Our guest writer this week is Ruda. Enjoy!</p>
<p>* * *</p>
<p>Here are the Week 3 Rankings! Attached to each usual paragraph is a ‘Prophet’s Suggestion’ about what moves that the team should take. Some are serious, some not so much. Take them with a grain of salt. <br/><br/><br/>As always, blame Adit if something feels wrong.<br/><br/><br/><strong>10. Ashwin (-1) {0-2}</strong><br/>The initial results of tanking Week 1 are mediocre at best for Ashwin. Many of the top waiver picks were WR’s, but he’s already set at that position with 3 legitimate WR1’s, albeit we’ll see how Juju meshes with the new Steelers QB. Speaking of new QB, Big Ben going on IR means that this is another position Ashwin needs to worry about, along with his glaring holes at the RB spots. Freeman really needs to get going, as he might be the only RB on Ashwin’s team who is going to get atleast 10 carries. The bench is uninspiring, as Lutz just lost his QB, and Bill O’Buttchin is still the HC. These have been a dark two weeks, but the future looks even bleaker. A tough matchup with Brian awaits.<br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: Leverage Ertz to a TE-needy team for RB’s, and dump mediocre bench WR’s for high upside, handcuff RB’s, and pray. Alternatively, start practicing your Stand-Up skills.<br/><br/></p>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/power-rankings/2019/02-week-3-e7cc4e39.png"/>
<p><strong>9. Anish (+1) {0-2}</strong><br/>A smart trade last week has Anish jumping up one spot, albeit still with a dim outlook for the playoffs. The price of gaining WR depth was losing Matt Ryan, which stings even more now that Cam is re-injured. A potential bellcow in Montgomery and sudden star John Ross help things, but Anish needs to start winning—and fast. Winning the division may already be out of the question, but I have a gut feeling he can make a push for a wild card spot with the right moves. He almost pulled off the upset against Ruda this past week, and this week he gets another shot at a division foe in Arjun. <br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: Cry over Cam. Cry over Tyreek. DM Melvin on Twitter to come back soon. <br/><br/><br/><strong>8. Girish (-1) {0-2}</strong><br/>Girish’s season has been like the Panthers, in that a tremendous amount of hope was quickly dashed. His team is littered with injury concerns between Conner, Jacobs, and Desean. Michael “Handsome Squidward” Thomas also has Teddy Bumwater throwing him the ball now. Kittle isn’t producing because Shanahan keeps letting FREAKING JEFF WILSON JR to get all the redzone TD’s. Hope remains for this team though, as the bench is honestly pretty decent, and depending on matchups, a lot of players can be useful. First, a test with Will awaits.<br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: Build a shrine of Dan Bailey. Hope AB acts like a normal human being for once in his life so Brady keeps feasting. Perhaps pair a bench player/starter for an upgraded starter?<br/><br/><br/><strong>7. Sid (+1) {0-2}</strong><br/>Unequivocally one of the best 0-2 teams I’ve ever seen. The RB’s as solid, and Bell will only continue to get insanely high touches with the Jets down so many QB’s. Defenders still look scared shitless of tackling Derrick Henry. Odell is still the handsomest man in the league. QB and TE remain problem spots, but not due to a lack of talent. I would bet that one of Baker or OJ Howard figures it out as the season goes along, but time is not something that Sid has. The bench outside of Ballagio and Lindsay is weak, but it just takes one quality waiver pick to change fortune. Buoyed by a high PF, Sid is my sleeper pick for the playoffs (to be fair, I think everyone is secretly rooting for this!).<br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: Know when to cut bait and bench Baker and OJ. Add a TE from someone with too many (Darren Waller!). Ballage.<br/><br/></p>

<p><strong><br/>6. Akash (-1) {0-2}</strong><br/>Heartbreaking is the only way to describe Akash’s slim loss to Will (maybe funny is a good word too). Nonetheless, Akash must put his rage aside and focus on a winnable matchup this week against Sid. His QB and RB1 are stellar (nearly 50 average points between them), but that unfortunately means his team is lacking in other areas. Diggs is definitely hurt by misfortune, but also by Cousins turning into a pumpkin over the summer. “Gardener Minshew” had more rushing yards than Fournette last week. Cook was bad with Brees—imagine how he’ll be with Teddy. Michel should be helped by the Pats having an insanely easy schedule and an amazing defense, and Kerroyn and Freeman both showed signs of life last week. <br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: Funnel that rage from last week into Sid. Scour the waiver for TE’s. Don’t procrastinate adding a good HC. Keep Washington, he might be a good play in the future with Rudolph. <br/><br/><br/><strong>5. Arjun (-3) {2-0}</strong><br/>A stellar Week 1 showing was followed up by a subpar Week 2. Still, 2-0 is 2-0, and Arjun’s team looks like it’s rounding into form. CMC’s performance is something to be monitored going forward with Cam out. Usually, when a star QB goes down, the RB takes a volume jump and efficiency hit. In CMC’s case, there might not be much of a volume jump to be had. Arjun can rest easy knowing that the suddenly explosive combo of Mark Andrews, Adams, and Allen are good enough to carry him. Gore is a sneaky good play while Singletary remains out. Add in a stellar defense playing the high school JV level Dolphins, and Arjun should cruise to 3-0 vs Anish this week. <br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: Give Duke as an offering to the Prophet. Figure out the flex spot-perhaps double TE is the move? Hope Dak doesn’t start holding out in the middle of the season. <br/><br/><br/><strong>4. Ruda (+1) {2-0}</strong><br/>Solid, yet unspectacular. Somehow appointed the “Prophet” despite starting Mike Geico at TE last week. Perhaps regression will come eventually for Patty Mahomes, but for now, he’s carrying this team. Zeke should have a whole Thanksgiving feast vs the tankers this weekend. Add in a variety of decent WR’s, and Ruda looks primed to atleast compete for the playoffs. The big key here is Mixon; can he return to last seasons form? I don’t think people understand how bad he has been. Man has 17 carries for 27 yards total. Outside of injured players, he has been the biggest bust inside of the top 20. If he can even become a decent RB2, this team can jump into the truly elite. <br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: No more “Fuck It Mask Off” moves. Also finish that BSST homework soon.<br/><br/></p>

<p><strong><br/>3. Adit (+1) {2-0}</strong><br/>Aditian. The Air Force seems to be taking off, propelled by the trio of Jones, Carson, and Chubb. Julio and Joey “Mothafucking” Slye have added additional fuel to this team. Rodgers has been more mustache than fantasy player the first two weeks, but we can expect that to change in the future once his schedule lightens up. I love the addition of Marquise Brown, and “Hollywood” is as cool a nickname you’re going to find. His matchup vs the Chiefs barely-there defense might be the X-Factor in the matchup vs Ruda this week. Let’s see if this team has even more juice for full liftoff to 3-0.<br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: Pick the right players for the WR2 slot. Easier said than done, especially since Adit knows about the problems with having many talented players at the same position.<br/><br/><br/><strong>2. Brian (+1) {2-0}</strong><br/>For all the shit Brian talks, he sure has been backing it up recently. A dominating win over Akash was followed up by another crushing of bitter rival Girish. Now, he plays Ashwin, and I honestly think the trio of Josh Allen/DJ/Ekeler will be enough for victory. Speaking of Ekeler, he does not only have the shiniest head in the league, but has also been THE overall RB1. Brian also has a massive edge on the margins, as the Pats D(!!!) and Greg the Leg will provide vital advantages in tight matchups. As long as Brown’s hormones stay stable and Gurley’s knee holds up, I think Brian has a legitimate shot at dethroning Will. <br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: Streaming QB’s is viable, as long as you’re smart with it. Keep up future thinking moves, such as adding the Chargers D two weeks before they play the Dolphins.<br/><br/></p>

<p><strong><br/>1. Will (-) {2-0}</strong><br/>What’s there left to say? Dubbed the team of destiny by yours truly, I think Will is going to be atop this list for the foreseeable future. Cook looks like he might channel his inner Kemba and will this team to their first ring. The WR’s have been much better than expected, and Will’s faith in his NC State man Brissett and the Colts Offense has paid dividends. In a year where TE is truly a wasteland, Kelce has proved himself to be a cut above Ertz and Kittle. This narrow win over Akash (.1 points!!) proves that the only thing standing between Will and his first ring are injuries and misfortune. He’s that good.<br/><br/>Prophet’s Suggestion: I actually think Will should be giving me suggestions for this year. <br/><br/></p>
