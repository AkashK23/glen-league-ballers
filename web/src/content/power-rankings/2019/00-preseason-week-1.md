<p>Ladies and gentlemen, welcome back. <br/><br/>That felt like the longest offseason ever. Remember the Rams Patriots Super Bowl? Actually, I don't think anyone wants to.<br/><br/>But here we are, and it's finally over. Tomorrow marks the start of the 2019 NFL season, and the beginning of a new era of fantasy football. I hesitate to say a league name; it really seems up in the air right now.<br/><br/>Judging teams in the preseason is enormously difficult. Our idea of team strength is ten times better after even just Week 1. The NFL is just too unpredictable to know exactly what's going to happen. Last year, we started with Akash as 10 and Anish as 1. Guess who made the playoffs and who didn't?<br/><br/>Still, that doesn't mean we can't try. Newly inaugurated Head Ranker Arjun worked with me to put together a solid list that factors in all the most recent news and drama. We'll keep careful track of these rankings over time, with the help of Arjun's famous charts, to see just how good they are. I'm also throwing in a fun little record prediction, just to piss everyone off even more.<br/><br/>For now, enjoy the following list, and let it keep you until kickoff tomorrow. After that, we finally enter the bliss/cardiovascular disease that is the fantasy season. As for those of you in the bottom half, you can find pitchforks at your local home improvement store, although Amazon Prime is probably your best bet these days. Preseason rankings have no extremes, meaning that everyone is fairly close together. James White can be a FLEX where he belongs now, but let's be real. This is a Patriots RB. They hate their own RBs. What a WR duo, though. Baker Mayfield was so exciting in his rookie year, but don't most quarterbacks suffer a major sophomore slump?
