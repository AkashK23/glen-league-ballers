<p>Five years ago, Thanos snapped half of the GLB's teams out of existence. It couldn't have been more clear who remained: the #top5gang consisting of Adit, Ruda, Brian, Will, and Arjun. No one else even existed, and the playoffs were certain for these fortunate souls.<br/><br/>But then, something happened. Someone... acquired the infinity stones and REVERSED the snap. Hulk...<br/><br/>Now, everyone is back. The #top5gang has lost its footing as the only contenders, because the un-snapped are back with a vengeance. The returned Avengers are all powerful, and all want a spot in the Big Dance. Let's see who's in the running.<br/><br/>And remember, with just three short weeks of the season left... we are clearly in the ENDGAME.<br/><br/></p>

He is the true leader of the late-season surge... because he caused it.<br/><br/>Avenger: Hulk. With a single snap, Anish revived all those who had fallen and has singlehandedly ruined the previous elite group. Also, both Anish and Hulk are devilishly handsome.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>6: Ruda (-3) {6-4}</strong><br/><br/>Playoff Hopes: Iffy?<br/><br/>How the mighty have fallen. I want to start off by saying that Ruda will probably beat me this week because of my byes. But I think it's time to recognize, objectively speaking, that this is a team in serious turmoil right now. He has scored in the 70's for three of the past four weeks, and has also lost three of the past four games. Add in Kittle's injury woes and things look a little dire. Throw in a tough remaining schedule (me, Brian, Arjun) on top of all that, and you might say it's time to hit the panic button. This is Ruda we're talking about, though, so don't count him out ever. At 6-4 he's still in decent position to make the playoffs.<br/><br/>Avenger: Thor. Badass af at first and didn't get snapped, but then became an overweight alcoholic who couldn't do anything.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>5: Brian (-) {6-4}</strong><br/><br/>Playoff Hopes: Low<br/><br/>Brian somehow finds himself at the top of the East, but it sure doesn't feel like he's the best team in the division. In fact, he's probably the third best team in the division at it stands. He has averaged a wonderful 79 points in the past four weeks, after averaging 119 a week for the first six. Mounting injuries and the complete collapse of first-rounder David Johnson have taken their toll. Brian's saving grace is that he somehow managed to go 2-2 in this miserable past month, and due to his solid current record there is absolutely no counting him out come playoff time. But hoo man, look at that remaining schedule. Girish, Ruda, Will. There's a reason Brian only has a 39% chance of playoffs, according to that site.<br/><br/>Avenger: Iron Man. His self-sacrifice will never be forgotten. Especially by whichever team makes the playoffs instead of him.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>4: Will (-) {5-5}</strong><br/><br/>Playoff Hopes: Iffy? <br/><br/>Man, I'm really starting to feel for Will. I don't think enough attention has been given to how unlucky he's getting. After an incredible 5-2 start, he has lost the last three games in a row because his opponents have averaged 123 points a week in that span. One hundred twenty-three points. When Sid drops 120+ on you, you know someone upstairs isn't happy with you. The thing is, I'm still pretty convinced that Will might have the best team in the league. The trade with Girish was interesting but I think in the long run it makes him slightly better. It's also easy to forget: Will still has the highest PF in the league, by a shrinking but decent margin. Being a game behind in the East is the only reason his playoff hopes are iffy, but I think some regression to the mean for Will's opponents will give him the wins he needs and put him in alright position.<br/><br/>Avenger: Hawkeye. Didn't get snapped, but turned into a vengeful lunatic because of all the tragedy that has befallen him.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>3: Arjun (+3) {6-4}</strong><br/><br/>Playoff Hopes: Solid<br/><br/>Well then. Where did this come from? One of the more notable late-season surges, Arjun suddenly finds himself in an excellent position. I'm not kidding, just think about it. He has the highest PF in the West and is sitting at second place in the division. And he's won three in a row, scoring 100+ all three of those weeks. We might dismiss Arjun as someone entering the fringe competition, but he's actually right in the thick of it. If I lose this week and he wins, he will probably take the division lead. Having a division lead going into Week 12 is one of the best things you can do in terms of playoff chances.<br/><br/>Avenger: Drax. Got snapped but came back and helped win the battle. Both Arjun and Drax are literally the hugest and most jacked people I've ever seen. <br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>2: Girish (-1) {6-4}</strong><br/><br/>Playoff Hopes: Solid<br/><br/>An absolute thriller of a Monday Night fantasy game ended with Girish crashing back down to earth after a historic win streak. The loss was a painful one as it threw him out of the division lead and in a little more precarious of a position than we envisioned Girish being in. However, this is a still a team that is sitting at 6-4 after starting 0-3, a truly impressive feat. Girish can stomp his way to a high playoff chance with a rivalry win this week, and there's a merciful game against Sid to end the season. Plenty of clear paths for a second-ever playoff appearance for this franchise.<br/><br/>Avenger: Captain Marvel. Literally wasn't even around as the entire world ended early on, but came back to destroy everyone down the stretch. <br/><br/></p>
