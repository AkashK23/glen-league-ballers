<p><em>Week 6 somehow done;<br/>Isn't fantasy so fun?<br/>Until it isn't.</em></p>
<p><br/>Weirdly enough, we are just about at the midway point of the fantasy season. Arjun and I might have naively hoped that this would result in the rankings making themselves more clear, but the opposite has happened. This was definitely one of the tougher weeks to rank. Things are looking a little messier now, and there might be a chance opening up for a few teams to make a second-half push. For some of you guys right in the middle, it's gonna be really important to look ahead to the coming matchups and plan accordingly; I'll touch on some of those.<br/><br/>I'm feeling extra poetic today, so I'm carefully crafting a beatiful Haiku for every team. Prepare to be blown away by my artistic genius.<br/><br/><u>#nottop5gang</u><br/><br/><strong>10: Sid (-1) {1-5}</strong></p>
<p><em><br/>"It's just luck", he said;<br/>Well that's where you're wrong, Judha<br/>OJ Howard tho</em></p>
<p><br/>Well Sid, I hate to break it to you, but there's more than just luck involved. Some things haven't gone your way this season, but your ranking here at the bottom of the list isn't just the result of bad luck. For one, you are the lowest scorer in the league and so far, on pace for a bottom ten all-time scoring finish. But what stands out to me is just your consistently strange and nonchalant management style. Why did you have three QBs before this week? Why were you hanging onto Jordan Reed for so long, who is literally contemplating retirement with the remaining half of his brain? Why do you still have Ballage (please spare me, oh Meme Gods)? Why are you hanging on to the Rams D when they suck and the Panthers are doing just fine (even on this Panthers bye week, the Rams are an iffy start)? It's just weird decision after weird decision for this franchise. Until you learn from your mistakes, Sid, you will never sniff the playoffs. I'm sorry for the harsh reality.<br/><br/>Actually according to Brian you'll beat me in the playoffs so I take it all back.<br/><br/>Note: I wrote this before the RAMSey trade and I'm too stubborn to change it.<br/><br/><br/><strong>9: Akash (-2) {1-5}</strong></p>
<p><em><br/>Poor little Akash;<br/>Killed by Jamaal Williams' butt<br/>But ElItE FrAnChIsE?</em></p>
<p><br/>Ooooooooooof. Painful loss after painful loss has pushed you to 1-5, something that hasn't happened to your franchise since 2014. Jamaal Williams intentionally sitting down at the 1 yard line was pretty symbolic of your season as a whole. I'll point to your respectable PF, though. You're not scoring terribly, and there's one big headline for you now: Barkley is back. You've averaged over 100 in the three weeks without him, so there's no reason you can't hit that again with him back. Buttttt DisslyWorld is gone for good. The waiver wire is an absolute mess this week, so good luck with that. I'm sure Jared Cook will serve you well.<br/><br/>In all honesty I expect you to beat me. #trapgame2019<br/><br/><br/><strong>8: Anish (+2) {2-4}</strong></p>
<p><em><br/>"Hey Anish, let's trade!"<br/>"Your top RB for &lt;bench guy&gt;"<br/>"Aight never mind, bye."</em></p>
<p><br/>You got what you had waited so long for: Gordon and Tyreek are back. And you used that to beat down Girish with a resounding... loss? Get yourself a quarterback who doesn't turn it over 57 times a game, and understand that your running backs aren't good. Damien Williams and David Montgomery have very little value at this point, and if you're going to make a trade then you should know that. Making a trade is what you should be trying to do right now, because at 2-4 you're on the edge of the cliff. There's no one for you to add on waivers, except for a few QB options you might find useful. Hunter Henry is promising, though. You aren't far behind the coveted 5th spot in terms of record, but you are not getting there without some substantial changes.<br/><br/>You're not tenth anymore so no complaining.<br/><br/><br/><strong>7: Ashwin (+1) {2-4}</strong></p>
<p><em><br/>In a real franchise<br/>Ashwin would never be fired;<br/>Jason Garrett clone</em></p>
<p><br/>You tend to look terrible 90% of the time, but then pull something out of your ass to stay somewhat respectable. In weeks 1, 2, and 4 you averaged 58.3 points. In weeks 3, 5, and 6 you averaged 110.8 points. I guess I'll call that an upward trend, even though I'm pretty sure it's totally random. We couldn't rank a team that has scored 100+ in three of the past four weeks any lower than seventh, though. A lot of that is thanks to the excellent pickup of Russell Wilson. This week's narrow win makes up for your heartbreaking loss last week, so you don't have to hate me anymore. Surprisingly enough, you are in position to make a push here. To do that, you'll need to stop the complete flop weeks. Tough matchup coming up against Will, but perhaps even more important is the following week where you play Girish. That is a crucial game; if you maintain your 1-win or less difference with him, then handing him a loss while taking a win for yourself could be HUGE.<br/><br/>Make some moves, waiver or trade, to get consistent players. You have a shot here.<br/><br/><br/><strong>6: Girish (-) {3-3}</strong></p>
<p><em><br/>In this team's history,<br/>'19 is already its<br/>second best season.</em></p>
<p><br/>The surge continues, a rare statement for this franchise (unless you're talking about its record-setting 9-game losing streak). That fifth spot is wide open for the taking, and you aren't THAT far behind Arjun in PF either. I have literally no idea what is going on with Matt Ryan and Austin Hooper but I assume it's some sort of love affair. Picking up Hooper was a huge move for you, and sending away Brady at least helped you settle with Watson as your QB, even if Chris Thompson isn't giving you what you hoped in return. Speaking of which, your RB situation is a mess this week. In a tough matchup against Ruda, it's easy to be worried. The good news is that Arjun has an equally difficult matchup, so a loss might not kill you. But then, weirdly enough, don't forget about Ashwin. You don't want to be in a position where you both are 3-4 and you lose to him the week after. <br/><br/>Win this week, or open the door to those beneath you. Your goal is to make this a competition between you and Arjun, and no one else.<br/><br/><br/><u>#top5gang<br/></u><br/><strong>5: Arjun (-) {3-3}</strong></p>
<p><em><br/>Stopped a three-game skid<br/>Now faces angry B. Wu<br/>Without McCaffrey</em></p>
<p><br/>The Haiku pretty much says it all. Relief, relief, relief in Arjuntown after it looked like a tragic rollercoaster of a season. It was a solid performance that saw Matt Ryan, new acquisition Julian Edelman, and the Steelers D/ST step up in a "down" week for McCaffrey. That has to spark some hope, especially considering how often the Falcons will likely continue to chuck it (to everyone but Julio) while down. But on the topic of down weeks for McCaffrey... he's gonna be relaxing on a couch next Sunday. A few weeks ago that was instant doom for you. But both Texans running backs did great on Sunday, you'll just have to correctly choose who to start this time. And I think Breida was a pretty smart acquisition too. He'll be a huge help with a good matchup this week. Beating Brian is no easy task, take it from me, but this isn't an auto-loss for you at all. What you need to be worried about is Girish hot on your tail.<br/><br/>Davante turf toe no moe? <br/> <br/><br/><strong>4: Will (-2) {4-2}</strong></p>
<p><em><br/>Will is bipolar;<br/>Literally makes no sense<br/>Take medication</em></p>
<p><br/>Ummmmm okay then. I do want to say I think it was a little lost in the fray that you had your Colts were on bye, as well as a few other key depth players. And, as we do know, both your receivers basically collapsed dead the second they stepped on the field. I will never expect to see this team perform this poorly ever again. What we should be concerned about is whether it will continue to have random flop weeks like this. You have had three weeks over 136, and two weeks under 72. That's nuts. However, I'll be expecting your thank-you card any day now for beating Brian, because it kept him from not only taking first seed in the East but distancing himself as well. He did take first, but you're tied in record and not far behind in PF. A few more of those 136+ weeks and you can easily grab it right back. <br/><br/>A few more of those 70 weeks, though, and you can enjoy a play-in against fifth seed. <br/><br/><br/><strong>3: Ruda (-) {5-1}</strong><br/></p>
<p><em><br/>The West is the Best;<br/>Please give someone else a chance<br/>Go away Ruda</em></p>
<p><br/>It may not have been an exciting game, but it was a resounding win that kept you tied for the best record in the league. Your only problem is me, but since I'll play you in several weeks without Aaron Jones or Chris Carson you can go ahead and take the win now. So really, you're 6-1. Congrats. What we learned on Sunday is that the West is by far the better division. It was a nice, fleeting thought that maybe I wasn't in the hard division for once, but oh well. Some things never change. This week is interesting for you, though. You'll be playing Girish, who has actually outscored your team both of the last two weeks. Not a gimme at all, and a game you'll want to win to keep pace with me after my "theoretically" "easy" "matchup" against "Akash". Or something like that. Brb gonna go knock on all the trees in all the forests in the world. <br/><br/>Thank you for benching Joe Mixon. That was painful to watch. Also, Kittle is this year's Julio. Stop convincing Girish to give you studs right before they turn into monsters.<br/><br/><br/><strong>2: Brian (-1) {4-2}</strong></p>
<p><em><br/>Gurley could die and<br/>McVay would mark him as Q.<br/>Ice up son, ice up.</em></p>
<p><br/>Gurley is about as healthy as we all expected him to be. Well, all of us except you, Brian. The scary part is that clearly the entire Rams team suffers when he's not on the field, because Lord knows Goff can't carry an offense. As a result, your team slows down big time. Luckily, slowing down for you still apparently means putting up 100+ and staying competitive all day. Please get rid of Gerald Everett, for your own mental health, and that's already one less Rams piece. The Patriots D is basically one of the best players in the league at any position right now. They'll stop being QUITE this good as the schedule gets harder, but it's hard to imagine them putting up single digits more than once or twice. And, for as much as I made fun of Will Fuller's drops, I doubt those happen again. The fact that Watson is still targeting him, and finding him open, on many deep bombs a game is actually very encouraging. I want to emphasize just how close the upper part of this ranking is. Ranking a #1 was very difficult and not something we jumped on immediately. If I could, I'd call us 1A and 1B. I'll explain the reasoning more coming up. Anyway, next up for Brian: big chance to turn things around against a CMC-less Arjun. As I said before, this is not an easy game, but you clearly have the better team.<br/><br/>Don't drop to 4-3. William and Sujay will make you pay.<br/><br/><br/><strong>1: Adit (+3) {5-1}</strong></p>
<p><em><br/>This exact same shit<br/>happened just two years ago;<br/>I expect the worst</em></p>
<p><br/>Fun fact: I have started the season 5-1 every other year for the past five years. 2015, 2017, and now. <br/><br/>Less fun fact: in that 2017 season, Brian and I were ranked the same (2nd and 1st) heading into Week 7. Both of us had major collapses shortly after, finishing 7-6 with Brian narrowly squeezing into the playoffs ahead of me. Let's hope history doesn't repeat itself. <br/><br/>Anyway, as I was saying, 1A and 1B. I actually didn't want to put myself at the top, and neither did Arjun. Independently speaking, I don't feel like I have the best team in the league. But look at it this way: I just beat the former top team, led the league in scoring, and took first seed. If there's ever a week for me to be 1, before I likely fall a spot or two again, it's this one. I have three of the top 10 RBs (including two of the top 5), a great WR in Julio, a solid QB in Brady, and I think I've been pretty smart about my defenses and depth so far this year. Next up, #trapgame2019 against cash money himself. Please go easy on me Akash.<br/><br/>Least fun fact: 8 teams ever have started the season 5-1 or better. Only one of them didn't make the playoffs.<br/><br/>Help. I can feel it coming.<br/><br/></p>
