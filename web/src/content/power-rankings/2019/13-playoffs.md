<p>Welcome to the postseason. After 13 long weeks, the standings have been finalized and we are ready to roll. The Top 5 compete for a championship and eternal glory, while the Bottom 5 fight to escape the punishment and eternal embarrassment.<br/><br/>I'm going to take a purely analytical approach here, outlining every team's path within their respective brackets. Keep this info in mind as the second season kicks off on Thursday. <br/><br/>This is the final Power Rankings of the 2019 season. Been a good run. Let's send this year off with a bang.<br/><br/>Here are the two brackets as they stand right now. I will also post these separately, below all rankings, so they can be viewed easily anytime.<br/></p>

<p><u>#nottop5gang</u><br/><br/><strong>10: Ashwin (-) {4-9}</strong><br/><br/>A win didn't really do anything for this team, especially considering it only took a 68 point effort against my abysmal performance. Ashwin ends the regular season as the last seed for the third straight year. In fact, he has been last seed for four out of the league's six years. <br/><br/>While there is no saving this lost season, Ashwin has one important mission left: avoid the punishment. It's certainly doable, but more because fantasy is so random than because of the level this team plays at. If he's going to win a game, it will be through his three best players: Wilson, Hopkins, and Chark. Those three can certainly put up a couple dozen points on their own. The battle beyond them, then, is who to start at the remaining positions. Ashwin has a bunch of decent running backs, but no one stands out. He'll have to choose carefully between Drake, Freeman, Scarbrough, Hunt, Damien, and Jamaal each week that he plays. That is no easy task, and it's very possible that all of them do poorly. That's what puts Ashwin at 10.<br/><br/>As for the path ahead: Ashwin is unfortunately one of the three teams who is a lock for the Reaper Bracket, meaning he essentially has a bye next week. After that, he will take on the loser of the play-in, either Anish or Sid. There is no doubt that both of those teams is far superior to his. Unless he gets lucky, he's going to be playing for his life in the final week against either Akash or Will. <br/><br/><strong>9: Will (-1) {5-8}</strong><br/><br/>In a season full of crazy storylines, perhaps none was more shocking and upsetting than Will's epic collapse. Enough has been said about it, so I'll just add one thing: this somehow became Will's second worst season of all time. Imagine if someone told you that halfway through the season, as Will was 5-2 and leading the league in PF.<br/><br/>On paper, the team is still good. Watson just shredded the Patriots, Jacobs continues to impress, Kelce is everything he was drafted to be, and Golladay and Singletary (though benched last week) look excellent. Will just needs to cement his lineup properly and he should end up playing much better than his 5-8 record suggests. There are huge concerns with Kamara and an injured Hilton, though. Not to mention Amari Cooper has slowed down. <br/><br/>Somehow, Will is another lock for the Reaper Bracket. At 8th seed, he will be playing Akash next week. I'd say that's going to be a pretty even game. Will is better on paper, but is on a SIX game losing streak (tied for third all-time), while Akash has put together a small win streak. Not a great position to be in, but this team has the potential to redeem itself.<br/><br/><strong>8: Akash (+1) {5-8}</strong><br/><br/>Akash experienced a spurt at the very end of the season. Now, we shouldn't get carried away here; Week 12 was impressive at 119 points, but the surrounding weeks were wins with less than 90 scored. <br/><br/>With Lamar Jackson, anything is possible. Then again, that's what I told myself with Patrick Mahomes last year. Rudolph looks to be an excellent addition at tight end, though (SKOL). If Marlon Mack returns, that will basically eliminate Jonathan Williams from fantasy relevance, and Akash will be left searching for a FLEX. It isn't entirely clear who that will be. If Akash can solve his FLEX issue, he might be okay.<br/><br/>The last team locked into the Reaper Bracket, Akash will skip a week and then take on a crumbling Will. As I said before, that's going to be a very even game. Hard to tell whether Sid's miraculous victory (plunging Will into this game) will end up being better or worse for Akash. If he loses, he's suddenly in the dreaded final punishment game.<br/><br/><strong>7: Sid (-) {6-7}</strong><br/><br/>Wow. It might have been a bit overshadowed because of the competition between Anish and me, but Sid just pulled off one of the craziest wins of all time. Literally ever. Girish was up on him by 1.9 points before Dalvin Cook fumbled and went out for the rest of the game, sealing a narrow victory by 0.1. And this miraculous event totally shook up the bracket; Sid flew up out of the Reaper Bracket and into the play-in. <br/><br/>And this is a team that actually is performing very well. 121, 106, 115, and 95 the past four weeks. Derrick Henry continues to Ballage the fuck out, and the unsung hero Josh Allen is a huge reason for Sid's success. Expect some positive regression for OBJ and Hunter Henry, and throw in that Thielen is coming back, and we've got a solid squad here. <br/><br/>As I mentioned earlier, Sid now has the luxury of being in the losers' play-in game. If he can continue his streak of success, he can avoid the Reaper Bracket entirely. Anish is no pushover, though. Should Sid lose this week, he will then face bottom-seed Ashwin. It would take a shocking loss there to really put him in a threatened position. Things could be worse.<br/><br/><strong>6: Anish (-) {6-7}</strong><br/><br/>The other crazy game of last week ended in huge disappointment for Anish. He was unable to get the win over Akash thanks to a fourth quarter one-handed touchdown snag by Kyle Rudolph, and therefore remained in the losers' play-in, instead of replacing me in the winners' play-in. Ouch.<br/><br/>I'll say what I told Anish already: he did everything right. Being patient with injuries and making solid trades throughout the season led to this team becoming extremely competitive. Stafford being out continues to hurt, especially as Brissett begins to struggle a bit. But Gordon, Chubb, and Evans pave the way for victories. Plenty of great talent elsewhere, too, between Montgomery, Gallup, Landry, etc. This is really going to be a great game against Sid.<br/><br/>But being evenly matched is a blessing and a curse. If he loses, it will be Anish who takes on Ashwin in the first week of the Reaper Bracket. That, like for Sid, should be a win. I don't foresee a major panic for this team, but anything can happen.<br/><br/><u>#top5gang</u><br/><br/><strong>5: Adit (-1) {7-6}</strong><br/><br/>After starting 7-3, I suffered another major collapse that ended with me backing into the Top 5. I made it thanks to help from Akash, but I have still scored terribly and dropped three straight.<br/><br/>There really isn't a question that I'm currently the worst of the Top 5. I have seemed just unable to put points on the board the past few weeks. However, I also think that my team has been massively underperforming and is due for some positive regression. I'll be getting Julio and Ertz back from injury, and it's not hard to imagine Aaron Jones and Tyreek getting back to their high-scoring ways. I'm at full strength for the first time in a while, but we can't be confident in my team after these past few showings. Very hard to predict how this will go.<br/><br/>Saved at last from the clutches of the Reaper Bracket, I find myself with an outside shot at a playoff run with no stress about the punishment. I will start by taking on Girish to see who gets fourth seed. Girish is probably a little better than me, but he has also dropped three of the past four. If I manage to win that, I will then have a very difficult matchup with a red-hot Arjun. It will take an upset there to reach the championship.<br/><br/><strong>4: Girish (-1) {7-6}</strong><br/><br/>As I mentioned, this team has lost three of its past four games, and the only win came off an easy game against a flailing Will. Yesterday, Girish experienced one of the most horrific losses we have ever seen, throwing him out of the top 3 and into the play-in game. That fumble and injury by Cook may prove super significant.<br/><br/>This is a good team, even if it has struggled recently. Michael Thomas is absolute fire, and DJ Moore is looking like a top-tier receiver all of a sudden. Throw in Sutton's quickly-formed chemistry with new QB Drew Lock, and things don't look bad at all. This week is very up in the air though, as all three of Girish's main running backs are questionable. In the case of Conner and Mack, that's a good thing because they have both missed quite a bit of time and may make a return. In the case of Cook, it's disastrous as he's Girish's best player. Closely monitor these injuries as the week progresses; they may make or break Girish's season.<br/><br/>Beaten down into the play-in, Girish will have to beat me to have hopes of going anywhere. If his running backs heal up properly, I think he has the upper-hand in this one, but if one or two of them are out (especially Cook), this becomes a very scary game for him. If he wins, he'll have to take on Arjun, which no one wants to do right now.<br/><br/><strong>3. Brian (+2) {8-5}<br/></strong><br/>Congratulations Brian on your first ever division championship. This also marks the third straight year you have made the playoffs. This turnaround for your franchise has been nothing short of remarkable. Interestingly enough, you are the only team in the Top 5 to not currently have a ring. Here's your chance to change that. <br/><br/>Performance-wise, Brian has still been a bit all over the place recently. Last week saw a narrow victory and a solid performance, but it followed a pretty bad loss where he scored only 80. The unpredictability here has to make Brian very thankful that he doesn't have to worry about an extra game. It stems from how truly random players like Gurley and Ekeler can be. Looks like he's found an absolute gem in Devante Parker, though. <br/><br/>After a week of rest, Brian will take on Ruda to see who gets into the championship game. Just two weeks ago, Brian was routed 131-80 against that man, so that can't make him too comfortable. Really just depends on which Brian's team shows up. A win there will send him straight to the championship for the second year in a row - it might even end up being a rematch of last year's championship against Arjun.<br/><br/><strong>2. Ruda (-) {8-5}</strong><br/><br/>For the FIFTH year in a row, Ruda has made the playoffs. It's basically a given at this point. There was a major threat of him getting knocked down to the play-in game, but the crazy win by Sid puts him comfortably towards the top where he can take a week off.<br/><br/>This is another team that can be a little random. For example, scoring the past four weeks has been: 72, 84, 131, 98. A good chunk of that is Godwin and Lockett, who both seem to alternate between amazing and terrible whenever they feel like it. Mahomes and Zeke provide a lot of stability here, though, and even Mixon seems to be finding his footing. <br/><br/>The week after this coming on, Ruda will take on Brian. As I've been saying, this is going to be a very strange and unpredictable game. I could see it being a 75-70 win for either one of them, or a 120-110 one. Or perhaps a 115-80 blowout. Really no idea at all what's going to happen. It's a better position to be in than Girish/me though, as Ruda won't have to face the consistency of Arjun again. If he can pull off the win, it's only one more until the championship is his for the second time. <br/><br/><strong>1. Arjun (-) {9-4}</strong><br/><br/>Arjun earned his first ever division championship on Monday, marking his second straight playoff appearance. Since joining the league, he has gone 22-17 and won a ring. This year, he looks to be the first franchise to win multiple rings. Doing that will be impressive considering he has only been in the league for half of its history.<br/><br/>Red flaming hot. That's Arjun's team right now. He has topped the 100 mark in five of the past six weeks, winning all six of those games. Christian McCaffrey is the foundation on which this team is built, but don't forget that Dak is currently a top 5 quarterback. Edelman and Davante continue to impress, and Arjun is spoiled by two great tight end options. An underrated aspect of this team is the Steelers defense, which has put up double digit points in EIGHT of the past nine weeks. That's crazy.<br/><br/>Arjun earned first seed and, deservedly so, is in by far the best position to finish with a ring. After a bye week, he will take on the winner of me and Girish, and that is certainly a favorable matchup for him given his recent performance. After that, it will be either Brian or Ruda, and we have discussed how they are prone to sudden collapses. Even if they play well, Arjun is capable of hitting back just as hard, as he has done for the past month and a half. Fantasy playoffs are always wild, but Arjun is at the top for a reason. He can smell that second consecutive ring.<br/><br/></p>
