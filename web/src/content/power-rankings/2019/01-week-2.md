<p>Just like that, the first week of football is done. In the NFL, it was a week of blowouts (with a few close ones here and there) (fuck the Rams) (FUCK THE SAINTS). In the GLB, it was actually a pretty normal week, although we hit a few extremes that we usually don't. Now that we have a bit of a clearer picture into team strengths, let's dive into some rankings.<br/><br/><strong>10. Anish {0-1} (-2)</strong><br/><br/>Anish, wake up. You're having a nightmare. You have to be, right? No one can have as bad a start as this in real life.<br/><br/>Catastrophe after catastrophe has beaten down this team, and it's not even Week 2 yet. Anish managed to put up just 54 points in what is apparently Damien's highest-usage week. Do I have to name all the alarms going off? Hill is out for at least a month, Henry broke his knee, Chiefs got McCoy, etc. You guys know the headline ones. Don't forget one alarm that has been overshadowed a bit: the Bears flat out lied about David Montgomery and gave him even less usage than some random idiot named Mike Davis. Montgomery looked really good, so I expect his usage to rise, but it's ridiculous that's he's not being treated as a first-and-second down back like he was supposed to. Another alarm: Ashwin just shoved Anish out of the way while tanking for the first waiver pick. Not sure how much it ended up mattering, but it can't have helped. Oh, and this went pretty under the radar too: Gordon is planning to sit out for 6-8 weeks. By the time him and Hill come back, this ship may have sunk a long time ago.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>9. Ashwin {0-1} (+1)</strong><br/><br/>Congrats Ashwin on rising one spot. It was mainly because it's pretty much impossible to have done as badly as Anish did, but also because Ashwin actually would've scored in the high 80s if he hadn't tanked. Not ideal, but not terrible either. All of us are dying to see if this first-ever tank pays off. I have to say, I respect the ballsy move by an organization that has really needed some after recent years. Still, Freeman sucked ass and that's really concerning, although the way things are going Ashwin probably still won the trade. One big concern is that Guice got injured, meaning this team is once again desperate for running backs, especially considering IT'S STILL JUST SITTING KAREEM HUNT THERE ON THE BENCH. DROP HIS ASS. One good thing though? Sammy Watkins looks like an absolute steal now that Hill is gone for a while. If Ashwin does start winning a few games, pay close attention to the PF battle as the season goes on to see how this whole tanking thing works out. <br/><br/><strong>8. Sid {0-1} (+1)</strong><br/><br/>Sid actually had a really solid day, topping 100 in an unfortunate loss against a dominant William. Keep in mind that in our early rankings, we take in account team strength on paper (aka preseason rankings), because there's a pretty good chance that Week 1 performances are flukes for a lot of teams. So while Chiddeh exceeded all expectations, we're going to wait another week or two before we hop on the hype train. Derrick Henry scored over a quarter of Judha's points, and OJ Howard looks like a complete mistake. The bench was extremely underwhelming too. Chuddah's solution was to pickup A. Brown from the Titans, who I expect to be arrested for aggravated assault within a week. Let's see if the team can hold up in a cross-division matchup with yours truly this week.<br/><br/><strong>7. Girish {0-1} (-4)</strong><br/><br/>Big drop, yikes. We all saw that Girish actually put together a solid performance by the end of Monday night, but the concerns are definite. 31% of his points came from Watson, and no quarterback will score that much week in and week out. Jacobs looked great, but the Steelers offense was inept and Conner seems a little scary right now. Girish's receivers did fine, but Kittle doesn't look to have the same chemistry with JimmyG yet that he did with... whoever played QB last year. Maybe that will develop with time. I'm not as concerned about Cohen because Girish had some great news from his bench. This is definitely looking to be a team that is deep at receiver, but we need to see more before we let Girish re-enter the top echelon. He'll get a chance to prove himself in a huge rivalry game against his higher-ranked nemesis.<br/><br/><strong>6. Akash {0-1} (-4)</strong><br/><br/>So yeah, top 3 had a rough week. You wanna talk about percentages? Lamar Jackson scored a ridiculous 38% of Akash's points. He was, as Akash has pointed out, the only player on this team to score a touchdown. That even includes his bench, save for backup QB Wilson. It's inexplicable why the Giants gave Barkley the ball so little, but at least we can expect that to change. The really worrying parts are the dissapointing performances by Fournette and the receivers. Diggs being hampered by an injury shows that maybe Akash's WR problems aren't too different from Brian's. Well, Brian's before all the... you know. We'll get to that. Also, everyone point at Akash and laugh at his love for Sony Michel, who managed to have less yards than he did carries in a blowout win. That's honestly tough to do. Also also, Jared Cook will not be a viable TE - Akash's Hock pickup was a good one. Anyway, unless Lamar Jackson continues his pace of &lt;checks math&gt; 80 touchdowns this year, 4Shepards might be in trouble.<br/><br/></p>

<p><br/><strong>5. Ruda {1-0} (-)</strong><br/><br/>That went pretty much exactly as expected. Ruda won, but not in spectacular fashion. It confirms the idea that this isn't his strongest team ever, but it's still a good one. Mahomes shined, and Elliot can only be expected to improve off his 12-point performance. Lockette, Edelman, and Godwin proved to be solid receiving options, though none of them will be top 10 guys every week. What's interesting now is a vastly different lineup for Week 2. Ruda signed Me So Hard Man and immediately put him to work as the WR2. Tyrell Williams is coming off the bench to lead the receiving group. Mixon got relegated to flex, what with some injury concerns and all, and was replaced by Matt Breida who will see an increased role with Coleman hurt. With that many changes, it would be hard to predict what will happen this week - if it weren't for the fact that Ruda is playing Anish. 2-0 here we come.<br/><br/><strong>4. Adit {1-0} (-)</strong><br/><br/>The matchup may have been a dud, but one thing you have to note about my team is that it did pretty darn well on what was supposed to be a down week. Both Aarons and Julio had arguably their toughest matchups of the year, away games at top 5 defenses. And it showed in their point totals. Despite all that, I managed to put up about a hundred points, with Chris Carson and Delanie Walker leading the way. By the way, I think I've received offers for Chris Carson from at least 6/10 teams. Let's make that 10/10 just for the heck of it. I traded up in the draft specifically to take this man. I'm not trading him unless you give me sexual favors. Also, Chubb wasn't stellar but keep in mind he got 8 points in a blowout loss. When the Browns aren't losing by 30 to random ass teams, I think it's fair to expect double digit performances. Lastly, take a look at my bench, which was supposed to be the worst in the league. It surprisingly kinda looked good. My receiver depth is solid af, and hey even Chris Thompson looks aight now that Guice is out. <br/><br/><strong>3. Brian {1-0} (+4)</strong><br/><br/>Yes, yes. Brian and Girish actually completely switched places, so there's your newspaper headline as the two rivals clash once again this week. I'll be the first to admit, Brian's team exceeded all expectations. Now, it looks like those of us warning him about Gurley may have been right, because that definitely wasn't classic Gurley playing on Sunday. But Ingram might live up to the hype Brian has given him, and Engram might actually make that ridiculous fifth-round reach worth it. And give it up for the Arizona offense, actually utilizing David Johnson the proper way. With Ekeler just waiting to be unleashed, I think this team is looking pretty exciting. I wouldn't be surprised to see Brian bounce around a little because I can see occasional bad games for guys like Ingram, Ekeler, and Gurley, but he's earned a top 3 spot for this week. Also, when I said Brian would have a tie this season, what I obviously meant was that Brian's HC would get a tie. Check out that 0.5 point total for the Cardinals coach. Why did you start him again Brian?<br/><br/>Last thing... Antonio Brown. At this point I'm assuming that Brown will either not play, or sneak in a game or two before Goodell exempts his ass. What a wild storyline this has been, and what a ridiculous rollercoaster for Brian, but it's a shame it might end in this way. <p><br/><strong>2. Arjun {1-0} (+4)</strong><br/><br/>Aaaand Arjun also switches spots with Akash. Interesting how the rankings worked. The bottom all kinda stayed around each other, the middle stayed exactly the same, but a few of the top guys and mid-lower guys went in opposite directions. Arjun's team was dominant last weekend, scoring 134 points. Now, temper your expectations for Dak just a bit. This is Dak, after all. More important, though, is that Christian Jesus McCaffrey has not lost a step. Man went wild all over a tough Rams defense, and we can all expect him to easily do the same to weaker defenses. Mark Andrews may be the cure to Arjun's Vance-related depression, although temper expectations for him too - his touchdown came from RG3, of all people, in garbage time. Evans and Cooks were pretty bad, but even if that does last for some reason, we've got a fantastic bench here with some top-notch depth receivers. This is a hella solid team that has gotten off to an explosive start. Watch out.<br/><br/><strong>1. Will {1-0} (-)</strong><br/><br/>Last week I said that on paper, this might be the best preseason team this league has ever had. I don't think anyone will argue with that now. Will very nearly hit the record books for points in Week 1, coming just shy of his own 3rd-place record of 155 (NO ONE WILL EVER BEAT MY RECORD YOU HEAR ME? NO ONE). It was marvelous, magnificent, maleficent, magical, macktastic. Kamara and Cook are exactly who we expected them to be, and the Cowboys + Amari look smooth af. If there was a question mark for this team, it was the level at which Colts offensive players could produce without Luck at the helm. Coach Frank Reich and Wolfpack QB Jacoby Brissett silenced all doubts, with Hilton and Mack combining for 46 points. Kelce appears to score more than most tight ends even in his down weeks, so that's good too. We won't see 17 points from Buttkicker every week, but I think it's also safe to assume that Will won't start defenses that get -1 in easy matchups every week too. Man, is this team good. Just ask around if you have to. There's a feeling in the air. Will and Sujay, it might be your year.<br/><br/></p>
